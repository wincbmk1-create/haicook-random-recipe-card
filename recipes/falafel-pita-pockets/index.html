Falafel Pita Pockets

Easy

Falafel pita pockets are crisp, creamy, and packed with vegetables. Warm pita holds falafel, cucumber, tomato, lettuce, and tahini sauce for a handheld meal with plenty of texture. It is casual, colorful, and filling.

Ingredients

  • 2 pita pockets
  • 6 falafel balls
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1 cup lettuce
  • 3 tbsp tahini sauce

Why You'll Love It: Crisp falafel and creamy tahini make pita pockets satisfying.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Warm the pita until flexible.
  2. Cut pockets open carefully.
  3. Fill with lettuce, cucumber, tomato, and falafel.
  4. Drizzle with tahini sauce.
  5. Serve immediately while the falafel is warm.
