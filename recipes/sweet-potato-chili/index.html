Sweet Potato Chili

Easy

Sweet potato chili is hearty, gently sweet, and smoky. Tender sweet potato cubes simmer with tomatoes, black beans, peppers, corn, and chili spices, then get finished with avocado, cilantro, and sour cream.

Ingredients

  • 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 1 can black beans
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 cup corn
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • avocado and cilantro

Why You'll Love It: Sweet potatoes make chili naturally rich, colorful, and filling.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute onion and bell pepper until softened.
  2. Add sweet potatoes, tomatoes, beans, corn, and spices.
  3. Simmer until sweet potatoes are tender.
  4. Adjust seasoning with salt and lime if desired.
  5. Top with avocado, cilantro, and sour cream.
