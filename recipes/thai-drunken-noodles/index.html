Thai Drunken Noodles

Medium

Thai drunken noodles, or pad kee mao, are glossy, spicy, and full of basil aroma. Wide rice noodles are stir-fried with chicken, bell peppers, onion, chili, and a savory sauce until the edges pick up a little char.

Ingredients

  • 400g wide rice noodles
  • 250g chicken strips
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 red chili, sliced
  • 1 cup Thai basil
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce

Why You'll Love It: Thai basil and chili make these noodles bold and fragrant.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Stir-fry garlic and chili in hot oil.
  2. Add chicken and cook until browned.
  3. Add bell pepper and onion and cook briefly.
  4. Toss in noodles and sauces over high heat.
  5. Fold in Thai basil just before serving.
