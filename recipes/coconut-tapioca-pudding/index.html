Coconut Tapioca Pudding

Easy

Coconut tapioca pudding is creamy, chewy, and tropical. Small tapioca pearls simmer in coconut milk until glossy, then get topped with toasted coconut.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup small tapioca pearls
  • 2 cups coconut milk
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • pinch salt
  • toasted coconut
  • mango, optional

Why You'll Love It: Coconut milk makes tapioca pudding lush and fragrant.
