Minestrone

Easy

This rustic Italian soup is loaded with whatever vegetables you have on hand, along with hearty beans and small pasta shapes simmered in a rich, tomato-based broth. It's a flexible, waste-nothing kind of recipe that changes slightly every time you make it, yet always turns out satisfying. Wholesome and filling enough to be a complete meal, it's a great way to sneak in extra vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 can white beans, drained
  • 1 cup small pasta shapes
  • 4 cups vegetable broth

Why You'll Love It: A veggie-packed soup that's a meal in itself.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Sauté onion, carrot, and celery in a large pot until softened.
  2. Add the diced tomatoes and vegetable broth, and bring to a simmer.
  3. Stir in the white beans and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Add the pasta and cook until al dente.
  5. Season to taste and serve hot with a drizzle of olive oil.
