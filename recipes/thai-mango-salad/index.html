Thai Mango Salad

Easy

Thai mango salad is crisp, bright, and full of contrast. Julienned mango, carrot, and cucumber are tossed with herbs, peanuts, lime, and a light chili dressing. It is refreshing enough for hot days and bold enough to stand beside grilled mains.

Ingredients

  • 2 firm mangoes, julienned
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 cucumber, julienned
  • 1/3 cup roasted peanuts, chopped
  • fresh cilantro and mint
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce or soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar

Why You'll Love It: A crunchy salad with sweet mango, lime, herbs, and peanuts.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Whisk lime juice, fish sauce or soy sauce, sugar, and chili to make the dressing.
  2. Combine mango, carrot, cucumber, and herbs in a large bowl.
  3. Pour over the dressing and toss gently.
  4. Add chopped peanuts just before serving.
  5. Taste and adjust with more lime if needed.
