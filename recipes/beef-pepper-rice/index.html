Beef Pepper Rice

Easy

Beef pepper rice is built around hot rice, thin beef slices, sweet corn, butter, and a generous hit of black pepper. As everything warms together, the butter melts into the rice and the sauce coats the beef. It is rich, quick, and deeply savory.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups hot cooked rice
  • 250g thin sliced beef
  • 1/2 cup corn
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey or sugar
  • black pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: Thin beef, corn, butter, and pepper turn rice into a bold skillet meal.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Mix soy sauce with honey and black pepper.
  2. Sear beef quickly in a hot skillet until just browned.
  3. Add rice, corn, and butter to the pan.
  4. Pour in the sauce and toss until everything is hot and glossy.
  5. Finish with scallions and extra black pepper.
