Beef Pepper Rice

Easy

Beef pepper rice is built around hot rice, thin beef slices, sweet corn, butter, and a generous hit of black pepper. As everything warms together, the butter melts into the rice and the sauce coats the beef. It is rich, quick, and deeply savory.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups water or broth
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 250g thin sliced beef
  • 1/2 cup corn
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey or sugar
  • black pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: Thin beef, corn, butter, and pepper turn rice into a bold skillet meal.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Beef Pepper Rice, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
