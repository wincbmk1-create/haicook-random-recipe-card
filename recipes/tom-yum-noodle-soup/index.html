Tom Yum Noodle Soup

Medium

Tom yum noodle soup adds rice noodles to the Thai hot-and-sour classic. Shrimp, mushrooms, tomatoes, lime, cilantro, and chili oil swim in a bright red-orange broth that feels bold but refreshing.

Ingredients

  • 200g rice noodles
  • 250g shrimp
  • 4 cups broth
  • 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tom yum paste
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • fresh cilantro
  • chili oil

Why You'll Love It: Hot, sour broth and shrimp make a lively noodle soup.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Simmer broth with tom yum paste.
  2. Add mushrooms and tomatoes and cook until softened.
  3. Add shrimp and simmer until pink.
  4. Cook rice noodles separately and divide into bowls.
  5. Ladle soup over noodles and finish with lime, cilantro, and chili oil.
