Roasted Red Pepper Pasta

Easy

Roasted red peppers blend into a smooth, vibrant sauce with garlic, olive oil, and parmesan. The result is creamy, sweet, and gently smoky, coating short pasta beautifully. Fresh basil on top gives it a lively finish that keeps the whole bowl balanced.

Ingredients

  • 350g dry rigatoni or penne
  • 2 roasted red peppers
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 1/4 cup cream or dry pasta water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh basil
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: A colorful pasta sauce that tastes rich without feeling heavy.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Roasted Red Pepper Pasta, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
