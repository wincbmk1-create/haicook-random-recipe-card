Ham Cheese Croissant Melt

Easy

Ham cheese croissant melt is flaky, warm, and savory. A buttery croissant is filled with ham, gruyere, and Dijon, then baked until the cheese melts into the layers. It is simple cafe comfort at home.

Ingredients

  • 1 croissant
  • 2 slices ham
  • 1/3 cup grated gruyere
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp butter

Why You'll Love It: Flaky pastry and melted cheese make a polished cafe bite.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Split the croissant without cutting all the way through.
  2. Spread Dijon inside.
  3. Layer ham and gruyere.
  4. Brush the top lightly with butter.
  5. Bake until warm and melted.
