Seafood Saffron Soup

Medium

Seafood saffron soup is golden, briny, and elegant. Shrimp, mussels, fish, tomato, fennel, and saffron simmer into a fragrant coastal bowl.

Ingredients

  • 200 g mixed seafood
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1/2 fennel bulb
  • 3 cups seafood broth
  • parsley
  • lemon

Why You'll Love It: Saffron and seafood make a fragrant golden soup.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and chop the vegetables, then cut any raw meat, seafood, tofu, or aromatics into even pieces before heating the pot.
  2. Saute the aromatics in a little fat until fragrant, then add spices or tomato paste so the soup base develops flavor before liquid goes in.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Stir the Seafood Saffron Soup gently as it finishes so the ingredients stay evenly distributed and the broth tastes balanced.
  5. Taste the soup, adjust salt and brightness, then ladle into bowls with fresh herbs or toppings.
