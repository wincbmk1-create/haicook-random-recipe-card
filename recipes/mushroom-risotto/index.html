Mushroom Risotto

Medium

Risotto is a labor of love — starchy Arborio rice is toasted, then slowly bathed in warm broth one ladle at a time while you stir, coaxing out its natural creaminess without any cream at all. Earthy sautéed mushrooms add a deep, savory note, while a generous finish of butter and parmesan makes the whole dish silky and rich. The gentle, repetitive stirring process is almost meditative, making it as relaxing to cook as it is comforting to eat.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups arborio rice
  • 300g mixed mushrooms, sliced
  • 4 cups warm vegetable broth
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 3 tbsp butter

Why You'll Love It: Slow-stirred comfort food that feels like a warm hug.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Sauté mushrooms in a little butter until golden; set aside.
  2. In the same pan, cook onion until translucent, then add rice and toast for 1-2 minutes.
  3. Pour in the wine and stir until absorbed.
  4. Add warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring often, until each addition is absorbed before adding more.
  5. Once rice is creamy and tender, stir in mushrooms, butter, and parmesan before serving.
