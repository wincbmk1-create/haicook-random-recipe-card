Spanish Paella

Medium

Paella is Spain's beloved celebration dish, built around short-grain rice slowly simmered in a saffron-infused broth until it turns a deep golden hue. Juicy shrimp, tender chicken, and sweet bell peppers are layered on top, while the rice at the bottom develops a coveted crispy crust called socarrat. Traditionally cooked in a wide shallow pan and served straight from it, paella turns any weekend dinner into a shared, festive occasion.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups short-grain rice (bomba or arborio)
  • 1/2 tsp saffron threads
  • 4 cups chicken or seafood stock
  • 8 large shrimp, peeled
  • 1 chicken thigh, cut into pieces
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: A show-stopping one-pan dish perfect for weekend gatherings.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a wide paella pan and brown the chicken pieces on both sides; set aside.
  2. Sauté onion, garlic, and bell pepper in the same pan until softened.
  3. Stir in rice, coating it in the oil, then add saffron-infused stock.
  4. Nestle the browned chicken back into the pan and simmer without stirring for 15-18 minutes.
  5. Add shrimp on top for the last 5 minutes of cooking, and let a crispy socarrat form on the bottom before serving.
