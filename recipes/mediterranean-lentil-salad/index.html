Mediterranean Lentil Salad

Easy

Mediterranean lentil salad is hearty, briny, and refreshing. Lentils, cucumber, tomatoes, olives, feta, and herbs are tossed in lemony olive oil dressing.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked lentils
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup olives
  • 1/3 cup feta
  • parsley
  • 2 tbsp lemon dressing

Why You'll Love It: Lentils give a fresh chopped salad lasting substance.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Combine lentils, cucumber, tomatoes, and olives.
  2. Add feta and parsley.
  3. Pour over lemon dressing.
  4. Toss gently.
  5. Rest briefly before serving.
