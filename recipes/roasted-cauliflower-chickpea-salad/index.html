Roasted Cauliflower Chickpea Salad

Easy

Roasted cauliflower and chickpeas bring golden edges and nutty flavor to a bed of arugula. Tahini lemon dressing, parsley, pomegranate, and almonds add creaminess, freshness, and crunch. It is a hearty plant-based salad that works warm or at room temperature.

Ingredients

  • 1 head cauliflower, florets
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 3 cups arugula
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1/4 cup pomegranate seeds
  • 1/4 cup almonds
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: Roasted vegetables and tahini make a hearty plant-based salad.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Roast cauliflower and chickpeas at 200C (400F) until golden.
  2. Whisk tahini with lemon juice and a splash of water.
  3. Arrange arugula in a bowl.
  4. Add roasted cauliflower and chickpeas.
  5. Drizzle with tahini dressing and finish with parsley, pomegranate, and almonds.
