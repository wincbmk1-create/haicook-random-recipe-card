Lobster Linguine

Medium

Lobster linguine brings sweet seafood, garlic, tomato, and parsley together in a glossy pasta sauce. The dish is polished without being overly heavy, letting the lobster stay the star. A little lemon at the end keeps the whole plate bright.

Ingredients

  • 350g linguine
  • 250g cooked lobster meat
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1/3 cup white wine or broth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: Sweet lobster makes simple linguine feel luxurious.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook linguine until al dente; reserve pasta water.
  2. Saute garlic and cherry tomatoes in olive oil.
  3. Add wine or broth and simmer briefly.
  4. Fold in lobster meat just to warm through.
  5. Toss with linguine, lemon juice, parsley, and pasta water as needed.
