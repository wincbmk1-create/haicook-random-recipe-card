Potato Mushroom Gratin

Medium

Potato mushroom gratin is creamy, earthy, and golden on top. Thin potato slices are layered with mushrooms, thyme, cream, and gruyere, then baked until bubbling.

Ingredients

  • 4 potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups mushrooms
  • 1 cup cream
  • 1 cup gruyere
  • 1 clove garlic
  • thyme
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Mushrooms and cream bring extra depth to classic gratin.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Potato Mushroom Gratin, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
