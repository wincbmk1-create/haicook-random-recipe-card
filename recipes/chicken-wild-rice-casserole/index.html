Chicken Wild Rice Casserole

Medium

Chicken wild rice casserole is creamy, savory, and full of texture. Wild rice brings a nutty chew, mushrooms add depth, and chicken makes it satisfying enough for a full dinner. It is the kind of bake that tastes calm and familiar.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked wild rice blend
  • 1 1/2 cups water or broth
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 raw chicken breast or thigh
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 cup cream or milk
  • 1 cup broth
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1/2 cup parmesan

Why You'll Love It: Wild rice and chicken bake into a cozy, family-style dinner.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Chicken Wild Rice Casserole, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
