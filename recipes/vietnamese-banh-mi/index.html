Vietnamese Banh Mi

Medium

Vietnamese banh mi is crisp, fresh, and savory. A light baguette holds pork, pickled vegetables, cucumber, cilantro, and a swipe of mayo for a sandwich with snap and balance. It is layered without feeling heavy.

Ingredients

  • 1 small baguette
  • 120 g pork slices
  • 1/2 cup pickled carrot and daikon
  • 1/4 cucumber, sliced
  • cilantro sprigs
  • 1 tbsp mayo
  • chili slices

Why You'll Love It: Pickles, herbs, and crisp bread make every bite lively.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the fillings from raw ingredients: wash and slice vegetables, season any meat or seafood, and mix spreads or sauces from their listed components.
  2. Cook raw proteins or vegetables until tender, then toast or warm the bread so it can hold the filling without turning soggy.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Layer the cooked fillings, fresh toppings, and sauce into the bread for Vietnamese Banh Mi while balancing moisture and crunch.
  5. Serve while the bread is warm or crisp, adding delicate herbs, greens, or pickles at the very end.
