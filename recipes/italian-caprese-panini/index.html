Italian Caprese Panini

Easy

Italian caprese panini is warm, crisp, and full of fresh flavor. Ciabatta is layered with tomato, mozzarella, basil, and pesto, then pressed until the cheese melts and the bread turns golden. It is a quick bistro-style sandwich with clean, familiar flavors.

Ingredients

  • 1 ciabatta roll
  • 2 slices mozzarella
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1 tbsp pesto
  • fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Tomato, basil, and melted mozzarella bring classic Italian comfort.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Slice the ciabatta and spread pesto inside.
  2. Layer mozzarella, tomato, and basil.
  3. Brush the outside lightly with olive oil.
  4. Press in a panini press or skillet until golden.
  5. Slice and serve warm.
