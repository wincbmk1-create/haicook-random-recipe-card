Greek Lemon Rice Pilaf

Easy

Greek lemon rice pilaf is simple, sunny, and flexible. Rice simmers in broth with olive oil and aromatics, then gets finished with lemon juice, zest, and herbs. It works as a side for grilled meats, but with chickpeas or vegetables it becomes a cheerful light meal.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups long-grain rice
  • 3 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • salt to taste

Why You'll Love It: Lemon and dill make this rice bright enough for any plate.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute onion in olive oil until softened.
  2. Add rice and toast for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in broth, cover, and simmer until tender.
  4. Rest the rice for 10 minutes off heat.
  5. Fluff with lemon zest, lemon juice, dill, and parsley.
