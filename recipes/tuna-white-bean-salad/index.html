Tuna White Bean Salad

Easy

Tuna white bean salad is bright, protein-rich, and easy. Tuna, white beans, green beans, red onion, parsley, and lemon dressing make a clean lunch plate.

Ingredients

  • 1 can tuna
  • 1 cup white beans
  • 1 cup green beans
  • 1/4 red onion
  • parsley
  • 2 tbsp lemon dressing
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: White beans make tuna salad hearty without weighing it down.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Blanch green beans until tender-crisp.
  2. Combine tuna and white beans.
  3. Add green beans, red onion, and parsley.
  4. Toss with lemon dressing.
  5. Season with black pepper.
