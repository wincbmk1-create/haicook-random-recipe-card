Patatas Bravas

Easy

Patatas bravas is a Spanish tapas favorite built around crisp potato cubes, smoky tomato sauce, and a cool garlic aioli drizzle. It is punchy, shareable, and excellent when you want potatoes with a little attitude.

Ingredients

  • 700g potatoes, cubed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp chili flakes
  • 1/3 cup aioli
  • fresh parsley
  • salt to taste

Why You'll Love It: Crispy potatoes, smoky sauce, and creamy aioli make perfect tapas.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Patatas Bravas, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
