Eggplant Parmesan Sub

Medium

Eggplant parmesan sub is saucy, cheesy, and deeply comforting. Breaded eggplant slices tuck into a sub roll with marinara and mozzarella, then bake until hot and bubbling. Basil keeps the richness balanced.

Ingredients

  • 1 sub roll
  • 3 breaded eggplant slices
  • 1/2 cup marinara
  • 1/2 cup mozzarella
  • 2 tbsp parmesan
  • fresh basil

Why You'll Love It: Crisp eggplant and bubbling cheese make a hearty sandwich.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the fillings from raw ingredients: wash and slice vegetables, season any meat or seafood, and mix spreads or sauces from their listed components.
  2. Cook raw proteins or vegetables until tender, then toast or warm the bread so it can hold the filling without turning soggy.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Layer the cooked fillings, fresh toppings, and sauce into the bread for Eggplant Parmesan Sub while balancing moisture and crunch.
  5. Serve while the bread is warm or crisp, adding delicate herbs, greens, or pickles at the very end.
