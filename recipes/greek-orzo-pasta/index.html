Greek Orzo Pasta

Easy

Greek orzo pasta is fresh, tangy, and colorful. Tender orzo is tossed with cucumber, cherry tomatoes, olives, feta, herbs, lemon, and olive oil. It is lovely chilled for lunch or served at room temperature beside grilled mains.

Ingredients

  • 350g orzo
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1/2 cup feta cubes
  • 1/3 cup olives
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: A bright pasta bowl with crunchy vegetables and salty feta.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook orzo until tender, then rinse briefly and drain.
  2. Toss orzo with lemon juice and olive oil.
  3. Fold in tomatoes, cucumber, olives, and feta.
  4. Season with salt, pepper, and parsley.
  5. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
