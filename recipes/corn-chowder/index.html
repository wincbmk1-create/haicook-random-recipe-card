Corn Chowder

Easy

Sweet corn kernels are simmered with tender chunks of potato in a rich, creamy broth that highlights corn's natural sweetness. Blending a portion of the soup before stirring it back in gives the chowder its signature thick, velvety body while still leaving plenty of texture. It's the ultimate way to celebrate corn at its sweetest, whether that's peak summer or any time you're craving comfort.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups corn kernels (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 potatoes, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 cup cream
  • 3 cups vegetable broth

Why You'll Love It: A creamy bowl that celebrates sweet summer corn.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Sauté the onion in a pot until softened.
  2. Add the diced potatoes, corn, and vegetable broth, and simmer until the potatoes are tender.
  3. Blend about a third of the soup until smooth, then stir it back into the pot.
  4. Stir in the cream and heat through gently.
  5. Season to taste and serve warm.
