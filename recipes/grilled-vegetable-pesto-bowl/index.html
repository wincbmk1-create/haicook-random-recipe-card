Grilled Vegetable Pesto Bowl

Easy

Grilled vegetable pesto bowl is herby, colorful, and satisfying. Grilled zucchini, peppers, tomatoes, grains, mozzarella, and pesto make a warm-weather bowl.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked grains
  • 1 cup grilled zucchini
  • 1 cup grilled peppers
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup mozzarella
  • 2 tbsp pesto
  • basil

Why You'll Love It: Pesto ties smoky vegetables and grains together.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Spoon grains into a bowl.
  2. Add grilled vegetables and tomatoes.
  3. Scatter mozzarella over the top.
  4. Drizzle with pesto.
  5. Finish with basil.
