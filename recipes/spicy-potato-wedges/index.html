Spicy Potato Wedges

Easy

Spicy potato wedges are smoky, crisp, and tender inside. Potato wedges are tossed with paprika, cayenne, garlic, and oil, then roasted until browned and served with a cooling dip.

Ingredients

  • 4 potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • salt
  • yogurt dip

Why You'll Love It: Smoky spices make roasted wedges bold and snackable.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Spicy Potato Wedges, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
