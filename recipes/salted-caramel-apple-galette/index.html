Salted Caramel Apple Galette

Medium

Salted caramel apple galette is rustic, buttery, and fragrant. Sliced apples bake in flaky pastry with caramel and a pinch of sea salt.

Ingredients

  • 1 pastry round
  • 3 apples, sliced
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp caramel sauce
  • 1 egg wash
  • sea salt
  • cinnamon

Why You'll Love It: Salted caramel makes apple galette glossy and cozy.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Arrange apple slices on pastry.
  2. Sprinkle with sugar and cinnamon.
  3. Fold pastry edges over apples.
  4. Brush with egg wash.
  5. Bake and drizzle with salted caramel.
