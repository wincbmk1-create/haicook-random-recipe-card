Tomato Egg Rice

Easy

Tomato egg rice is the kind of quick home-style meal that turns pantry basics into something deeply comforting. Juicy tomatoes cook down into a light sauce, soft scrambled eggs soak up the flavor, and warm rice catches every spoonful. It is gentle, colorful, and ready fast.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked white rice
  • 3 ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • salt to taste

Why You'll Love It: A humble comfort bowl with soft eggs and bright tomato sauce.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Scramble the beaten eggs in hot oil until just set, then remove from the pan.
  2. Add tomatoes to the same pan and cook until juicy and softened.
  3. Season with sugar, soy sauce, and a pinch of salt.
  4. Return the eggs and fold gently into the tomato sauce.
  5. Spoon over warm rice and finish with sliced scallions.
