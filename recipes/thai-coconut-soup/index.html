Thai Coconut Soup

Easy

Thai coconut soup is creamy, aromatic, and surprisingly quick. Coconut milk softens the heat of chili while lime, herbs, and mushrooms keep the broth fresh. Tender chicken pieces make it filling enough for dinner without losing its light, fragrant character.

Ingredients

  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 250g chicken breast, sliced
  • 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 stalk lemongrass, bruised
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce or soy sauce
  • cilantro and chili oil, to serve

Why You'll Love It: A soothing coconut broth with bright lime and gentle heat.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and chop the vegetables, then cut any raw meat, seafood, tofu, or aromatics into even pieces before heating the pot.
  2. Saute the aromatics in a little fat until fragrant, then add spices or tomato paste so the soup base develops flavor before liquid goes in.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Stir the Thai Coconut Soup gently as it finishes so the ingredients stay evenly distributed and the broth tastes balanced.
  5. Taste the soup, adjust salt and brightness, then ladle into bowls with fresh herbs or toppings.
