Spicy Peanut Ramen

Easy

Spicy peanut ramen is creamy, nutty, and gently fiery. A quick peanut-chili broth coats springy ramen noodles while bok choy and scallions keep the bowl fresh. It is comforting without feeling heavy.

Ingredients

  • 2 packs ramen noodles
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp chili oil
  • 2 cups broth
  • 1 cup bok choy
  • scallions

Why You'll Love It: Peanut sauce and chili oil make ramen rich and lively.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Simmer broth with peanut butter, soy sauce, and chili oil.
  2. Cook ramen noodles until just tender.
  3. Add bok choy to the broth until wilted.
  4. Combine noodles with the peanut broth.
  5. Top with scallions and extra chili oil.
