Mushroom Wild Rice Pilaf

Medium

Wild rice brings a nutty chew that pairs beautifully with browned mushrooms and herbs. Toasted almonds add crunch, while onion and parsley round out the pilaf. It can stand alone as a vegetarian main or sit beside roasted meats as a hearty side.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups wild rice blend
  • 300g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 1/3 cup sliced almonds
  • 2 tbsp butter or olive oil
  • fresh parsley
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: Nutty wild rice and mushrooms make a deeply savory side or main.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Mushroom Wild Rice Pilaf, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
