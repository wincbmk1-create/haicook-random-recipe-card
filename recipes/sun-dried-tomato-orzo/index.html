Sun-Dried Tomato Orzo

Easy

Sun-dried tomato orzo is creamy, colorful, and quick. The orzo cooks into a tender base that carries sweet-tangy tomatoes, spinach, basil, and parmesan. It works as a cozy main or a rich side for grilled chicken or seafood.

Ingredients

  • 350g dry orzo
  • 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • fresh basil

Why You'll Love It: Tiny orzo pasta turns creamy sauce into a spoonable comfort bowl.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Sun-Dried Tomato Orzo, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
