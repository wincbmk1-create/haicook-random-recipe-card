Taiwanese Beef Noodle Soup

Medium

Taiwanese beef noodle soup brings tender braised beef, thick wheat noodles, bok choy, scallions, and chili oil into a fragrant dark broth. It is bold, warming, and deeply comforting without needing many garnishes.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions wheat noodles
  • 400g braised beef chunks
  • 4 cups beef broth
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chili bean paste
  • 2 heads bok choy
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • chili oil

Why You'll Love It: Braised beef and deep broth make a hearty noodle classic.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Simmer beef broth with soy sauce and chili bean paste.
  2. Cook noodles until tender.
  3. Blanch bok choy briefly.
  4. Divide noodles into bowls and add beef and bok choy.
  5. Ladle hot broth over the top and finish with scallions and chili oil.
