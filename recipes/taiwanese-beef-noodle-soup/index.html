Taiwanese Beef Noodle Soup

Medium

Taiwanese beef noodle soup brings tender braised beef, thick wheat noodles, bok choy, scallions, and chili oil into a fragrant dark broth. It is bold, warming, and deeply comforting without needing many garnishes.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions wheat dry noodles
  • 400g braised beef chunks
  • 4 cups beef broth
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chili bean paste
  • 2 heads bok choy
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • chili oil

Why You'll Love It: Braised beef and deep broth make a hearty noodle classic.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Taiwanese Beef Noodle Soup, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
