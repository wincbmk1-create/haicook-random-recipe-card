White Bean Kale Soup

Easy

White beans and kale simmer in a savory broth with carrots, garlic, rosemary, and olive oil. Some of the beans soften into the broth, giving it a gentle creaminess without needing cream. It is simple, nourishing, and exactly the kind of soup that feels good to eat.

Ingredients

  • 2 cans cannellini beans
  • 3 cups kale, chopped
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 5 cups broth
  • 1 tsp rosemary
  • olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Beans make it creamy and filling while kale keeps it fresh.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute onion, carrot, and garlic in olive oil until softened.
  2. Add beans, rosemary, and broth, then simmer for 15 minutes.
  3. Mash a small portion of the beans to thicken the broth.
  4. Stir in kale and cook until tender.
  5. Season with salt, pepper, and a final drizzle of olive oil.
