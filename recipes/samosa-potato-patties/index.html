Samosa Potato Patties

Medium

Samosa potato patties are crisp outside and warmly spiced inside. Mashed potatoes, peas, cumin, coriander, and cilantro are shaped into patties and pan-fried until golden.

Ingredients

  • 3 potatoes, boiled
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • cilantro
  • chutney

Why You'll Love It: Samosa-inspired spices make potato patties bold and snackable.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Mash boiled potatoes with peas and spices.
  2. Fold in cilantro and season well.
  3. Shape mixture into patties.
  4. Pan-fry until crisp and golden.
  5. Serve with chutney.
