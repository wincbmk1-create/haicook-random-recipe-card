Banana Pudding

Easy

Layers of creamy vanilla pudding, fresh banana slices, and soft vanilla cookies are stacked together and chilled until the cookies soften into a cake-like texture. As it sits, the flavors meld together into something greater than the sum of its parts. This nostalgic, no-bake layered dessert is a guaranteed hit at potlucks, barbecues, and family gatherings alike.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 bananas, sliced
  • 1 box vanilla wafer cookies
  • 1 cup heavy cream

Why You'll Love It: A layered treat that's always a hit at potlucks.
