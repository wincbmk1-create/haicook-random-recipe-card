Kimchi Fried Rice

Easy

Kimchi fried rice is a fast, flavorful way to use day-old rice and a little fermented heat. Chopped kimchi seasons the whole pan with tang, spice, and depth, while a fried egg on top makes it feel complete. It is simple, satisfying, and especially good when you want comfort food with energy.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups water or broth
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup chopped kimchi
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp gochujang, optional
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds

Why You'll Love It: A punchy fried rice that turns leftovers into a bold, cozy meal.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Kimchi Fried Rice, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
