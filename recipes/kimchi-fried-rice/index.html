Kimchi Fried Rice

Easy

Kimchi fried rice is a fast, flavorful way to use day-old rice and a little fermented heat. Chopped kimchi seasons the whole pan with tang, spice, and depth, while a fried egg on top makes it feel complete. It is simple, satisfying, and especially good when you want comfort food with energy.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups day-old rice
  • 1 cup chopped kimchi
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp gochujang, optional
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds

Why You'll Love It: A punchy fried rice that turns leftovers into a bold, cozy meal.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Heat sesame oil in a skillet and stir-fry kimchi for 2 minutes.
  2. Add rice and break up any clumps as it fries.
  3. Season with soy sauce and gochujang if using.
  4. Fry eggs separately until the edges are crisp.
  5. Top the rice with eggs, scallions, and sesame seeds before serving.
