Shepherd's Pie

Medium

Shepherd's pie layers savory beef and vegetables under a blanket of mashed potatoes, then bakes until the top is browned and crisp at the peaks. It is hearty, family-friendly, and perfect for a cozy dinner.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups mashed potatoes
  • 400g ground beef or lamb
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 cup peas
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce

Why You'll Love It: Fluffy mashed potato topping turns savory filling into comfort food.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Shepherd's Pie, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
