Asian Chicken Salad Bowl

Easy

Crisp shredded cabbage and tender chicken are tossed together in a nutty, tangy sesame dressing, then topped with toasted almonds for extra crunch. The dressing is bold enough to stand up to the crisp vegetables without wilting them, so it holds up beautifully even after a few hours in a packed lunch. It's a zesty, flavor-packed salad that makes eating your veggies genuinely exciting.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups shredded cabbage
  • 2 cooked chicken breasts, shredded
  • 1/4 cup sliced almonds, toasted
  • 3 tbsp sesame dressing
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 2 scallions, sliced

Why You'll Love It: A crunchy, flavor-packed salad that's a lunchbox favorite.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Combine shredded cabbage, carrot, and scallions in a large bowl.
  2. Add the shredded chicken on top.
  3. Drizzle with sesame dressing and toss well to coat.
  4. Sprinkle with toasted almonds.
  5. Serve immediately, or pack for a lunchbox that holds up for hours.
