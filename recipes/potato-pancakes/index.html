Potato Pancakes

Easy

Potato pancakes are crisp, golden, and deeply comforting. Shredded potatoes and onion are bound with egg, fried until crunchy at the edges, and served with sour cream, chives, or applesauce.

Ingredients

  • 4 potatoes, grated
  • 1 small onion, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • oil for frying
  • sour cream
  • chives

Why You'll Love It: Shredded potatoes fry into crisp edges and tender centers.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Squeeze excess moisture from grated potatoes and onion.
  2. Mix with egg, flour, and salt.
  3. Spoon small patties into hot oil.
  4. Fry until golden and crisp on both sides.
  5. Drain and serve with sour cream and chives.
