Poke Bowl

Easy

Cubes of fresh ahi tuna are marinated briefly in soy sauce and sesame oil, then served over a bed of warm rice with creamy avocado and your favorite crunchy toppings. The dish celebrates the natural quality of the fish, so freshness matters more than complicated technique. It's a vibrant, restaurant-style bowl that's surprisingly simple to recreate in your own kitchen.

Ingredients

  • 300g sushi-grade ahi tuna, cubed
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 scallion, sliced

Why You'll Love It: A restaurant-style bowl you can build at home in minutes.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Combine soy sauce and sesame oil in a bowl.
  2. Add the cubed tuna and marinate for 10-15 minutes in the fridge.
  3. Divide the warm rice between bowls.
  4. Top with the marinated tuna and diced avocado.
  5. Garnish with sesame seeds and sliced scallion before serving.
