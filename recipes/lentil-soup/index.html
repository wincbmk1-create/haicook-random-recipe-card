Lentil Soup

Easy

Earthy lentils are simmered with sweet carrots and a warm blend of cumin and other spices until they break down into a thick, hearty, nutritious soup. Lentils cook quickly and require no soaking, making this an easy weeknight option that's also incredibly budget-friendly. Packed with plant-based protein, it's a filling meal that doesn't skimp on flavor or nourishment.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups red lentils, rinsed
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 5 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: A protein-packed soup that's budget-friendly and filling.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a pot and sauté the onion and carrot until softened.
  2. Stir in the cumin and cook until fragrant.
  3. Add the lentils and vegetable broth, and bring to a boil.
  4. Reduce heat and simmer for 20-25 minutes until the lentils are soft and the soup has thickened.
  5. Blend partially for a creamier texture if desired, season to taste, and serve hot.
