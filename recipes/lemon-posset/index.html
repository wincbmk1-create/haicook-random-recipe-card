Lemon Posset

Easy

Lemon posset turns cream, sugar, and lemon into a silky chilled dessert with almost no effort. The citrus gently sets the cream while keeping the flavor bright and clean. Serve it in small glasses with berries or a shortbread cookie for a polished finish.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 2/3 cup sugar
  • 2 lemons, juiced and zested
  • berries
  • shortbread cookies

Why You'll Love It: Just a few ingredients set into a smooth citrus cream.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Simmer cream and sugar for a few minutes until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Remove from heat and stir in lemon juice and zest.
  3. Pour into small glasses or cups.
  4. Chill until softly set.
  5. Serve with berries and shortbread cookies.
