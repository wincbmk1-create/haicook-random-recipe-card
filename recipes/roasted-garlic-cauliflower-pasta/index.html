Roasted Garlic Cauliflower Pasta

Easy

Roasted cauliflower turns golden and nutty, then gets tossed with pasta, roasted garlic, parmesan, and herbs. The sauce is simple but flavorful, relying on olive oil and pasta water to bring everything together. It is cozy, vegetarian, and satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 350g pasta
  • 1 head cauliflower, florets
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1/3 cup parmesan
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp parsley
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Roasted cauliflower and garlic bring deep flavor without heaviness.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Roast cauliflower and garlic with olive oil until golden.
  2. Cook pasta until al dente; reserve pasta water.
  3. Mash roasted garlic with olive oil and lemon juice.
  4. Toss pasta with cauliflower, garlic oil, and parmesan.
  5. Finish with parsley and black pepper.
