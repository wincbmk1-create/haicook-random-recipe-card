Roasted Garlic Cauliflower Pasta

Easy

Roasted cauliflower turns golden and nutty, then gets tossed with pasta, roasted garlic, parmesan, and herbs. The sauce is simple but flavorful, relying on olive oil and pasta water to bring everything together. It is cozy, vegetarian, and satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 350g dry pasta
  • 1 head cauliflower, florets
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1/3 cup parmesan
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp parsley
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Roasted cauliflower and garlic bring deep flavor without heaviness.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Roasted Garlic Cauliflower Pasta, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
