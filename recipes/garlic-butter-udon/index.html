Garlic Butter Udon

Easy

Garlic butter udon is glossy, chewy, and deeply savory. Thick udon noodles are tossed with garlic, butter, soy sauce, and a little parmesan for a fast bowl with big comfort.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions udon noodles
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp parmesan
  • scallions
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Garlic butter turns chewy udon into a quick comfort bowl.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook udon until loose and tender.
  2. Melt butter and saute garlic gently.
  3. Add soy sauce and noodles.
  4. Toss until glossy.
  5. Finish with parmesan, scallions, and pepper.
