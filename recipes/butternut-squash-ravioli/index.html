Butternut Squash Ravioli

Medium

Butternut squash ravioli is sweet, savory, and gently rich. Tender pasta pockets are tossed in browned butter with sage, then finished with parmesan and a little black pepper. It is elegant enough for guests but simple enough when using prepared ravioli.

Ingredients

  • 400g butternut squash ravioli
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 8 sage leaves
  • 1/3 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 tbsp toasted walnuts, optional
  • salt and black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Sweet squash and sage butter make a cozy pasta plate.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook ravioli according to package directions and drain gently.
  2. Melt butter in a skillet until it smells nutty.
  3. Add sage leaves and cook until crisp.
  4. Toss ravioli carefully in the sage butter.
  5. Finish with parmesan, pepper, and walnuts if using.
