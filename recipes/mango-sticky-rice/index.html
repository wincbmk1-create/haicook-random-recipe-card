Mango Sticky Rice

Easy

Glutinous sticky rice is steamed until tender, then soaked in sweetened coconut milk until it turns rich and slightly chewy. Served alongside thick slices of ripe, juicy mango and an extra drizzle of coconut cream, the combination of warm rice and cool fruit is a beloved Thai dessert. One bite and you'll feel like you're enjoying it fresh off a street cart in Bangkok.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups glutinous sticky rice, soaked overnight
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 2 ripe mangoes, sliced

Why You'll Love It: A sweet finish that transports you straight to Thailand.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Steam the soaked sticky rice for 20-25 minutes until tender.
  2. Warm the coconut milk with sugar and salt until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Pour most of the sweetened coconut milk over the hot rice and let it soak in for 10-15 minutes.
  4. Slice the ripe mangoes just before serving.
  5. Plate the sticky rice with mango slices and drizzle with the remaining coconut milk.
