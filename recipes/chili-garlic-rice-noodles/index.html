Chili Garlic Rice Noodles

Easy

Chili garlic rice noodles are punchy, glossy, and fast. Soft rice noodles are tossed with garlic, chili crisp, soy, herbs, and peanuts for a simple dish with big flavor.

Ingredients

  • 200 g dry rice noodles
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp chili crisp
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp lime juice
  • cilantro
  • 2 tbsp peanuts

Why You'll Love It: Chili crisp and garlic make rice noodles bold in minutes.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Chili Garlic Rice Noodles, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
