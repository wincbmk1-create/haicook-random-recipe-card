Spicy Sausage Rigatoni

Easy

Spicy sausage rigatoni is hearty, saucy, and full of flavor. Crumbled sausage browns until savory, then simmers with tomato, chili flakes, and herbs before coating thick rigatoni. Parmesan rounds out the heat with a salty finish.

Ingredients

  • 350g dry rigatoni
  • 300g Italian sausage
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1/2 tsp chili flakes
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • grated parmesan
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: Sausage and chili make a punchy tomato pasta with real depth.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Spicy Sausage Rigatoni, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
