Cuban Black Beans and Rice

Easy

Cuban-style black beans and rice is humble, hearty, and full of flavor. Beans simmer with peppers, onion, garlic, and cumin until saucy, then get spooned over white rice with avocado and lime. It is budget-friendly comfort food with a bright finish.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked white rice
  • 1 can black beans, drained
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • fresh cilantro

Why You'll Love It: Saucy black beans over rice make an easy, comforting bowl.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute onion, bell pepper, and garlic until softened.
  2. Add black beans, cumin, and a splash of water.
  3. Simmer until the beans turn saucy and flavorful.
  4. Spoon beans over warm rice.
  5. Top with avocado, cilantro, and lime wedges.
