Chicken Noodle Soup

Easy

A gentle, golden broth is simmered with tender pieces of chicken, sweet carrots, and crisp celery until every ingredient melds into one comforting bowl. Soft egg noodles soak up the savory broth, making each spoonful feel like an edible hug. It's the timeless dish people reach for whether they're feeling under the weather or just craving something simple and soothing.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 6 cups chicken broth
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery stalks, sliced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1.5 cups egg noodles

Why You'll Love It: The ultimate cure for a cold day, or an actual cold.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring the chicken broth to a boil and add the chicken breasts.
  2. Simmer for 15-20 minutes until the chicken is cooked through, then remove and shred.
  3. Add carrots, celery, and onion to the broth and simmer until tender.
  4. Add the egg noodles and cook until al dente.
  5. Return the shredded chicken to the pot, season to taste, and serve hot.
