Chicken Noodle Soup

Easy

A gentle, golden broth is simmered with tender pieces of chicken, sweet carrots, and crisp celery until every ingredient melds into one comforting bowl. Soft egg noodles soak up the savory broth, making each spoonful feel like an edible hug. It's the timeless dish people reach for whether they're feeling under the weather or just craving something simple and soothing.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 6 cups chicken broth
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery stalks, sliced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1.5 cups egg noodles

Why You'll Love It: The ultimate cure for a cold day, or an actual cold.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and chop the vegetables, then cut any raw meat, seafood, tofu, or aromatics into even pieces before heating the pot.
  2. Saute the aromatics in a little fat until fragrant, then add spices or tomato paste so the soup base develops flavor before liquid goes in.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Stir the Chicken Noodle Soup gently as it finishes so the ingredients stay evenly distributed and the broth tastes balanced.
  5. Taste the soup, adjust salt and brightness, then ladle into bowls with fresh herbs or toppings.
