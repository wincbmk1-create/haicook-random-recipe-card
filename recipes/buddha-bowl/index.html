Buddha Bowl

Easy

Fluffy quinoa forms the base for this nourishing bowl, topped with crispy roasted chickpeas, creamy avocado slices, and hearty massaged kale. Every component is chosen for both nutrition and color, making the final bowl as beautiful to look at as it is satisfying to eat. It's endlessly customizable, so you can swap in whatever grains, greens, or proteins you have on hand.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup quinoa, cooked
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 2 cups kale, massaged with olive oil
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika

Why You'll Love It: A colorful, customizable bowl that's as healthy as it is pretty.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Toss chickpeas with olive oil and paprika, then roast at 200°C (400°F) for 20 minutes until crispy.
  2. Massage the kale with a little olive oil and a pinch of salt until softened.
  3. Divide the cooked quinoa between bowls as the base.
  4. Arrange the kale, roasted chickpeas, and sliced avocado on top.
  5. Drizzle with your favorite dressing before serving.
