Ciabatta Steak Sandwich

Medium

Ciabatta steak sandwich is hearty and full of contrast. Sliced steak, roasted peppers, arugula, and aioli are tucked into crusty ciabatta for a meal that feels robust but still fresh. The bread soaks up juices while keeping its bite.

Ingredients

  • 1 ciabatta roll
  • 150 g cooked steak, sliced
  • 1/2 cup arugula
  • 1/4 cup roasted peppers
  • 1 tbsp aioli
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Juicy steak and peppery arugula make a strong bistro sandwich.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Toast the ciabatta lightly.
  2. Spread aioli on both sides.
  3. Layer steak slices with roasted peppers.
  4. Add arugula and season lightly.
  5. Close the sandwich and serve warm.
