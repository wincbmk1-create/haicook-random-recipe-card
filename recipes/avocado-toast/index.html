Avocado Toast

Easy

Thick slices of crusty sourdough are toasted until golden, then piled high with mashed avocado seasoned simply with salt, lime, and a pinch of chili flakes for gentle heat. A perfectly cooked egg on top adds protein and a rich, runny finish that ties the whole thing together. It looks impressive with almost no effort, making it the ultimate quick breakfast for busy mornings.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices sourdough bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 lime, juiced
  • 1/4 tsp chili flakes
  • salt to taste

Why You'll Love It: A trendy breakfast that's ready in just 5 minutes.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Toast the sourdough slices until golden and crisp.
  2. Mash the avocado with lime juice and a pinch of salt.
  3. Fry or poach the egg to your preference.
  4. Spread the mashed avocado generously over the toast.
  5. Top with the egg and sprinkle with chili flakes before serving.
