Vietnamese Beef Pho Soup

Medium

Vietnamese beef pho soup is aromatic, fresh, and restorative. Rice noodles, thin beef, herbs, bean sprouts, and lime meet in a steaming spiced broth.

Ingredients

  • 200 g rice noodles
  • 120 g thin beef slices
  • 4 cups pho broth
  • bean sprouts
  • Thai basil
  • lime wedges
  • cilantro

Why You'll Love It: Fragrant broth and herbs make this soup bright and restorative.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook rice noodles and divide into bowls.
  2. Add beef slices over the noodles.
  3. Bring pho broth to a strong simmer.
  4. Pour hot broth over beef.
  5. Serve with herbs, sprouts, and lime.
