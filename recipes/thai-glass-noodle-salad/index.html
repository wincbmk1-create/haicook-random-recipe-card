Thai Glass Noodle Salad

Easy

Thai glass noodle salad is bright, tangy, and full of texture. Clear noodles are tossed with shrimp, lime dressing, herbs, chili, and peanuts for a refreshing bowl with a lively bite.

Ingredients

  • 150 g glass noodles
  • 8 shrimp
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • fresh cilantro and mint
  • 2 tbsp peanuts

Why You'll Love It: Lime, herbs, and chili keep glass noodles light and vivid.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak glass noodles until tender, then drain.
  2. Cook shrimp until pink.
  3. Whisk lime juice, fish sauce, sugar, and chili.
  4. Toss noodles with shrimp, herbs, and dressing.
  5. Top with peanuts.
