Mediterranean Couscous Salad

Easy

Pearl couscous gives this salad a tender, satisfying base, while cucumber, tomatoes, chickpeas, feta, and olives bring freshness and color. Lemon dressing keeps it lively and clean. It holds up well in the fridge, making it a strong choice for meal prep.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked pearl couscous
  • 1 cup cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta
  • 1/3 cup olives
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced

Why You'll Love It: A bright grain salad that works for lunch, dinner, or meal prep.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook couscous according to package directions and cool slightly.
  2. Combine couscous with cucumber, tomatoes, chickpeas, feta, and olives.
  3. Whisk olive oil with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  4. Toss the salad with the dressing until evenly coated.
  5. Chill briefly or serve at room temperature with extra herbs.
