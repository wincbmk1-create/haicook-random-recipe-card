Crispy Rice Salad Bowl

Medium

This salad bowl uses crisped rice clusters for crunch, then tosses them with fresh herbs, vegetables, peanuts, and a lime dressing. It is light but exciting, with contrast in every bite. Use leftover rice for the best texture.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice, chilled
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1/2 cup herbs, chopped
  • 1/3 cup peanuts
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce or soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar

Why You'll Love It: Crispy rice turns a salad bowl into something playful and satisfying.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Press chilled rice into a thin layer and pan-fry until crisp.
  2. Break crispy rice into bite-size pieces.
  3. Whisk lime juice, fish sauce or soy sauce, and sugar.
  4. Toss vegetables, herbs, peanuts, and crispy rice.
  5. Serve immediately so the rice stays crunchy.
