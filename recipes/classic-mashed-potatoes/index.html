Classic Mashed Potatoes

Easy

Potatoes are boiled until fork-tender, then whipped with plenty of butter, warm milk, and a touch of garlic until impossibly smooth and creamy. The key to the fluffiest texture is not over-mixing, which keeps the potatoes light rather than gluey. This is the ultimate cozy side dish that pairs effortlessly with everything from roast chicken to a simple weeknight meatloaf.

Ingredients

  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 1/2 cup warm milk
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • salt to taste

Why You'll Love It: Creamy comfort food that pairs with almost anything.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water until fork-tender, about 15-20 minutes; drain well.
  2. Warm the milk with the minced garlic in a small pan.
  3. Mash the potatoes while still hot until mostly smooth.
  4. Stir in the butter and warm garlic milk gradually.
  5. Season with salt to taste and serve warm.
