Classic Mashed Potatoes

Easy

Potatoes are boiled until fork-tender, then whipped with plenty of butter, warm milk, and a touch of garlic until impossibly smooth and creamy. The key to the fluffiest texture is not over-mixing, which keeps the potatoes light rather than gluey. This is the ultimate cozy side dish that pairs effortlessly with everything from roast chicken to a simple weeknight meatloaf.

Ingredients

  • 1kg potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 1/2 cup warm milk
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • salt to taste

Why You'll Love It: Creamy comfort food that pairs with almost anything.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Classic Mashed Potatoes, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
