Burmese Coconut Noodles

Medium

Burmese coconut noodles are inspired by ohn no khao swe, a creamy chicken coconut curry noodle soup. Tender noodles, shredded chicken, lime, cilantro, chili oil, and crispy noodles make it rich, bright, and textured.

Ingredients

  • 300g egg noodles
  • 2 cups coconut milk
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 250g shredded cooked chicken
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 lime
  • fresh cilantro
  • crispy noodles
  • chili oil

Why You'll Love It: Coconut curry broth and crunchy toppings make every bite layered.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute onion with curry powder until fragrant.
  2. Add coconut milk and chicken broth and simmer.
  3. Stir in shredded chicken until warmed through.
  4. Cook egg noodles and divide into bowls.
  5. Ladle soup over noodles and top with lime, cilantro, crispy noodles, and chili oil.
