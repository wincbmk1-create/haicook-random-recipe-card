Taco Salad Bowl

Easy

Taco salad turns seasoned beef, crisp romaine, beans, corn, avocado, cheese, and tortilla chips into a lively dinner bowl. The contrast of warm beef and cool vegetables makes it especially satisfying. It is casual, colorful, and perfect when you want taco flavors with extra crunch.

Ingredients

  • 250g ground beef
  • 1 tbsp taco seasoning
  • 3 cups romaine, chopped
  • 1/2 cup black beans
  • 1/2 cup corn
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese
  • tortilla chips

Why You'll Love It: All the taco-night flavors in a crisp, colorful bowl.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook ground beef with taco seasoning until browned.
  2. Arrange romaine in a large bowl.
  3. Add beans, corn, avocado, cheese, and tomatoes if using.
  4. Spoon warm beef over the salad.
  5. Top with tortilla chips and your favorite dressing or salsa.
