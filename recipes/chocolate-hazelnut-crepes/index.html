Chocolate Hazelnut Crepes

Easy

Chocolate hazelnut crepes are delicate, nutty, and indulgent. Thin crepes are folded with chocolate spread, banana, and toasted hazelnuts.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp melted butter
  • 1/2 cup toasted hazelnuts
  • 80 g dark chocolate
  • 1 tbsp cocoa
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 banana, sliced
  • powdered sugar

Why You'll Love It: Thin crepes and chocolate hazelnut spread feel instantly special.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Measure the flour, sugar, dairy, eggs, fruit, or chocolate, then prepare the pan, mold, or serving cups before mixing.
  2. Make the batter, custard, dough, cream, or fruit filling from the listed ingredients, mixing only until the texture is smooth or just combined.
  3. Cook, bake, chill, steam, or set the mixture until the dessert reaches the proper texture, watching closely so it stays tender rather than dry.
  4. Shape, pour, layer, or portion the Chocolate Hazelnut Crepes mixture carefully so the finished dessert sets evenly.
  5. Cool, chill, or rest the dessert as needed, then garnish and serve once the texture has set properly.
