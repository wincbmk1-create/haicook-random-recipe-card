Crispy Smashed Potatoes

Easy

Crispy smashed potatoes are golden, crunchy, and tender inside. Baby potatoes are boiled, gently crushed, brushed with garlic oil, and roasted until the edges crisp beautifully.

Ingredients

  • 600 g baby potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp parsley
  • 2 tbsp parmesan
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Crisp edges and fluffy centers make these potatoes hard to stop eating.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Boil potatoes until fork-tender.
  2. Drain and gently smash each potato on a tray.
  3. Brush with olive oil, garlic, salt, and pepper.
  4. Roast until deeply golden and crisp.
  5. Finish with parmesan and parsley.
