Butternut Squash Soup

Easy

Roasted butternut squash is blended until silky smooth, then gently spiced with warm nutmeg and finished with a touch of cream for extra body. Roasting the squash first deepens its natural sweetness and adds a subtle caramelized flavor you can't get from boiling alone. This velvety soup captures the essence of autumn in a single comforting bowl.

Ingredients

  • 1 large butternut squash, cubed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: A silky soup that tastes like fall in a bowl.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Toss the squash cubes with olive oil and roast at 200°C (400°F) for 30 minutes until tender and caramelized.
  2. Sauté the onion in a pot until softened.
  3. Add the roasted squash and vegetable broth, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Blend until smooth, then stir in the nutmeg and cream.
  5. Warm through gently, season to taste, and serve.
