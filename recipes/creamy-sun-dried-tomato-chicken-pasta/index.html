Creamy Sun-Dried Tomato Chicken Pasta

Easy

Creamy sun-dried tomato chicken pasta is rich, savory, and packed with flavor. Sun-dried tomatoes bring sweetness and intensity, while spinach and cream make the sauce colorful and silky. It is a dependable dinner when you want something hearty and polished.

Ingredients

  • 350g fusilli or penne
  • 2 chicken breasts, sliced
  • 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup cream
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 1/3 cup parmesan
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Sun-dried tomatoes add bold flavor to a creamy chicken pasta.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Season and sear chicken until cooked through.
  2. Saute garlic and sun-dried tomatoes in the same pan.
  3. Add cream and parmesan, simmering until smooth.
  4. Wilt spinach into the sauce.
  5. Toss with cooked pasta and sliced chicken.
