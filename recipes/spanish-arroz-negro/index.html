Spanish Arroz Negro

Medium

Arroz negro is a dramatic Spanish rice dish colored with squid ink and enriched with seafood flavor. Garlic, peppers, and stock give the rice depth, while shrimp or squid add a briny finish. A spoonful of aioli on the side makes it especially good.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups short-grain rice
  • 2 tsp squid ink
  • 300g shrimp or squid
  • 3 cups seafood stock
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 roasted red pepper, diced
  • olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Black rice with seafood feels bold, coastal, and memorable.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Spanish Arroz Negro, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
