Potato Leek Soup

Easy

Sweet, mild leeks are gently sautéed before simmering with potatoes and broth until everything turns soft enough to blend into a silky, velvety soup. A splash of cream at the end rounds out the flavor and gives it a luxuriously smooth finish. It's the kind of soothing, elegant soup that feels equally at home on a chilly weeknight or as the starter to a special dinner.

Ingredients

  • 3 leeks, sliced (white and light green parts)
  • 4 potatoes, chopped
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 2 tbsp butter

Why You'll Love It: A velvety soup that's perfect for chilly evenings.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Melt butter in a pot and sauté the leeks until soft, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the chopped potatoes and vegetable broth, and bring to a simmer.
  3. Cook for 20 minutes until the potatoes are very tender.
  4. Blend the soup until smooth using an immersion blender.
  5. Stir in the cream, season to taste, and serve warm.
