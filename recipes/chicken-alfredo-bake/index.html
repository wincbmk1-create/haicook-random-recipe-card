Chicken Alfredo Bake

Easy

Chicken Alfredo bake is creamy, cheesy, and built for sharing. Pasta and chicken are folded into Alfredo sauce, topped with mozzarella and parmesan, then baked until bubbling. It is straightforward comfort food with leftovers that reheat well.

Ingredients

  • 350g dry penne
  • 1 raw chicken breast or thigh
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 2 cups Alfredo sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella
  • 1/3 cup parmesan
  • 1 cup broccoli, optional

Why You'll Love It: Creamy chicken pasta bakes into a bubbling comfort dish.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Chicken Alfredo Bake, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
