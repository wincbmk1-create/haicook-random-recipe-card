Creamy Asparagus Soup

Easy

Fresh asparagus and leek simmer into a pale green soup that tastes delicate, clean, and springlike. A splash of cream rounds the edges while lemon keeps it from feeling heavy. It is elegant enough for a starter and simple enough for a weekday lunch.

Ingredients

  • 2 bunches asparagus
  • 1 leek or onion, sliced
  • 3 cups broth
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • lemon juice
  • fresh herbs

Why You'll Love It: A light green soup that feels polished but cooks quickly.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Trim asparagus and reserve a few tips for garnish.
  2. Cook leek or onion in butter until soft.
  3. Add asparagus and broth, then simmer until tender.
  4. Blend until smooth and stir in cream.
  5. Season with lemon juice and garnish with asparagus tips and herbs.
