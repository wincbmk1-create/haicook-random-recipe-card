Mushroom Miso Soup

Easy

Mushroom miso soup is earthy, light, and calming. Mushrooms, tofu, and scallions float in a savory miso broth with gentle umami.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp miso paste
  • 3 cups dashi or broth
  • 1 cup mushrooms
  • 1/2 cup tofu cubes
  • 2 scallions
  • wakame
  • sesame oil

Why You'll Love It: Mushrooms add depth to a simple miso broth.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Simmer mushrooms in broth until tender.
  2. Add tofu and wakame.
  3. Turn off heat and whisk in miso.
  4. Ladle into bowls.
  5. Top with scallions and a little sesame oil.
