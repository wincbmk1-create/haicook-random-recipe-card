Turkey Club Sandwich

Easy

The turkey club is a deli classic for good reason: toasted bread, tender turkey, crisp lettuce, juicy tomato, and smoky bacon all stack into one satisfying bite. A thin layer of mayo keeps everything creamy without weighing it down. Cut diagonally, it is lunchbox-friendly and instantly familiar.

Ingredients

  • 3 slices sandwich bread, toasted
  • 120g sliced turkey
  • 2 strips bacon
  • 2 lettuce leaves
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: A stacked sandwich with crisp toast and satisfying layers.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the fillings from raw ingredients: wash and slice vegetables, season any meat or seafood, and mix spreads or sauces from their listed components.
  2. Cook raw proteins or vegetables until tender, then toast or warm the bread so it can hold the filling without turning soggy.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Layer the cooked fillings, fresh toppings, and sauce into the bread for Turkey Club Sandwich while balancing moisture and crunch.
  5. Serve while the bread is warm or crisp, adding delicate herbs, greens, or pickles at the very end.
