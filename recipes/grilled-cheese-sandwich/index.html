Grilled Cheese Sandwich

Easy

There's something timeless about the combination of buttery, golden-crisp bread and a gooey center of melted cheddar cheese. Cooked low and slow in a pan, the outside develops a satisfying crunch while the inside turns perfectly stretchy and melty. It's the kind of nostalgic, no-fuss comfort food that never fails to hit the spot, whatever the occasion.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices white or sandwich bread
  • 2 slices cheddar cheese
  • 2 tbsp butter, softened

Why You'll Love It: A classic comfort food that never goes out of style.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Butter one side of each bread slice.
  2. Place cheese between the slices with the buttered sides facing out.
  3. Cook in a skillet over medium-low heat for 3-4 minutes per side.
  4. Press gently with a spatula until the cheese melts and the bread turns golden.
  5. Slice diagonally and serve hot.
