Molten Chocolate Cake

Medium

Rich chocolate cake batter is baked just until the edges set while the center stays deliberately underbaked, creating a warm, gooey molten core that oozes out the moment you cut into it. Timing is everything here, so pulling it from the oven at just the right moment is key to that dramatic reveal. It's a decadent, romantic dessert that turns any evening into something special.

Ingredients

  • 115g dark chocolate
  • 115g butter
  • 2 eggs plus 2 egg yolks
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup flour

Why You'll Love It: A gooey molten center that makes every bite feel special.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F) and butter individual ramekins.
  2. Melt the chocolate and butter together until smooth.
  3. Whisk the eggs, egg yolks, and sugar until pale and thick.
  4. Fold in the melted chocolate mixture, then gently fold in the flour.
  5. Divide between ramekins and bake for 10-12 minutes until the edges are set but the center is still soft; invert onto plates and serve immediately.
