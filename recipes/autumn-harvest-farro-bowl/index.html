Autumn Harvest Farro Bowl

Easy

Autumn harvest farro bowl is cozy, colorful, and nutty. Farro, roasted squash, apples, cranberries, kale, pecans, and maple vinaigrette make a seasonal bowl.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked farro
  • 1 cup roasted squash
  • 1 apple, sliced
  • 1/4 cup cranberries
  • 2 cups kale
  • 2 tbsp pecans
  • maple vinaigrette

Why You'll Love It: Roasted squash and apples make farro feel autumnal.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Massage kale with a little dressing.
  2. Add cooked farro to a bowl.
  3. Top with squash, apple, and cranberries.
  4. Add pecans.
  5. Drizzle with maple vinaigrette.
