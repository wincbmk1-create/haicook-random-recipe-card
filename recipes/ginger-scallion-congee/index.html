Ginger Scallion Congee

Easy

Congee turns a small amount of rice into a silky, soothing bowl. Ginger adds warmth, scallions bring freshness, and a drizzle of sesame oil makes the whole dish fragrant. It is easy to customize with chicken, mushrooms, egg, or crisp toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup jasmine rice
  • 8 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 4 slices ginger
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • white pepper to taste
  • salt to taste

Why You'll Love It: A soft rice porridge that feels calm, warm, and nourishing.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Ginger Scallion Congee, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
