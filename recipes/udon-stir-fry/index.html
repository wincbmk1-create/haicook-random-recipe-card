Udon Stir Fry

Easy

Thick, chewy udon noodles are stir-fried with crisp seasonal vegetables in a glossy, savory soy-based sauce that clings to every strand. Their satisfying bounce makes each bite feel substantial and comforting, even though the dish itself is quick and simple to prepare. It's an easy weeknight option when you want something warm, hearty, and packed with umami flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions fresh udon noodles
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 carrot, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil

Why You'll Love It: Thick, chewy noodles tossed in a glossy savory glaze.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Loosen the udon noodles under warm water and drain well.
  2. Heat oil in a wok and stir-fry broccoli, carrot, and bell pepper until tender-crisp.
  3. Add the udon noodles to the wok along with soy sauce and oyster sauce.
  4. Toss everything together over high heat until the noodles are well coated and heated through.
  5. Serve immediately while hot.
