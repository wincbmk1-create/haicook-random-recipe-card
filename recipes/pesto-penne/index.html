Pesto Penne

Easy

Vibrant basil pesto — bright with garlic, pine nuts, and parmesan — needs no cooking at all, so it comes together while your penne boils. Sweet burst cherry tomatoes add juicy freshness, and a scattering of toasted pine nuts brings a satisfying crunch. It's a wonderfully fast, herb-forward dinner that tastes like a little taste of an Italian summer, any time of year.

Ingredients

  • 350g penne
  • 1/2 cup basil pesto
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp pine nuts, toasted
  • grated parmesan, to serve

Why You'll Love It: A no-cook sauce that tastes like summer in a bowl.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook penne in salted boiling water until al dente; reserve 1/4 cup pasta water and drain.
  2. Toss the hot pasta with basil pesto, loosening with a little pasta water if needed.
  3. Fold in the halved cherry tomatoes.
  4. Sprinkle with toasted pine nuts and grated parmesan.
  5. Serve warm or at room temperature.
