Fluffy Pancakes

Easy

A simple batter of flour, eggs, and milk is whisked just enough to keep the pancakes tender, then cooked in a hot pan until each one puffs up light and fluffy. Stacked high and finished with a generous pour of warm maple syrup, they feel indulgent enough to double as a dessert. It's a weekend breakfast tradition that the whole family looks forward to.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1.25 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp melted butter
  • maple syrup, to serve

Why You'll Love It: A weekend breakfast treat that doubles as dessert.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Whisk together flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir just until combined (a few lumps are fine).
  4. Heat a lightly greased skillet over medium heat and pour in batter for each pancake.
  5. Cook until bubbles form on top, flip, and cook until golden; serve stacked with maple syrup.
