Watermelon Feta Salad

Easy

Watermelon feta salad is crisp, juicy, and built for warm days. Sweet watermelon cubes meet salty feta, cool cucumber, mint, and a simple lime dressing for a salad that tastes clean and lively. It is fast to assemble and looks beautiful with almost no effort.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups watermelon, cubed
  • 1 cucumber, ribboned or sliced
  • 1/2 cup feta, crumbled
  • 1/4 cup fresh mint
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Sweet watermelon, salty feta, and mint make a bright summer bowl.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Combine watermelon and cucumber in a large bowl.
  2. Whisk lime juice with olive oil and a pinch of salt.
  3. Drizzle the dressing over the salad and toss gently.
  4. Scatter feta and mint over the top.
  5. Finish with black pepper and serve chilled.
