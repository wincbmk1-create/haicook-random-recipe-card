Thai Pumpkin Soup

Easy

Thai pumpkin soup is silky, fragrant, and gently spicy. Pumpkin puree simmers with coconut milk, curry paste, and lime for a bright orange bowl.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pumpkin puree
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp curry paste
  • 2 cups broth
  • 1 lime
  • pepitas
  • cilantro

Why You'll Love It: Pumpkin and coconut curry make a silky fragrant soup.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Warm curry paste in a pot until fragrant.
  2. Add pumpkin puree, broth, and coconut milk.
  3. Simmer until smooth and hot.
  4. Season with lime juice.
  5. Top with pepitas and cilantro.
