Japanese Onigiri

Easy

Onigiri are hand-shaped rice balls that have been a staple of Japanese home cooking and bento boxes for generations. Warm, lightly salted rice is pressed around a savory filling of flaked salmon, then wrapped in a crisp sheet of nori seaweed for a satisfying textural contrast. They travel beautifully, taste great at room temperature, and make an easy grab-and-go breakfast or lunch for busy mornings.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups water or broth
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 sheet nori, cut into strips
  • 1 salmon fillet
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • lemon wedge
  • water for wetting hands

Why You'll Love It: Portable rice balls perfect for lunchboxes and busy mornings.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Japanese Onigiri, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
