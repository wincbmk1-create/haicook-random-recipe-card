Mediterranean Pita Pockets

Easy

Mediterranean pita pockets are quick, colorful, and satisfying. Warm pita halves hold falafel, cucumber, tomato, lettuce, onion, herbs, and a cooling tzatziki sauce for a fresh handheld meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 pita breads, halved
  • 6 falafel balls
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • lettuce leaves
  • 1/4 red onion, sliced
  • 1/2 cup tzatziki
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: Soft pita pockets make crisp vegetables and falafel easy to love.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Warm pita halves until soft.
  2. Heat falafel according to package directions or pan-fry until crisp.
  3. Fill pita pockets with lettuce, falafel, cucumber, tomato, and onion.
  4. Spoon tzatziki over the filling.
  5. Garnish with parsley and serve.
