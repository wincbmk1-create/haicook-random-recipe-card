Arroz con Pollo

Medium

Arroz con pollo is a generous one-pan dish where chicken seasons the rice as it cooks. Bell peppers, peas, olives, and warm spices bring color and depth, while the rice absorbs all the savory juices. It is a family-style meal that feels both rustic and celebratory.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 chicken thighs or drumsticks
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1/3 cup olives
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric or saffron
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Chicken and golden rice cook together into a comforting one-pan meal.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Arroz con Pollo, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
