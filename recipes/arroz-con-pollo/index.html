Arroz con Pollo

Medium

Arroz con pollo is a generous one-pan dish where chicken seasons the rice as it cooks. Bell peppers, peas, olives, and warm spices bring color and depth, while the rice absorbs all the savory juices. It is a family-style meal that feels both rustic and celebratory.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 chicken thighs or drumsticks
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1/3 cup olives
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric or saffron
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Chicken and golden rice cook together into a comforting one-pan meal.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Brown the chicken in olive oil, then remove from the pan.
  2. Saute bell pepper until softened.
  3. Stir in rice, paprika, turmeric, broth, peas, and olives.
  4. Nestle the chicken back into the pan.
  5. Cover and simmer until the rice is tender and chicken is cooked through.
