Sesame Miso Ramen

Easy

Sesame miso ramen is creamy, cozy, and layered. Ramen noodles sit in miso broth enriched with sesame, then get topped with corn, greens, and a soft egg.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions ramen noodles
  • 2 tbsp miso paste
  • 1 tbsp sesame paste
  • 3 cups broth
  • 1/2 cup corn
  • 1 soft-boiled egg
  • greens

Why You'll Love It: Sesame and miso create a rich broth with gentle depth.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Heat broth and whisk in sesame paste.
  2. Turn off heat and whisk in miso.
  3. Cook ramen noodles separately.
  4. Add noodles to bowls and pour over broth.
  5. Top with corn, greens, and egg.
