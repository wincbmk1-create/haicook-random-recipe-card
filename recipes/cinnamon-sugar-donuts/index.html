Cinnamon Sugar Donuts

Medium

Cinnamon sugar donuts are warm, soft, and nostalgic. Fresh donuts are brushed with butter and rolled in cinnamon sugar for a simple bakery-style treat.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • cinnamon sugar

Why You'll Love It: Cinnamon sugar makes fresh donuts nostalgic and irresistible.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Mix dry ingredients with milk, egg, and butter.
  2. Shape or pipe into donut molds.
  3. Bake or fry until golden.
  4. Brush with melted butter.
  5. Roll in cinnamon sugar.
