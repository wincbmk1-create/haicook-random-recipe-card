Spinach Artichoke Flatbread

Easy

Spinach artichoke flatbread is creamy, cheesy, and shareable. A crisp flatbread is topped with spinach, artichokes, mozzarella, and cream cheese, then baked until bubbling. It has the comfort of a dip with the structure of a meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 flatbread
  • 1 cup spinach
  • 1/2 cup chopped artichokes
  • 1/3 cup cream cheese
  • 1/2 cup mozzarella
  • 1 clove garlic, minced

Why You'll Love It: A creamy dip-inspired topping turns flatbread into dinner.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Mix spinach, artichokes, cream cheese, garlic, and mozzarella.
  2. Spread mixture over the flatbread.
  3. Bake until edges are crisp and topping bubbles.
  4. Rest briefly before slicing.
  5. Serve warm.
