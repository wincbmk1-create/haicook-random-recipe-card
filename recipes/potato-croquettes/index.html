Potato Croquettes

Medium

Potato croquettes are crisp outside and creamy inside. Mashed potatoes are mixed with cheese and herbs, shaped, breaded, and fried until golden, making them a satisfying snack or side.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups mashed potatoes
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • fresh parsley
  • oil for frying
  • dipping sauce

Why You'll Love It: Golden breadcrumb shells hide soft, creamy potato centers.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Potato Croquettes, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
