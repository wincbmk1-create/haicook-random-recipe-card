Sopa de Fideo

Easy

Sopa de fideo starts by toasting short noodles until golden, then simmering them in a bright tomato broth. The noodles absorb the savory flavor as they soften, creating a quick and cozy soup. Avocado, cilantro, and lime make the finish fresh and colorful.

Ingredients

  • 200g fideo or broken vermicelli
  • 1 can tomatoes
  • 1/2 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • fresh cilantro
  • lime wedges

Why You'll Love It: Toasted noodles in tomato broth make a quick, comforting bowl.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and chop the vegetables, then cut any raw meat, seafood, tofu, or aromatics into even pieces before heating the pot.
  2. Saute the aromatics in a little fat until fragrant, then add spices or tomato paste so the soup base develops flavor before liquid goes in.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Stir the Sopa de Fideo gently as it finishes so the ingredients stay evenly distributed and the broth tastes balanced.
  5. Taste the soup, adjust salt and brightness, then ladle into bowls with fresh herbs or toppings.
