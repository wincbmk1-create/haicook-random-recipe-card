Vietnamese Vermicelli Salad Bowl

Medium

This Vietnamese-inspired bowl layers cool rice vermicelli with grilled lemongrass chicken, cucumber, carrots, herbs, peanuts, lime, and a light dressing. It is fresh, fragrant, and full of texture without feeling heavy. The contrast of warm chicken and cool noodles makes every bite lively.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups washed salad greens or vegetables
  • 200g rice vermicelli
  • 2 chicken breasts, grilled and sliced
  • 1 cucumber, julienned
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • fresh mint and cilantro
  • 1/4 cup peanuts, crushed
  • 1 lime
  • nuoc cham or light dressing

Why You'll Love It: Cool noodles, herbs, and grilled chicken make a bright meal bowl.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash and dry the greens or vegetables thoroughly, then slice the fresh toppings and measure the dressing ingredients.
  2. If the recipe includes grains, meat, tofu, seafood, or potatoes, cook them from raw and let them cool slightly; otherwise keep the salad components crisp and fresh.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Arrange the washed greens, toppings, and dressing for Vietnamese Vermicelli Salad Bowl, keeping crisp items separate until serving if needed.
  5. Toss just before serving, then adjust salt, acidity, herbs, or crunchy toppings to taste.
