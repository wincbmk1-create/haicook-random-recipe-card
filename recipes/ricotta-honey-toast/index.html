Ricotta Honey Toast

Easy

Ricotta honey toast is simple but quietly elegant. Thick sourdough is toasted and topped with creamy ricotta, figs, pistachios, lemon zest, and honey for a balance of richness, fruit, crunch, and brightness.

Ingredients

  • 2 thick slices sourdough
  • 1/2 cup ricotta
  • 2 figs, quartered
  • 2 tbsp pistachios, chopped
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • black pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: Creamy ricotta, fruit, and honey make toast feel refined.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Toast sourdough until crisp at the edges.
  2. Spread ricotta generously over each slice.
  3. Top with figs and pistachios.
  4. Drizzle with honey.
  5. Finish with lemon zest and a little black pepper.
