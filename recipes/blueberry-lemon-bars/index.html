Blueberry Lemon Bars

Medium

Blueberry lemon bars are tangy, buttery, and colorful. A crisp shortbread base holds lemon custard dotted with blueberries and a light dusting of sugar.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup lemon juice
  • 1 cup blueberries
  • lemon zest

Why You'll Love It: Blueberries and lemon make buttery bars vivid and fresh.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Bake a simple shortbread crust.
  2. Whisk eggs, sugar, lemon juice, and zest.
  3. Pour filling over the crust.
  4. Scatter blueberries on top.
  5. Bake until just set and cool fully.
