Spicy Dan Dan Udon

Medium

Spicy dan dan udon is bold, nutty, and satisfying. Thick udon noodles are coated with sesame-chili sauce and topped with savory pork and greens for a rich bowl with heat.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions udon
  • 120 g ground pork
  • 1 tbsp chili oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame paste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 cup bok choy
  • scallions

Why You'll Love It: Chewy udon gives dan dan flavors extra comfort.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Brown pork until crisp at the edges.
  2. Whisk chili oil, sesame paste, and soy sauce.
  3. Cook udon and bok choy.
  4. Toss udon with sauce.
  5. Top with pork, greens, and scallions.
