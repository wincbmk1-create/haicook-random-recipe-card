Potato Gratin

Medium

Potato gratin turns thinly sliced potatoes into a golden, creamy bake with tender layers and a browned top. A little cheese and thyme add depth without overpowering the potatoes. It works as a cozy main with salad or as a polished side for a special dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1kg potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1.5 cups cream
  • 1 cup grated gruyere or cheddar
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: Thin potato layers bake into a creamy, bubbling side dish.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 190C (375F) and butter a baking dish.
  2. Layer sliced potatoes with garlic, thyme, salt, pepper, and cheese.
  3. Pour cream evenly over the layers.
  4. Bake covered for 35 minutes, then uncover and bake 20 minutes more.
  5. Rest for 10 minutes before serving so the layers set.
