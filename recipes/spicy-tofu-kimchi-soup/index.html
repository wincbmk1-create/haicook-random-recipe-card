Spicy Tofu Kimchi Soup

Easy

Spicy tofu kimchi soup is tangy, hot, and deeply savory. Kimchi, tofu, gochugaru, scallions, and broth simmer into a bold red soup.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup kimchi
  • 200 g tofu
  • 3 cups broth
  • 1 tsp gochugaru
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • scallions
  • soy sauce

Why You'll Love It: Kimchi broth and tofu make a bold warming soup.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute kimchi in sesame oil.
  2. Add gochugaru and broth.
  3. Simmer for a few minutes.
  4. Add tofu and warm gently.
  5. Finish with scallions and soy sauce.
