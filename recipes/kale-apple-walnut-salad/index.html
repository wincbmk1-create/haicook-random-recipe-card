Kale Apple Walnut Salad

Easy

This kale salad is hearty enough for meal prep but still fresh and bright. Massaging the kale with lemon dressing softens the leaves, while apple slices, walnuts, cranberries, and shaved parmesan add layers of sweet, salty, and crunchy texture. It holds up beautifully in the fridge.

Ingredients

  • 1 bunch kale, chopped
  • 1 apple, thinly sliced
  • 1/3 cup walnuts, toasted
  • 1/4 cup dried cranberries
  • 1/3 cup shaved parmesan
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Massaged kale turns tender while apples and walnuts add crunch.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Whisk lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  2. Massage the dressing into chopped kale until the leaves soften.
  3. Add apple slices, walnuts, and cranberries.
  4. Toss gently to combine.
  5. Finish with shaved parmesan before serving.
