Smoked Turkey Pesto Sandwich

Easy

Smoked turkey pesto sandwich is fresh, savory, and easy to pack. Whole grain bread holds smoked turkey, provolone, spinach, and a swipe of pesto for a balanced lunch with herbal depth.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices whole grain bread
  • 4 slices smoked turkey
  • 1 slice provolone
  • 1 cup fresh basil
  • 2 tbsp grated parmesan
  • 2 tbsp nuts or seeds
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 cup spinach
  • 2 tomato slices

Why You'll Love It: Pesto and smoked turkey make a simple sandwich feel layered.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the fillings from raw ingredients: wash and slice vegetables, season any meat or seafood, and mix spreads or sauces from their listed components.
  2. Cook raw proteins or vegetables until tender, then toast or warm the bread so it can hold the filling without turning soggy.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Layer the cooked fillings, fresh toppings, and sauce into the bread for Smoked Turkey Pesto Sandwich while balancing moisture and crunch.
  5. Serve while the bread is warm or crisp, adding delicate herbs, greens, or pickles at the very end.
