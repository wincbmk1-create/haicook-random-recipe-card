Roasted Sweet Potato Kale Salad

Easy

Roasted sweet potato kale salad is earthy, sweet, and full of texture. Tender roasted sweet potatoes, kale, cranberries, walnuts, and goat cheese make a colorful bowl.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups kale
  • 1 sweet potato, cubed
  • 1/4 cup cranberries
  • 1/4 cup walnuts
  • 1/4 cup goat cheese
  • 2 tbsp vinaigrette
  • olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Roasted sweet potato softens kale with warm sweetness.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash and dry the greens or vegetables thoroughly, then slice the fresh toppings and measure the dressing ingredients.
  2. If the recipe includes grains, meat, tofu, seafood, or potatoes, cook them from raw and let them cool slightly; otherwise keep the salad components crisp and fresh.
  3. Whisk the dressing from oil, acid, salt, herbs, or spices, tasting for balance before it touches the salad.
  4. Arrange the washed greens, toppings, and dressing for Roasted Sweet Potato Kale Salad, keeping crisp items separate until serving if needed.
  5. Toss just before serving, then adjust salt, acidity, herbs, or crunchy toppings to taste.
