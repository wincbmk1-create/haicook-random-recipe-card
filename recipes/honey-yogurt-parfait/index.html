Honey Yogurt Parfait

Easy

Honey yogurt parfait is creamy, light, and naturally sweet. Greek yogurt layers with berries, granola, walnuts, and honey for a simple dessert cup.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 cup berries
  • 1/2 cup granola
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp walnuts
  • mint

Why You'll Love It: Honey and yogurt make a fresh layered dessert.
