Green Pea Mint Soup

Easy

Green pea mint soup is vivid, fresh, and gently creamy. Sweet peas blend with mint, broth, lemon, and a swirl of cream for a bright bowl.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups green peas
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 1/4 cup mint
  • 1/4 cup cream
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Peas and mint make a vivid soup that feels light and clean.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Simmer peas in broth until tender.
  2. Add mint and blend until smooth.
  3. Stir in cream.
  4. Season with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  5. Serve warm or chilled.
