Potato Gnocchi

Medium

Soft mashed potatoes are gently worked with flour and egg into a tender dough, then rolled and cut into small pillowy dumplings that cook in just a minute or two of boiling water. The key is a light touch — overworking the dough makes them dense instead of fluffy. Tossed simply in butter and sage or a rich tomato sauce, gnocchi feels like a warm, comforting hug on a plate.

Ingredients

  • 1kg potatoes
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 egg
  • salt to taste
  • butter and sage, to serve

Why You'll Love It: Soft potato dumplings that feel like a warm hug on a plate.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Boil the potatoes whole with skins on until tender; drain and let cool slightly.
  2. Peel and mash the potatoes while still warm until smooth.
  3. Mix in the egg and flour gradually to form a soft dough.
  4. Roll the dough into ropes and cut into small pillow-shaped pieces.
  5. Boil the gnocchi in batches until they float to the surface, then toss with butter and sage.
