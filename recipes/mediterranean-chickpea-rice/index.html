Mediterranean Chickpea Rice

Easy

This bowl turns rice into a fresh Mediterranean-style meal with chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, olives, and feta. Lemon and herbs keep the flavors bright, while chickpeas make it filling enough for lunch or dinner. It is colorful, balanced, and easy to prep ahead.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup feta
  • 1/3 cup olives
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: A colorful rice bowl with bright vegetables and creamy feta.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Fluff warm or cooled rice in a large bowl.
  2. Add chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, olives, and feta.
  3. Whisk olive oil with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  4. Toss everything gently with the dressing.
  5. Finish with fresh parsley before serving.
