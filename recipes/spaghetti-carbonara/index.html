Spaghetti Carbonara

Medium

True carbonara gets its silky sauce not from cream, but from a careful blend of whisked eggs and salty pecorino cheese, tossed with hot pasta and crisped pancetta off the heat. The residual warmth gently cooks the eggs into a glossy, luxurious coating rather than scrambling them. It takes a bit of technique to get the timing right, but the payoff is one of Italy's most iconic and satisfying pasta dishes.

Ingredients

  • 350g spaghetti
  • 150g pancetta, diced
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 cup grated pecorino
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • black pepper, freshly cracked

Why You'll Love It: A silky, no-cream classic that's deceptively simple to make.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente; reserve 1/2 cup pasta water and drain.
  2. Crisp the pancetta in a pan with garlic until golden; discard garlic.
  3. Whisk eggs with pecorino and a generous amount of black pepper in a bowl.
  4. Off the heat, toss the hot spaghetti with the pancetta, then quickly mix in the egg mixture, adding pasta water to create a silky sauce.
  5. Serve immediately with extra pecorino and pepper on top.
