Chicken Khao Soi

Medium

Chicken khao soi is fragrant, creamy, and full of contrast. Tender chicken and egg noodles sit in coconut curry broth, finished with crispy noodles, herbs, and lime.

Ingredients

  • 200 g egg noodles
  • 1 chicken thigh, sliced
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp curry paste
  • 1 cup broth
  • crispy noodles
  • lime wedges

Why You'll Love It: Creamy curry and crispy noodles make every bite layered.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Simmer coconut milk with curry paste.
  2. Add chicken and broth and cook until tender.
  3. Boil egg noodles until just done.
  4. Place noodles in bowls and ladle curry over them.
  5. Top with crispy noodles and lime.
