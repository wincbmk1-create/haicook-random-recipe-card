Beef Barley Soup

Medium

Beef barley soup is built on browned beef, vegetables, and broth simmered until the meat turns tender. Pearl barley adds a chewy, nutty texture that makes the bowl especially filling. It is robust, savory, and ideal for cooler days.

Ingredients

  • 400g beef chuck, cubed
  • 3/4 cup barley
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 celery stalk, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 5 cups beef broth
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: Slow-simmered beef and chewy barley make it deeply satisfying.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Brown beef cubes in a heavy pot, then remove briefly.
  2. Saute onion, carrot, and celery until softened.
  3. Stir in tomato paste, beef, broth, and barley.
  4. Simmer for 60-75 minutes until the beef and barley are tender.
  5. Season to taste and finish with parsley.
