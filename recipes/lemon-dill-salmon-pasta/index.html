Lemon Dill Salmon Pasta

Medium

This pasta pairs tender salmon with lemon, dill, and a light cream sauce. The flavor is rich but lifted by citrus and herbs, making it feel polished rather than heavy. It is a lovely dinner when you want seafood comfort with freshness.

Ingredients

  • 350g dry tagliatelle or linguine
  • 2 salmon fillets
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 2 tbsp dill, chopped
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Salmon, lemon, and dill make creamy pasta feel fresh.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Lemon Dill Salmon Pasta, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
