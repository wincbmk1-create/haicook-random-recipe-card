Pesto Tortellini Salad

Easy

Pesto tortellini salad is colorful, fast, and great at room temperature. Cheese tortellini are tossed with basil pesto, cherry tomatoes, mozzarella, and herbs for a bowl that feels fresh but still filling. It works beautifully for lunches, picnics, or a no-fuss dinner.

Ingredients

  • 400g cheese tortellini
  • 1 cup fresh basil
  • 2 tbsp grated parmesan
  • 2 tbsp nuts or seeds
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cup mini mozzarella
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Cheese tortellini and pesto make a bright make-ahead pasta salad.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Pesto Tortellini Salad, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
