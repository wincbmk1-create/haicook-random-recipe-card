Pesto Tortellini Salad

Easy

Pesto tortellini salad is colorful, fast, and great at room temperature. Cheese tortellini are tossed with basil pesto, cherry tomatoes, mozzarella, and herbs for a bowl that feels fresh but still filling. It works beautifully for lunches, picnics, or a no-fuss dinner.

Ingredients

  • 400g cheese tortellini
  • 1/2 cup basil pesto
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cup mini mozzarella
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh basil
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Cheese tortellini and pesto make a bright make-ahead pasta salad.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook tortellini until tender, then rinse briefly with cool water.
  2. Toss with pesto and olive oil.
  3. Fold in tomatoes and mozzarella.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Chill or serve at room temperature with basil.
