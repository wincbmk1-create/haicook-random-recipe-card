Potato Corn Chowder

Easy

Potato corn chowder is creamy, sweet, and comforting. Potatoes, corn, onion, and celery simmer together before cream gives the soup a soft, satisfying finish.

Ingredients

  • 3 potatoes, diced
  • 2 cups corn
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 celery stalk, diced
  • 3 cups broth
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • parsley

Why You'll Love It: Corn adds sweetness to a creamy potato soup bowl.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Potato Corn Chowder, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
