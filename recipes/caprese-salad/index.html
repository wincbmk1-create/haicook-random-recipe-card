Caprese Salad

Easy

Thick slices of ripe tomato and fresh mozzarella are layered together with fragrant basil leaves, then finished with a drizzle of sweet-tangy balsamic glaze and good olive oil. With only four main ingredients, the quality of each one really matters, so this is the perfect dish to make when tomatoes are at their peak. It's simple, elegant, and always looks impressive on the table.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups washed salad greens or vegetables
  • 3 large ripe tomatoes, sliced
  • 250g fresh mozzarella, sliced
  • handful of fresh basil leaves
  • 2 tbsp balsamic glaze
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: A simple 4-ingredient salad that always looks impressive.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash and dry the greens or vegetables thoroughly, then slice the fresh toppings and measure the dressing ingredients.
  2. If the recipe includes grains, meat, tofu, seafood, or potatoes, cook them from raw and let them cool slightly; otherwise keep the salad components crisp and fresh.
  3. Whisk the dressing from oil, acid, salt, herbs, or spices, tasting for balance before it touches the salad.
  4. Arrange the washed greens, toppings, and dressing for Caprese Salad, keeping crisp items separate until serving if needed.
  5. Toss just before serving, then adjust salt, acidity, herbs, or crunchy toppings to taste.
