Caprese Salad

Easy

Thick slices of ripe tomato and fresh mozzarella are layered together with fragrant basil leaves, then finished with a drizzle of sweet-tangy balsamic glaze and good olive oil. With only four main ingredients, the quality of each one really matters, so this is the perfect dish to make when tomatoes are at their peak. It's simple, elegant, and always looks impressive on the table.

Ingredients

  • 3 large ripe tomatoes, sliced
  • 250g fresh mozzarella, sliced
  • handful of fresh basil leaves
  • 2 tbsp balsamic glaze
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: A simple 4-ingredient salad that always looks impressive.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Arrange alternating slices of tomato and mozzarella on a platter.
  2. Tuck fresh basil leaves between the slices.
  3. Drizzle with olive oil and balsamic glaze.
  4. Season with salt and freshly cracked pepper.
  5. Serve immediately at room temperature for the best flavor.
