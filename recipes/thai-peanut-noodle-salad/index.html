Thai Peanut Noodle Salad

Easy

Thai peanut noodle salad is nutty, zesty, and crunchy. Chilled noodles are tossed with cabbage, carrots, herbs, peanuts, and a creamy peanut-lime dressing.

Ingredients

  • 200 g noodles
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 3 tbsp peanut dressing
  • 2 tbsp peanuts
  • cilantro
  • lime wedges

Why You'll Love It: Peanut-lime dressing makes chilled noodles bright and satisfying.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash and dry the greens or vegetables thoroughly, then slice the fresh toppings and measure the dressing ingredients.
  2. If the recipe includes grains, meat, tofu, seafood, or potatoes, cook them from raw and let them cool slightly; otherwise keep the salad components crisp and fresh.
  3. Whisk the dressing from oil, acid, salt, herbs, or spices, tasting for balance before it touches the salad.
  4. Arrange the washed greens, toppings, and dressing for Thai Peanut Noodle Salad, keeping crisp items separate until serving if needed.
  5. Toss just before serving, then adjust salt, acidity, herbs, or crunchy toppings to taste.
