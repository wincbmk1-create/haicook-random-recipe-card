Creamy Tomato Gnocchi

Easy

Creamy tomato gnocchi is soft, rich, and incredibly quick. The gnocchi simmer directly in a tomato cream sauce, absorbing flavor while staying pillowy. Basil and parmesan keep the dish fresh and savory.

Ingredients

  • 500g potato gnocchi
  • 1.5 cups tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1/3 cup grated parmesan
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh basil
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Pillowy gnocchi and tomato cream sauce are pure comfort.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Creamy Tomato Gnocchi, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
