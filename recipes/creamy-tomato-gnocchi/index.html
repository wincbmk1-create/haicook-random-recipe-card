Creamy Tomato Gnocchi

Easy

Creamy tomato gnocchi is soft, rich, and incredibly quick. The gnocchi simmer directly in a tomato cream sauce, absorbing flavor while staying pillowy. Basil and parmesan keep the dish fresh and savory.

Ingredients

  • 500g potato gnocchi
  • 1.5 cups tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1/3 cup grated parmesan
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh basil
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Pillowy gnocchi and tomato cream sauce are pure comfort.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute garlic in olive oil until fragrant.
  2. Add tomato sauce and cream; simmer gently.
  3. Stir in gnocchi and cook until tender.
  4. Fold in parmesan until the sauce thickens slightly.
  5. Finish with basil and black pepper.
