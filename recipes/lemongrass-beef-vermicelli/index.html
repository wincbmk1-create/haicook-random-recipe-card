Lemongrass Beef Vermicelli

Medium

Lemongrass beef vermicelli is fresh, aromatic, and colorful. Rice noodles are topped with seared lemongrass beef, herbs, cucumber, and nuoc cham for a bowl that feels light but flavorful.

Ingredients

  • 200 g rice dry vermicelli
  • 150 g beef slices
  • 1 stalk lemongrass, minced
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1/2 cucumber
  • fresh herbs
  • nuoc cham

Why You'll Love It: Lemongrass beef and herbs make vermicelli bright and satisfying.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Lemongrass Beef Vermicelli, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
