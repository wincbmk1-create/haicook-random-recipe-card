Carrot Ginger Soup

Easy

Carrots are simmered until tender with fresh ginger, onion, and broth, then blended into a smooth orange soup. Coconut milk adds a soft, creamy finish while ginger keeps each spoonful lively. It is clean, colorful, and light enough for lunch while still feeling cozy.

Ingredients

  • 700g carrots, sliced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1/2 cup coconut milk
  • pumpkin seeds
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Sweet carrots and fresh ginger make it sunny and warming.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute onion and ginger in olive oil until fragrant.
  2. Add carrots and broth, then simmer until the carrots are very tender.
  3. Blend until completely smooth.
  4. Stir in coconut milk and warm gently.
  5. Top with pumpkin seeds and a small drizzle of olive oil.
