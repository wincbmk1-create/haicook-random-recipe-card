Churros

Medium

Churros are crisp, ridged pastries rolled in cinnamon sugar while still warm. The dough is simple but fries into a golden shell with a tender center. Dip them in thick chocolate sauce and they become a joyful dessert for sharing.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • chocolate sauce

Why You'll Love It: Cinnamon sugar and chocolate sauce make every bite festive.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring water and butter to a boil, then stir in flour to form a dough.
  2. Beat in eggs once the dough cools slightly.
  3. Pipe strips of dough into hot oil.
  4. Fry until golden and crisp.
  5. Roll in cinnamon sugar and serve with chocolate sauce.
