Crispy Roasted Potatoes

Easy

Potato chunks are parboiled, then roughed up slightly before roasting in hot olive oil, which creates a wonderfully crispy, golden exterior while keeping the inside soft and fluffy. Fresh rosemary adds a fragrant, earthy note that pairs beautifully with the crunch. They're the classic finishing touch to a proper Sunday roast dinner, and disappear from the table fast.

Ingredients

  • 1kg potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
  • salt to taste

Why You'll Love It: The perfect crunchy side for any Sunday roast dinner.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Crispy Roasted Potatoes, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
