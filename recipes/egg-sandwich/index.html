Egg Sandwich

Easy

Soft-cooked eggs are gently folded with a touch of creamy mayo and piled between slices of soft bread with a layer of crisp lettuce for crunch. It's a simple, satisfying sandwich that comes together in minutes but keeps you full for hours thanks to the protein-rich filling. Perfect wrapped up for a grab-and-go breakfast or a quick lunchbox addition.

Ingredients

  • 4 slices soft bread
  • 3 eggs, hard-boiled
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • a few lettuce leaves
  • salt and pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: A protein-packed sandwich perfect for busy mornings.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Boil the eggs until hard-boiled, then peel and chop them.
  2. Mix chopped eggs with mayonnaise, salt, and pepper.
  3. Lay out the bread slices and top two of them with lettuce leaves.
  4. Spoon the egg mixture evenly over the lettuce.
  5. Top with the remaining bread slices, cut in half, and serve.
