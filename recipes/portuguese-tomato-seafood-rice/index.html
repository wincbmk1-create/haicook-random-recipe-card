Portuguese Tomato Seafood Rice

Medium

Portuguese-style tomato seafood rice is saucier than paella and full of bright seafood flavor. Rice simmers with tomato, garlic, paprika, shrimp, and mussels until tender but still spoonable. Parsley and lemon make it lively at the end.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups rice
  • 300g mixed seafood
  • 2 cups crushed tomatoes
  • 3 cups seafood stock
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • parsley and lemon

Why You'll Love It: Tomato seafood rice is brothy, bright, and full of coastal flavor.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute onion and garlic until softened.
  2. Add paprika, crushed tomatoes, and stock.
  3. Stir in rice and simmer until almost tender.
  4. Add seafood and cook until just done.
  5. Finish with parsley and lemon juice.
