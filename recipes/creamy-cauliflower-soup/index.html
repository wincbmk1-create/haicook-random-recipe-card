Creamy Cauliflower Soup

Easy

Creamy cauliflower soup is silky, mellow, and elegant. Cauliflower, onion, and garlic simmer until tender, then blend into a smooth soup with a touch of cream.

Ingredients

  • 1 cauliflower head
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 3 cups broth
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • chives
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Cauliflower blends into a naturally smooth, elegant soup.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute onion and garlic until fragrant.
  2. Add cauliflower and broth.
  3. Simmer until cauliflower is tender.
  4. Blend until smooth.
  5. Stir in cream and garnish with chives.
