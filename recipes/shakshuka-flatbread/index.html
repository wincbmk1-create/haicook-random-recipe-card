Shakshuka Flatbread

Easy

This flatbread turns the warm tomato-pepper flavors of shakshuka into a crisp, shareable meal. A soft egg sits in the center, feta adds salty creaminess, and fresh herbs brighten the rich sauce. It is perfect for brunch, lunch, or a quick dinner with a salad.

Ingredients

  • 2 flatbreads
  • 1 cup tomato pepper sauce
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup crumbled feta
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh parsley or cilantro
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • pinch of chili flakes

Why You'll Love It: A fun flatbread twist on saucy shakshuka flavors.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 220C (425F).
  2. Spread tomato pepper sauce over each flatbread and sprinkle with cumin.
  3. Crack an egg onto the center of each flatbread.
  4. Bake for 8-10 minutes until the flatbread is crisp and the egg is just set.
  5. Top with feta, herbs, chili flakes, and a drizzle of olive oil.
