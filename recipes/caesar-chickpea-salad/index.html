Caesar Chickpea Salad

Easy

Caesar chickpea salad is crunchy, savory, and satisfying. Romaine lettuce is tossed with Caesar dressing, parmesan, and crispy roasted chickpeas instead of croutons.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups romaine
  • 1 can chickpeas
  • 2 tbsp Caesar dressing
  • 1/4 cup parmesan
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • black pepper
  • lemon

Why You'll Love It: Crispy chickpeas make Caesar salad hearty and vegetarian.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Roast chickpeas with olive oil until crisp.
  2. Chop romaine and place in a bowl.
  3. Toss with Caesar dressing.
  4. Add parmesan and crispy chickpeas.
  5. Finish with black pepper and lemon.
