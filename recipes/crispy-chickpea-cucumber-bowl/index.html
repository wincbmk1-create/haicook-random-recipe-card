Crispy Chickpea Cucumber Bowl

Easy

Crispy chickpea cucumber bowl is crunchy, fresh, and cooling. Roasted chickpeas meet cucumber, tomatoes, herbs, and yogurt dressing for a lively vegetarian bowl.

Ingredients

  • 1 can chickpeas
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup yogurt dressing
  • mint
  • parsley
  • 1 tsp paprika

Why You'll Love It: Crispy chickpeas add crunch to a cool cucumber bowl.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Roast chickpeas with paprika until crisp.
  2. Combine cucumber, tomatoes, and herbs.
  3. Spoon into a bowl.
  4. Top with crispy chickpeas.
  5. Drizzle with yogurt dressing.
