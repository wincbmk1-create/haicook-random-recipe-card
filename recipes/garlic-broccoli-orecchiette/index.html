Garlic Broccoli Orecchiette

Easy

Orecchiette cups catch little pieces of broccoli, garlic, chili, and parmesan in every bite. The broccoli softens into the sauce while still keeping some texture. It is simple, green, and wonderfully savory.

Ingredients

  • 350g orecchiette
  • 3 cups broccoli florets
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp chili flakes
  • 1/3 cup parmesan
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Broccoli and garlic make a humble pasta taste deeply satisfying.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook orecchiette and broccoli together until pasta is al dente.
  2. Reserve pasta water, then drain.
  3. Saute garlic and chili flakes in olive oil.
  4. Toss pasta and broccoli in the garlic oil.
  5. Add parmesan and pasta water to make a light sauce.
