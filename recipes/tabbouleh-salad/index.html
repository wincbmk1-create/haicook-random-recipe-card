Tabbouleh Salad

Easy

Tabbouleh is a parsley-forward salad with bulgur, tomato, cucumber, mint, lemon, and olive oil. The herbs are the main event, making the salad taste intensely fresh and bright. It is excellent as a side, a mezze plate component, or a light lunch with hummus.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups washed salad greens or vegetables
  • 2 cups parsley, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup bulgur
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1/4 cup mint, chopped
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Parsley, lemon, and tomato make it clean and refreshing.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash and dry the greens or vegetables thoroughly, then slice the fresh toppings and measure the dressing ingredients.
  2. If the recipe includes grains, meat, tofu, seafood, or potatoes, cook them from raw and let them cool slightly; otherwise keep the salad components crisp and fresh.
  3. Whisk the dressing from oil, acid, salt, herbs, or spices, tasting for balance before it touches the salad.
  4. Arrange the washed greens, toppings, and dressing for Tabbouleh Salad, keeping crisp items separate until serving if needed.
  5. Toss just before serving, then adjust salt, acidity, herbs, or crunchy toppings to taste.
