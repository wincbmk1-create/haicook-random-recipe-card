Apple Pie

Medium

Tender apple slices are tossed with warm cinnamon and a touch of sugar, then baked between flaky, buttery layers of pie crust until golden and bubbling. The smell of cinnamon and baked apples filling the house is practically a tradition in itself. This timeless, nostalgic dessert has earned its place as the centerpiece of countless family gatherings and holiday tables.

Ingredients

  • 6 apples, peeled and sliced
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1.5 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 pie crusts (top and bottom)
  • 2 tbsp butter, cubed
  • 1 tbsp flour

Why You'll Love It: A timeless dessert perfect for family gatherings.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F) and line a pie dish with one crust.
  2. Toss the sliced apples with sugar, cinnamon, and flour.
  3. Fill the crust with the apple mixture and dot with butter.
  4. Cover with the second crust, sealing and crimping the edges, and cut a few vents on top.
  5. Bake for 45-50 minutes until the crust is golden and the filling is bubbling.
