Creamy Cajun Chicken Pasta

Easy

This creamy Cajun chicken pasta brings smoky spice, tender chicken, sweet bell pepper, and a velvety parmesan cream sauce together in one pan. It is bold without being heavy and makes a weeknight dinner feel a little more restaurant-worthy.

Ingredients

  • 350g penne
  • 2 chicken breasts, sliced
  • 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 cup cream
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Smoky spice and cream make a fast, satisfying pasta dinner.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Season chicken with Cajun spice and sear until cooked through.
  2. Saute bell pepper in the same pan until softened.
  3. Pour in cream and simmer gently.
  4. Stir in parmesan until smooth.
  5. Toss with cooked penne and sliced chicken before serving.
