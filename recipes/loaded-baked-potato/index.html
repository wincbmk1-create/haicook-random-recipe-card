Loaded Baked Potato

Easy

A whole potato is baked until the skin turns crisp and the inside becomes soft and fluffy, then split open and piled high with melted cheese, crispy bacon bits, and a generous dollop of cool sour cream. It's satisfying enough to be a meal on its own, yet flexible enough to customize with whatever toppings you have on hand. Perfect for busy nights when you want something hearty with minimal effort.

Ingredients

  • 4 large russet potatoes
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 4 strips bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 2 scallions, sliced

Why You'll Love It: A filling meal-in-one that's endlessly customizable.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F) and pierce each potato a few times with a fork.
  2. Bake the potatoes directly on the oven rack for 45-60 minutes until the skin is crisp and the inside is tender.
  3. Slice each potato open lengthwise and fluff the inside with a fork.
  4. Top with shredded cheese while hot so it melts, then add crumbled bacon.
  5. Finish with a dollop of sour cream and sliced scallions before serving.
