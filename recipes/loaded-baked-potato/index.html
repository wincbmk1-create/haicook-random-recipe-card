Loaded Baked Potato

Easy

A whole potato is baked until the skin turns crisp and the inside becomes soft and fluffy, then split open and piled high with melted cheese, crispy bacon bits, and a generous dollop of cool sour cream. It's satisfying enough to be a meal on its own, yet flexible enough to customize with whatever toppings you have on hand. Perfect for busy nights when you want something hearty with minimal effort.

Ingredients

  • 4 large russet potatoes
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 4 strips bacon, and crumbled
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 2 scallions, sliced

Why You'll Love It: A filling meal-in-one that's endlessly customizable.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Loaded Baked Potato, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
