Peanut Butter Blondies

Easy

Peanut butter blondies are chewy, nutty, and rich. Brown sugar and peanut butter create a dense bar with a salty-sweet finish.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • pinch salt

Why You'll Love It: Peanut butter gives blondies a chewy, salty-sweet bite.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Melt butter and mix with peanut butter.
  2. Stir in brown sugar, egg, and vanilla.
  3. Fold in flour and salt.
  4. Spread into a pan.
  5. Bake until edges are golden.
