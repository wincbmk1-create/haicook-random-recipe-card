Sourdough Avocado Egg Toast

Easy

Sourdough avocado egg toast is simple, colorful, and deeply satisfying. A crisp slice of sourdough holds mashed avocado, a soft egg, and a sprinkle of chili flakes. It is fresh enough for breakfast and hearty enough for a quick lunch.

Ingredients

  • 1 thick slice sourdough bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • pinch chili flakes
  • microgreens
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Creamy avocado and a runny egg make toast feel like a full meal.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the fillings from raw ingredients: wash and slice vegetables, season any meat or seafood, and mix spreads or sauces from their listed components.
  2. Cook raw proteins or vegetables until tender, then toast or warm the bread so it can hold the filling without turning soggy.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Layer the cooked fillings, fresh toppings, and sauce into the bread for Sourdough Avocado Egg Toast while balancing moisture and crunch.
  5. Serve while the bread is warm or crisp, adding delicate herbs, greens, or pickles at the very end.
