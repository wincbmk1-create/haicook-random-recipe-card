Tiramisu

Medium

Delicate ladyfinger biscuits are briefly dipped in strong coffee, then layered with a rich, airy mascarpone cream and dusted with cocoa powder. Since it needs to chill for a few hours before serving, the flavors have time to meld into something wonderfully cohesive and dreamy. This elegant, no-bake dessert tastes just like something from a fine Italian café, without ever needing to turn on the oven.

Ingredients

  • 250g mascarpone cheese
  • 2 eggs, separated
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 24 ladyfinger biscuits
  • 1.5 cups strong brewed coffee, cooled
  • cocoa powder, for dusting

Why You'll Love It: A no-bake dessert that tastes like a café treat.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Whisk egg yolks with sugar until pale, then fold in the mascarpone until smooth.
  2. Whip the egg whites to stiff peaks and gently fold into the mascarpone mixture.
  3. Quickly dip each ladyfinger into the cooled coffee and arrange in a single layer in a dish.
  4. Spread half the mascarpone cream over the ladyfingers, then repeat with another layer.
  5. Dust generously with cocoa powder and chill for at least 4 hours before serving.
