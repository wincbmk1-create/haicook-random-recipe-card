Garlic Shrimp Linguine

Medium

Plump shrimp are quickly seared in olive oil with plenty of garlic and a pinch of chili flakes, then tossed with linguine and a splash of the starchy pasta water to bring everything together into a light, glossy sauce. The whole dish takes barely 20 minutes from start to finish, but the bold garlicky, slightly spicy flavor tastes like something you'd order at a proper Italian restaurant.

Ingredients

  • 350g dry linguine
  • 400g shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 4 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp chili flakes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • fresh parsley, chopped

Why You'll Love It: A restaurant-style dish you can make on a busy weeknight.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Garlic Shrimp Linguine, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
