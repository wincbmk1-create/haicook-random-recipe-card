Korean Tuna Mayo Rice Bowl

Easy

This quick rice bowl combines warm rice with tuna mayo, cucumber, seaweed, sesame, and a drizzle of soy or gochujang sauce. It is creamy, salty, crisp, and incredibly fast. The bowl is perfect when you want something satisfying with very little cooking.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 can tuna, drained
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 sheet roasted seaweed, shredded
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • sesame seeds

Why You'll Love It: Pantry tuna becomes a creamy, savory rice bowl in minutes.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Mix tuna with mayonnaise and a little soy sauce.
  2. Spoon warm rice into bowls.
  3. Top with tuna mayo, cucumber, and shredded seaweed.
  4. Drizzle with sesame oil.
  5. Finish with sesame seeds before serving.
