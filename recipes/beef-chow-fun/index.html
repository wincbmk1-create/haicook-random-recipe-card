Beef Chow Fun

Medium

Beef chow fun is all about silky wide rice noodles, tender beef, crisp bean sprouts, and smoky wok flavor. A dark soy glaze coats everything lightly, giving the dish its signature savory depth without making it heavy.

Ingredients

  • 400g fresh wide rice noodles
  • 250g beef slices
  • 2 cups bean sprouts
  • 3 scallions, cut into pieces
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp oil

Why You'll Love It: Wide noodles and wok-charred beef make a deeply satisfying stir-fry.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Separate fresh rice noodles gently.
  2. Sear beef quickly in a hot wok and set aside.
  3. Stir-fry noodles with soy sauces and oyster sauce.
  4. Add bean sprouts, scallions, and beef back to the wok.
  5. Toss over high heat until glossy and lightly charred.
