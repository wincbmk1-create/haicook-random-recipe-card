Sweet Potato Breakfast Hash

Easy

Sweet potato breakfast hash is colorful, hearty, and sunny. Sweet potato cubes are cooked with peppers, onion, and sausage, then topped with eggs for a complete skillet meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 sweet potatoes, diced
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 1/2 onion
  • 100 g sausage, optional
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • paprika

Why You'll Love It: Sweet potatoes and eggs make a bright breakfast skillet.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Sweet Potato Breakfast Hash, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
