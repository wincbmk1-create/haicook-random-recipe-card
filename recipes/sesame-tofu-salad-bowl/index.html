Sesame Tofu Salad Bowl

Easy

Crispy tofu cubes sit over greens, cucumber, carrots, edamame, radish, and a sesame ginger dressing. The tofu adds warm, savory texture while the vegetables keep the bowl crisp and colorful. It is a satisfying plant-based lunch that still feels light.

Ingredients

  • 300g firm tofu, cubed
  • 3 cups mixed greens
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 carrot, shredded
  • 1 cup edamame
  • 4 radishes, sliced
  • sesame ginger dressing
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds

Why You'll Love It: Crispy tofu and sesame dressing make a satisfying meatless bowl.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash and dry the greens or vegetables thoroughly, then slice the fresh toppings and measure the dressing ingredients.
  2. If the recipe includes grains, meat, tofu, seafood, or potatoes, cook them from raw and let them cool slightly; otherwise keep the salad components crisp and fresh.
  3. Drain and pat dry the tofu, beans, or legumes, then season and warm them with aromatics until flavorful.
  4. Arrange the washed greens, toppings, and dressing for Sesame Tofu Salad Bowl, keeping crisp items separate until serving if needed.
  5. Toss just before serving, then adjust salt, acidity, herbs, or crunchy toppings to taste.
