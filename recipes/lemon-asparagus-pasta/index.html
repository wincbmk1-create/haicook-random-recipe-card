Lemon Asparagus Pasta

Easy

Lemon asparagus pasta is light, fragrant, and cheerful. Linguine is tossed with crisp asparagus, garlic olive oil, lemon zest, parmesan, and parsley. It is quick enough for lunch but polished enough for a relaxed dinner.

Ingredients

  • 350g dry linguine
  • 1 bunch asparagus, cut into pieces
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: Lemon and asparagus make pasta feel fresh, clean, and lively.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Lemon Asparagus Pasta, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
