Mushroom Barley Soup

Easy

Sliced mushrooms are browned until deeply savory, then simmered with pearl barley, carrots, celery, and broth. The barley turns tender and slightly chewy, giving the soup a satisfying texture that makes it feel like a full meal. It is earthy, humble, and wonderfully restorative.

Ingredients

  • 300g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 cup pearl barley
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 celery stalk, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 5 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: Chewy barley and mushrooms make it filling without feeling heavy.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and chop the vegetables, then cut any raw meat, seafood, tofu, or aromatics into even pieces before heating the pot.
  2. Saute the aromatics in a little fat until fragrant, then add spices or tomato paste so the soup base develops flavor before liquid goes in.
  3. Add the main ingredients and liquid, then simmer until the vegetables, grains, beans, or proteins are tender.
  4. Stir the Mushroom Barley Soup gently as it finishes so the ingredients stay evenly distributed and the broth tastes balanced.
  5. Taste the soup, adjust salt and brightness, then ladle into bowls with fresh herbs or toppings.
