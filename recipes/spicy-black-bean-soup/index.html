Spicy Black Bean Soup

Easy

Spicy black bean soup is smoky, hearty, and bright. Black beans simmer with tomatoes, cumin, and chili before avocado and lime bring freshness.

Ingredients

  • 2 cans black beans
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • avocado
  • lime wedges

Why You'll Love It: Black beans and chili make a hearty soup with deep flavor.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute onion until softened.
  2. Add tomato, cumin, and chili powder.
  3. Stir in black beans and a little water or broth.
  4. Simmer and mash slightly for thickness.
  5. Top with avocado and lime.
