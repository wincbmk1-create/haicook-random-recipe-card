Char Kway Teow

Medium

Char kway teow is a smoky Malaysian stir-fry with flat rice noodles, shrimp, Chinese sausage, egg, bean sprouts, garlic chives, and dark soy glaze. High heat gives the noodles their signature charred edges and savory depth.

Ingredients

  • 400g flat dry rice noodles
  • 200g shrimp
  • 1 Chinese sausage, sliced
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 cups bean sprouts
  • 1 cup garlic chives
  • 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp oil

Why You'll Love It: Dark soy glaze and wok heat create a rich street-food noodle plate.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Char Kway Teow, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
