Sausage and Kale Orecchiette

Easy

Sausage and kale orecchiette is rustic, hearty, and balanced. The pasta's little cup shape catches browned sausage, garlicky greens, chili flakes, and parmesan in every bite. It is a cozy one-pan style pasta with plenty of texture.

Ingredients

  • 350g orecchiette
  • 250g Italian sausage
  • 3 cups chopped kale
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 tsp chili flakes
  • grated parmesan

Why You'll Love It: The little pasta cups catch sausage, greens, and parmesan perfectly.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook orecchiette until al dente and reserve pasta water.
  2. Brown sausage in a skillet, breaking it into small pieces.
  3. Add garlic and chili flakes and cook briefly.
  4. Stir in kale until wilted.
  5. Toss with pasta, pasta water, and parmesan.
