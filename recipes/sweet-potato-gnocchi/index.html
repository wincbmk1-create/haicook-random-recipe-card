Sweet Potato Gnocchi

Medium

Sweet potato gnocchi is soft, golden, and gently sweet. The dumplings are tossed in sage butter for a cozy plate that feels special but still comforting.

Ingredients

  • 2 sweet potatoes
  • 1 egg
  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • fresh sage
  • parmesan
  • salt

Why You'll Love It: Sweet potato gives pillowy gnocchi a warm earthy sweetness.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Bake sweet potatoes until tender and mash them.
  2. Mix with egg, salt, and enough flour to form dough.
  3. Roll and cut into gnocchi pieces.
  4. Boil until they float.
  5. Toss with sage butter and parmesan.
