Roasted Red Pepper Soup

Easy

Roasted red peppers blend with tomato and garlic into a smooth, colorful soup that tastes both sweet and savory. A small swirl of cream rounds out the acidity, while basil adds freshness. It is simple, cheerful, and perfect with toasted bread.

Ingredients

  • 3 roasted red peppers
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1/4 cup cream
  • fresh basil
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: A smooth red-orange soup with sweet roasted pepper flavor.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute onion and garlic in olive oil until soft.
  2. Add roasted peppers, crushed tomatoes, and broth.
  3. Simmer for 15 minutes to let the flavors blend.
  4. Blend until smooth, then stir in cream.
  5. Serve with basil, black pepper, and toasted bread.
