Broccoli Cheddar Soup

Easy

Broccoli cheddar soup balances creamy richness with plenty of tender green florets. A simple roux gives the broth body, while shredded cheddar melts in at the end for a smooth, savory finish. It is familiar, generous, and perfect with toasted bread.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 carrot, shredded
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cups broth
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1.5 cups cheddar, shredded
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp flour

Why You'll Love It: A cheesy comfort bowl that still brings plenty of green.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Melt butter and cook onion until soft.
  2. Stir in flour and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Whisk in broth and milk until smooth.
  4. Add broccoli and carrot, then simmer until tender.
  5. Lower the heat, stir in cheddar, and season to taste.
