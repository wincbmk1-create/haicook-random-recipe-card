Caribbean Coconut Rice

Easy

This Caribbean staple swaps out plain water for rich coconut milk, giving the rice a subtly sweet, creamy backdrop that pairs beautifully with hearty beans and fragrant fresh thyme. It's traditionally served alongside grilled meats or fish, but it's satisfying enough to enjoy entirely on its own. One bite and you'll feel like you've been transported straight to a sunny island kitchen.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 can kidney beans, drained
  • 2 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • salt to taste

Why You'll Love It: Creamy coconut rice that brings island vibes to your dinner table.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Caribbean Coconut Rice, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
