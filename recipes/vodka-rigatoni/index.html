Vodka Rigatoni

Easy

Vodka rigatoni is glossy, creamy, and just tangy enough to stay balanced. Tomato paste cooks down with garlic before cream and parmesan turn it into a smooth sauce that clings to every ridge of pasta. Fresh basil adds a final lift, making it a weeknight bowl that still feels special.

Ingredients

  • 350g rigatoni
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1/3 cup grated parmesan
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh basil

Why You'll Love It: A silky tomato cream sauce makes rigatoni feel restaurant-worthy.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook rigatoni in salted boiling water until al dente; reserve pasta water.
  2. Saute garlic in olive oil until fragrant.
  3. Stir in tomato paste and cook until deepened in color.
  4. Add tomato sauce, cream, and parmesan to make a silky sauce.
  5. Toss with rigatoni, loosening with pasta water, and finish with basil.
