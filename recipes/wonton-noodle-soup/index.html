Wonton Noodle Soup

Medium

Wonton noodle soup is light, clear, and comforting. Thin egg noodles sit in golden broth with tender shrimp-pork wontons, bok choy, scallions, and a little sesame oil for a clean but satisfying bowl.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions thin egg noodles
  • 10 wontons
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 2 heads bok choy
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • white pepper

Why You'll Love It: Clear broth, springy noodles, and tender wontons feel soothing.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring broth to a gentle simmer.
  2. Cook wontons until they float and are cooked through.
  3. Blanch bok choy in the broth.
  4. Cook egg noodles separately and divide into bowls.
  5. Add wontons and bok choy, ladle broth over top, and finish with scallions and sesame oil.
