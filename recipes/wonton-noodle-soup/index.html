Wonton Noodle Soup

Medium

Wonton noodle soup is light, clear, and comforting. Thin egg noodles sit in golden broth with tender shrimp-pork wontons, bok choy, scallions, and a little sesame oil for a clean but satisfying bowl.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions thin dry egg noodles
  • 10 wontons
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 2 heads bok choy
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • white pepper

Why You'll Love It: Clear broth, springy noodles, and tender wontons feel soothing.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Wonton Noodle Soup, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
