Thai Pineapple Fried Rice

Easy

This fried rice balances sweet pineapple with savory shrimp, tender rice, and crunchy cashews. A little curry powder gives the rice a golden color, while peas and scallions keep it fresh. It feels festive but is still easy enough for a weeknight skillet meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked jasmine rice
  • 1 cup pineapple chunks
  • 200g shrimp, peeled
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1/3 cup cashews
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 scallions, sliced

Why You'll Love It: Pineapple and cashews make fried rice bright, crunchy, and fun.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Scramble the eggs in a hot wok, then set aside.
  2. Stir-fry shrimp until pink and just cooked.
  3. Add rice, curry powder, pineapple, and peas.
  4. Toss with soy sauce until the rice is evenly seasoned.
  5. Fold in eggs and cashews, then finish with scallions.
