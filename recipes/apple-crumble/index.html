Apple Crumble

Easy

Apple crumble is simple, cozy, and deeply satisfying. Cinnamon-spiced apples bake until tender under a buttery oat topping that turns golden and crisp. Serve it warm with vanilla ice cream and it becomes the kind of dessert that disappears quickly.

Ingredients

  • 5 apples, diced
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 6 tbsp cold butter

Why You'll Love It: Tender cinnamon apples and a golden oat topping are pure comfort.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Toss apples with sugar, cinnamon, and lemon juice in a baking dish.
  2. Mix oats, flour, brown sugar, and cold butter into crumbs.
  3. Scatter the crumble topping over the apples.
  4. Bake at 190C (375F) until bubbling and golden.
  5. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.
