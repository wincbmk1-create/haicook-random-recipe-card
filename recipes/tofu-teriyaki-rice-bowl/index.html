Tofu Teriyaki Rice Bowl

Easy

Crispy tofu cubes are coated in teriyaki sauce and served over rice with bright vegetables. The contrast of soft rice, sticky glaze, and crisp-tender broccoli makes every bite satisfying. It is an easy plant-based bowl that still feels hearty.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 400g firm tofu, cubed
  • 1/3 cup teriyaki sauce
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • sesame seeds and scallions

Why You'll Love It: Crispy tofu and glossy sauce make a satisfying meatless rice bowl.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Pat tofu dry and toss with cornstarch.
  2. Pan-fry tofu in sesame oil until golden on all sides.
  3. Steam broccoli until tender-crisp.
  4. Toss tofu with teriyaki sauce until glossy.
  5. Serve over rice with vegetables, sesame seeds, and scallions.
