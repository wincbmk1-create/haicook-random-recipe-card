Cherry Clafoutis

Easy

Cherry clafoutis is custardy, soft, and fruit-filled. Cherries bake in a simple egg batter until puffed and lightly golden.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cherries
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • butter

Why You'll Love It: Cherries bake into a soft custard-like French dessert.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Butter a baking dish and add cherries.
  2. Whisk eggs, milk, flour, sugar, and vanilla.
  3. Pour batter over cherries.
  4. Bake until puffed and set.
  5. Dust with sugar before serving.
