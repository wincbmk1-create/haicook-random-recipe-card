Potato Rosti with Smoked Salmon

Medium

Potato rosti with smoked salmon is crisp, savory, and elegant. Shredded potato is pan-fried into a golden cake, then topped with sour cream, smoked salmon, dill, and capers.

Ingredients

  • 2 large potatoes
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 salmon fillet
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • lemon wedge
  • 2 tbsp sour cream
  • dill
  • capers

Why You'll Love It: A crisp potato cake turns smoked salmon into a polished brunch plate.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Potato Rosti with Smoked Salmon, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
