Chicken Wild Rice Soup

Medium

Chicken wild rice soup is creamy, nutty, and nourishing. Chicken, wild rice, carrots, celery, and herbs simmer into a thick, comforting bowl.

Ingredients

  • 1 raw chicken breast or thigh
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 cup wild rice
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 celery stalk
  • 4 cups broth
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • thyme

Why You'll Love It: Wild rice gives chicken soup nutty texture and warmth.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and chop the vegetables, then cut any raw meat, seafood, tofu, or aromatics into even pieces before heating the pot.
  2. Saute the aromatics in a little fat until fragrant, then add spices or tomato paste so the soup base develops flavor before liquid goes in.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Stir the Chicken Wild Rice Soup gently as it finishes so the ingredients stay evenly distributed and the broth tastes balanced.
  5. Taste the soup, adjust salt and brightness, then ladle into bowls with fresh herbs or toppings.
