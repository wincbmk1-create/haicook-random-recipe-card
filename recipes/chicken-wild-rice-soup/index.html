Chicken Wild Rice Soup

Medium

Chicken wild rice soup is creamy, nutty, and nourishing. Chicken, wild rice, carrots, celery, and herbs simmer into a thick, comforting bowl.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked chicken
  • 1/2 cup wild rice
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 celery stalk
  • 4 cups broth
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • thyme

Why You'll Love It: Wild rice gives chicken soup nutty texture and warmth.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Simmer wild rice in broth until tender.
  2. Add carrot, celery, and thyme.
  3. Stir in chicken and warm through.
  4. Add cream near the end.
  5. Season and serve hot.
