Shanghai Scallion Oil Noodles

Easy

Shanghai scallion oil noodles are wonderfully simple. Scallions are slowly fried until dark and fragrant, then mixed with soy sauce and tossed through thin wheat noodles for a glossy, deeply aromatic bowl.

Ingredients

  • 300g thin wheat noodles
  • 6 scallions, cut into strips
  • 3 tbsp neutral oil
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • sesame seeds

Why You'll Love It: Fried scallion oil gives plain noodles deep savory fragrance.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Fry scallions slowly in oil until browned and crisp.
  2. Stir soy sauces and sugar into the scallion oil.
  3. Cook noodles until tender and drain.
  4. Toss noodles with scallion oil sauce.
  5. Top with fried scallions and sesame seeds.
