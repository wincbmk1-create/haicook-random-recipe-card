Shanghai Scallion Oil Noodles

Easy

Shanghai scallion oil noodles are wonderfully simple. Scallions are slowly fried until dark and fragrant, then mixed with soy sauce and tossed through thin wheat noodles for a glossy, deeply aromatic bowl.

Ingredients

  • 300g thin wheat dry noodles
  • 6 scallions, cut into strips
  • 3 tbsp neutral oil
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • sesame seeds

Why You'll Love It: Fried scallion oil gives plain noodles deep savory fragrance.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Shanghai Scallion Oil Noodles, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
