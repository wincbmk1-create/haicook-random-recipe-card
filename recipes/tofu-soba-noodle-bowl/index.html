Tofu Soba Noodle Bowl

Easy

Tofu soba noodle bowl is cool, nourishing, and balanced. Buckwheat noodles are paired with tofu, cucumber, edamame, and sesame dressing for a meal that feels light but complete.

Ingredients

  • 200 g dry soba noodles
  • 150 g tofu cubes
  • 1/2 cup edamame
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced
  • 2 tbsp sesame dressing
  • nori strips
  • scallions

Why You'll Love It: Soba, tofu, and sesame dressing make a nourishing bowl.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Drain and pat dry the tofu, beans, or legumes, then season and warm them with aromatics until flavorful.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Tofu Soba Noodle Bowl, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
