Shoyu Ramen

Medium

Shoyu ramen is a classic Japanese noodle bowl built around a clear, savory soy-based broth. Springy noodles, tender chashu, a jammy egg, bamboo shoots, scallions, and nori create a comforting bowl with clean, deep flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions ramen noodles
  • 4 cups chicken or dashi broth
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 slices chashu pork
  • 2 soft-boiled eggs
  • 1/2 cup bamboo shoots
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • nori sheets

Why You'll Love It: Clear soy broth and classic toppings make a balanced ramen bowl.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Warm broth with soy sauce until gently simmering.
  2. Cook ramen noodles according to package directions.
  3. Divide noodles between bowls and ladle broth over them.
  4. Top with chashu, eggs, bamboo shoots, scallions, and nori.
  5. Serve immediately while hot.
