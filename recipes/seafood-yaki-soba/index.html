Seafood Yaki Soba

Medium

Seafood yaki soba is savory, glossy, and full of texture. Shrimp, squid, cabbage, and noodles are stir-fried with sauce for a hearty plate that still feels fresh.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions yakisoba noodles
  • 6 shrimp
  • 1/2 cup squid rings
  • 1 cup cabbage
  • 2 tbsp yakisoba sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • scallions

Why You'll Love It: Seafood and stir-fried noodles make a satisfying coastal plate.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Stir-fry shrimp and squid quickly, then remove.
  2. Cook cabbage until slightly softened.
  3. Add noodles and sauce.
  4. Return seafood and toss until hot.
  5. Finish with scallions.
