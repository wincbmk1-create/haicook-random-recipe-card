Pad Thai

Medium

Pad Thai is a beloved Thai street food classic built around a bold balance of sweet, sour, and savory flavors from tangy tamarind, fish sauce, and a touch of sugar. Chewy rice noodles are stir-fried quickly with shrimp and scrambled egg, then finished with crushed peanuts and a squeeze of lime for extra brightness. The whole dish comes together fast over high heat, capturing that irresistible smoky flavor found at Bangkok's busiest food stalls.

Ingredients

  • 200g flat rice noodles
  • 200g shrimp, peeled
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1/4 cup crushed peanuts
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • bean sprouts, for garnish

Why You'll Love It: Sweet, sour, and savory noodles that taste like street food magic.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak rice noodles in warm water until softened; drain.
  2. Heat oil in a wok and stir-fry shrimp until pink; push to one side.
  3. Crack eggs into the wok and scramble briefly.
  4. Add drained noodles, tamarind paste, fish sauce, and sugar, tossing everything together over high heat.
  5. Serve topped with crushed peanuts, bean sprouts, and a lime wedge.
