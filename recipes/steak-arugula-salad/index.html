Steak Arugula Salad

Medium

Steak arugula salad is peppery, savory, and elegant. Sliced steak rests over arugula with tomatoes, parmesan, and balsamic for a simple bistro-style plate.

Ingredients

  • 150 g steak
  • 3 cups arugula
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • parmesan shavings
  • 2 tbsp balsamic dressing
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Sliced steak makes peppery arugula feel bistro-worthy.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Season and cook steak to preference.
  2. Rest and slice thinly.
  3. Arrange arugula and tomatoes on a plate.
  4. Top with steak and parmesan.
  5. Drizzle with balsamic dressing.
