Beet Lentil Feta Bowl

Easy

Beet lentil feta bowl is earthy, tangy, and colorful. Roasted beets, lentils, greens, feta, walnuts, and balsamic dressing create a hearty vegetarian bowl.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup lentils
  • 2 roasted beets
  • 2 cups greens
  • 1/3 cup feta
  • 2 tbsp walnuts
  • 2 tbsp balsamic dressing
  • parsley

Why You'll Love It: Beets and lentils make a colorful bowl with satisfying depth.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash and dry the greens or vegetables thoroughly, then slice the fresh toppings and measure the dressing ingredients.
  2. If the recipe includes grains, meat, tofu, seafood, or potatoes, cook them from raw and let them cool slightly; otherwise keep the salad components crisp and fresh.
  3. Drain and pat dry the tofu, beans, or legumes, then season and warm them with aromatics until flavorful.
  4. Arrange the washed greens, toppings, and dressing for Beet Lentil Feta Bowl, keeping crisp items separate until serving if needed.
  5. Toss just before serving, then adjust salt, acidity, herbs, or crunchy toppings to taste.
