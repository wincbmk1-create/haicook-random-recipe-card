Dan Dan Noodles

Medium

Originating from Sichuan cuisine, these noodles are tossed in a bold sauce built from nutty sesame paste, fiery chili oil, and savory minced pork, creating layers of heat and richness in every bite. The numbing spice from Sichuan peppercorns gives it a distinctive tingling kick that keeps you coming back for more. It's an intensely flavorful dish for anyone who loves noodles with a serious kick.

Ingredients

  • 250g wheat noodles
  • 150g ground pork
  • 2 tbsp sesame paste
  • 2 tbsp chili oil
  • 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns, ground
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 scallions, chopped

Why You'll Love It: Spicy, nutty noodles that pack serious flavor in every bite.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook noodles in boiling water according to package instructions; drain.
  2. Stir-fry ground pork in a hot pan until browned and crispy.
  3. Whisk together sesame paste, chili oil, soy sauce, and ground Sichuan peppercorns in each serving bowl.
  4. Add the cooked noodles to the bowls and toss with the sauce.
  5. Top with the crispy pork and chopped scallions before serving.
