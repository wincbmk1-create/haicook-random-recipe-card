Dan Dan Noodles

Medium

Originating from Sichuan cuisine, these noodles are tossed in a bold sauce built from nutty sesame paste, fiery chili oil, and savory minced pork, creating layers of heat and richness in every bite. The numbing spice from Sichuan peppercorns gives it a distinctive tingling kick that keeps you coming back for more. It's an intensely flavorful dish for anyone who loves noodles with a serious kick.

Ingredients

  • 250g wheat dry noodles
  • 150g ground pork
  • 2 tbsp sesame paste
  • 2 tbsp chili oil
  • 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns, ground
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 scallions, chopped

Why You'll Love It: Spicy, nutty noodles that pack serious flavor in every bite.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Dan Dan Noodles, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
