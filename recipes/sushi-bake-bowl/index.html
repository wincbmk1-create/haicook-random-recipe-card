Sushi Bake Bowl

Easy

This bowl captures the creamy, savory comfort of sushi bake without needing to assemble a casserole. Warm sushi rice is topped with seasoned salmon, avocado, cucumber, nori, sesame seeds, and a spicy mayo drizzle. It is playful, filling, and easy to customize.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked sushi rice
  • 250g cooked salmon, flaked
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp sriracha
  • nori strips
  • sesame seeds
  • 2 scallions, sliced

Why You'll Love It: All the cozy flavors of sushi bake in an easy rice bowl.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Season warm sushi rice with a little rice vinegar and salt.
  2. Mix flaked salmon with mayonnaise and sriracha.
  3. Add rice to bowls and top with salmon, avocado, and cucumber.
  4. Scatter nori strips, sesame seeds, and scallions on top.
  5. Drizzle with extra spicy mayo before serving.
