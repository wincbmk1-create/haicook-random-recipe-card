Sweet Potato Tacos

Easy

Roasted sweet potato cubes bring caramelized sweetness to these bright, satisfying tacos. Black beans add substance, avocado adds creaminess, and lime crema ties everything together. They are colorful, flexible, and easy to make vegetarian without feeling light.

Ingredients

  • 2 sweet potatoes, diced
  • 8 small corn tortillas
  • 1 can black beans, drained
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1/4 cup lime crema or yogurt
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • fresh cilantro
  • lime wedges

Why You'll Love It: Roasted sweet potatoes make these tacos hearty and naturally sweet.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Toss sweet potatoes with oil, smoked paprika, and salt.
  2. Roast at 220C (425F) for 20-25 minutes until tender and browned.
  3. Warm tortillas in a dry skillet.
  4. Fill each tortilla with black beans, sweet potatoes, and avocado.
  5. Drizzle with lime crema and finish with cilantro and lime.
