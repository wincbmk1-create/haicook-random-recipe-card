Falafel Salad Bowl

Medium

Crispy falafel turns a simple salad into a generous, satisfying bowl. Hummus, cucumber, tomato, pickled onion, herbs, and tahini bring creaminess, crunch, and brightness in every bite. It is fresh, filling, and easy to customize with whatever greens you have.

Ingredients

  • 6 falafel balls
  • 2 cups romaine, chopped
  • 1/2 cup hummus
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1/4 cup pickled red onion
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: Crispy falafel and creamy hummus make salad feel like a full meal.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Spread hummus along one side of a shallow bowl.
  2. Add romaine, cucumber, tomato, and pickled onion.
  3. Place warm falafel on top.
  4. Drizzle with tahini.
  5. Finish with parsley and serve immediately.
