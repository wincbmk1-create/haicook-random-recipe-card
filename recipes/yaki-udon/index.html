Yaki Udon

Easy

Yaki udon is a quick stir-fry built around thick, chewy udon noodles. Chicken, cabbage, carrots, mushrooms, scallions, and a glossy soy-based sauce make it hearty, colorful, and easy enough for a weeknight.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions dry udon noodles
  • 250g chicken strips
  • 2 cups cabbage, sliced
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp mirin

Why You'll Love It: Thick udon noodles make a fast stir-fry extra satisfying.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Yaki Udon, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
