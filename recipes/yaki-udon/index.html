Yaki Udon

Easy

Yaki udon is a quick stir-fry built around thick, chewy udon noodles. Chicken, cabbage, carrots, mushrooms, scallions, and a glossy soy-based sauce make it hearty, colorful, and easy enough for a weeknight.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions udon noodles
  • 250g chicken strips
  • 2 cups cabbage, sliced
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp mirin

Why You'll Love It: Thick udon noodles make a fast stir-fry extra satisfying.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Loosen udon noodles according to package directions.
  2. Stir-fry chicken until cooked through.
  3. Add cabbage, carrot, and mushrooms and cook until tender-crisp.
  4. Add udon, soy sauce, oyster sauce, and mirin.
  5. Toss until glossy and finish with scallions.
