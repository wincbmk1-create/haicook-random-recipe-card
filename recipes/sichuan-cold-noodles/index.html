Sichuan Cold Noodles

Easy

Sichuan cold noodles are cool, spicy, and aromatic. Noodles are tossed with chili oil, sesame, soy, vinegar, and cucumber for a refreshing dish with a satisfying tingle.

Ingredients

  • 200 g wheat dry noodles
  • 1 tbsp chili oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sesame paste
  • 1/2 cucumber, julienned
  • crushed peanuts

Why You'll Love It: Chili oil and vinegar give cold noodles a bold kick.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Sichuan Cold Noodles, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
