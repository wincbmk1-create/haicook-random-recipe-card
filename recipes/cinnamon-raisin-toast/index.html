Cinnamon Raisin Toast

Easy

Cinnamon raisin toast turns a simple slice of bread into a cozy breakfast. Toasted raisin bread gets a melting layer of cinnamon butter and honey, creating warm spice, sweetness, and crisp edges with almost no effort.

Ingredients

  • 4 slices raisin bread
  • 3 tbsp butter, softened
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • pinch of salt

Why You'll Love It: A warm, buttery breakfast with bakery-style comfort.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the fillings from raw ingredients: wash and slice vegetables, season any meat or seafood, and mix spreads or sauces from their listed components.
  2. Cook raw proteins or vegetables until tender, then toast or warm the bread so it can hold the filling without turning soggy.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Layer the cooked fillings, fresh toppings, and sauce into the bread for Cinnamon Raisin Toast while balancing moisture and crunch.
  5. Serve while the bread is warm or crisp, adding delicate herbs, greens, or pickles at the very end.
