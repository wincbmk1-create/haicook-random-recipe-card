Waldorf Chicken Salad

Easy

Waldorf chicken salad combines tender chicken with crisp apples, celery, grapes, walnuts, and a light creamy dressing. The fruit keeps it fresh while the walnuts add rich crunch. Serve it in lettuce cups, over greens, or as a make-ahead lunch.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chicken, diced
  • 1 apple, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 cup grapes, halved
  • 1/3 cup walnuts
  • 1/3 cup Greek yogurt or mayo
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • lettuce leaves

Why You'll Love It: Fruit, walnuts, and chicken make a creamy salad with real crunch.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Combine chicken, apple, celery, grapes, and walnuts.
  2. Mix yogurt or mayo with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Fold the dressing into the chicken mixture.
  4. Chill briefly so the flavors settle.
  5. Serve in lettuce cups or over greens.
