Harira Soup

Easy

Harira is a Moroccan tomato soup filled with chickpeas, lentils, vegetables, and warm spices. Cumin and cinnamon give the broth depth while cilantro and lemon keep it fresh. It is hearty without being heavy, making it a nourishing bowl for any season.

Ingredients

  • 1 can chickpeas
  • 1/2 cup lentils
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 celery stalk, diced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • fresh cilantro
  • lemon wedges
  • 4 cups water or broth

Why You'll Love It: Chickpeas, lentils, and warm spices make it deeply satisfying.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and chop the vegetables, then cut any raw meat, seafood, tofu, or aromatics into even pieces before heating the pot.
  2. Saute the aromatics in a little fat until fragrant, then add spices or tomato paste so the soup base develops flavor before liquid goes in.
  3. Drain and pat dry the tofu, beans, or legumes, then season and warm them with aromatics until flavorful.
  4. Stir the Harira Soup gently as it finishes so the ingredients stay evenly distributed and the broth tastes balanced.
  5. Taste the soup, adjust salt and brightness, then ladle into bowls with fresh herbs or toppings.
