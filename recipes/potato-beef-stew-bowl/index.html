Potato Beef Stew Bowl

Medium

Potato beef stew bowl is hearty, rustic, and warming. Potatoes, beef, carrots, and herbs simmer in savory broth until everything is tender and deeply flavored.

Ingredients

  • 300 g beef cubes
  • 3 potatoes, cubed
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 onion
  • 4 cups broth
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • thyme

Why You'll Love It: Potatoes make beef stew extra filling and comforting.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Potato Beef Stew Bowl, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
