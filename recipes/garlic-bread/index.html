Garlic Bread

Easy

Slices of baguette are brushed with a fragrant mix of melted butter, minced garlic, and fresh parsley, then toasted until the edges turn deeply golden and crisp. The garlicky aroma alone is enough to make everyone gather in the kitchen before dinner is even served. It's the classic finishing touch to any pasta night, perfect for mopping up extra sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 baguette, halved lengthwise
  • 4 tbsp butter, softened
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Why You'll Love It: The perfect crispy side for any pasta night.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the fillings from raw ingredients: wash and slice vegetables, season any meat or seafood, and mix spreads or sauces from their listed components.
  2. Cook raw proteins or vegetables until tender, then toast or warm the bread so it can hold the filling without turning soggy.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Layer the cooked fillings, fresh toppings, and sauce into the bread for Garlic Bread while balancing moisture and crunch.
  5. Serve while the bread is warm or crisp, adding delicate herbs, greens, or pickles at the very end.
