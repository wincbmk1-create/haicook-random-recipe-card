Clam Chowder

Medium

Tender clams, diced potatoes, celery, and onion simmer into a creamy chowder that feels both coastal and deeply comforting. Bacon adds a smoky backbone while the potatoes give the broth body without making it heavy. It is the kind of bowl that tastes best with crusty bread and a quiet evening.

Ingredients

  • 500g clams or clam meat
  • 2 potatoes, diced
  • 3 slices bacon, chopped
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 2 cups milk or cream
  • 2 cups seafood or chicken broth
  • fresh chives

Why You'll Love It: A cozy seafood soup with rich, savory depth.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and chop the vegetables, then cut any raw meat, seafood, tofu, or aromatics into even pieces before heating the pot.
  2. Saute the aromatics in a little fat until fragrant, then add spices or tomato paste so the soup base develops flavor before liquid goes in.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Stir the Clam Chowder gently as it finishes so the ingredients stay evenly distributed and the broth tastes balanced.
  5. Taste the soup, adjust salt and brightness, then ladle into bowls with fresh herbs or toppings.
