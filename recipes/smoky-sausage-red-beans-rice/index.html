Smoky Sausage Red Beans Rice

Easy

Smoky sausage red beans rice is hearty, savory, and built for comfort. Red beans simmer with sausage, celery, onion, and paprika, then get spooned over warm rice. It is budget-friendly, filling, and full of slow-cooked flavor even on a weeknight.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 can red beans, drained
  • 250g smoked sausage, sliced
  • 1 celery stalk, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 cup broth
  • 2 scallions, sliced

Why You'll Love It: Beans, sausage, and rice make a deeply satisfying one-bowl meal.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Brown sausage slices in a skillet.
  2. Add onion and celery; cook until softened.
  3. Stir in red beans, broth, and smoked paprika.
  4. Simmer until saucy and flavorful.
  5. Serve over rice with scallions.
