Lemongrass Chicken Vermicelli

Medium

Lemongrass chicken vermicelli is fresh, bright, and satisfying. Rice noodles are topped with grilled lemongrass chicken, lettuce, cucumber, carrots, herbs, peanuts, lime, and nuoc cham for a balanced bowl.

Ingredients

  • 200g rice dry vermicelli
  • 300g chicken thighs
  • 1 stalk lemongrass, minced
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce or soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 cucumber, julienned
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • lettuce and fresh herbs
  • crushed peanuts
  • nuoc cham sauce

Why You'll Love It: Grilled chicken and herbs make a light, colorful noodle bowl.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Lemongrass Chicken Vermicelli, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
