Hasselback Potatoes

Medium

Hasselback potatoes are sliced thin without cutting all the way through, then roasted with garlic herb butter until the edges fan out and turn crisp. Parmesan and parsley make them feel polished but approachable.

Ingredients

  • 6 medium potatoes
  • 4 tbsp butter, melted
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/3 cup parmesan
  • fresh parsley
  • salt and black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Thin slices create crisp ridges while the inside stays tender.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Slice potatoes thinly without cutting through the base.
  2. Brush with garlic butter and season well.
  3. Roast at 210C (410F) until tender and fanned open.
  4. Brush with more butter and sprinkle with parmesan.
  5. Bake until crisp and finish with parsley.
