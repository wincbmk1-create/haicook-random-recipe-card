Split Pea Soup

Easy

Split peas simmer down into a thick, velvety soup with carrots, celery, onion, and a smoky hint of ham. The peas naturally thicken the broth, so the finished bowl feels hearty with very little fuss. A few croutons or fresh herbs on top add just the right contrast.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups split peas
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 celery stalk, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 150g ham, optional
  • 5 cups broth
  • 1 bay leaf
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: A budget-friendly classic that turns simple pantry staples into comfort.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse split peas and add them to a pot with broth and bay leaf.
  2. Add carrot, celery, onion, and ham if using.
  3. Simmer for 45-60 minutes until the peas break down.
  4. Stir often near the end so the soup does not stick.
  5. Remove the bay leaf, season to taste, and finish with parsley.
