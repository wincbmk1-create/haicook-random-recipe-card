Cioppino Seafood Stew

Medium

Cioppino is a tomato-rich seafood stew loaded with mussels, shrimp, and tender white fish. Garlic, fennel, and herbs build a fragrant base before the seafood cooks quickly in the simmering broth. It feels special enough for guests but stays relaxed and rustic at the table.

Ingredients

  • 300g mussels
  • 250g shrimp
  • 250g white fish
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 3 cups seafood broth
  • 1 fennel bulb or onion, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: A dramatic seafood stew that still cooks surprisingly fast.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute fennel or onion with garlic until fragrant.
  2. Add crushed tomatoes and seafood broth, then simmer for 15 minutes.
  3. Add white fish and shrimp and cook until almost done.
  4. Add mussels, cover, and cook until they open.
  5. Discard unopened mussels and finish with parsley.
