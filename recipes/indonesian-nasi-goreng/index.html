Indonesian Nasi Goreng

Easy

Nasi goreng gets its signature flavor from sweet soy sauce, aromatics, and a fried egg on top. The rice turns deeply savory and slightly caramelized, while cucumber, tomato, and crispy shallots add freshness and crunch. It is simple but packed with personality.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups water or broth
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp kecap manis or sweet soy sauce
  • 1 shallot, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 red chili, sliced
  • cucumber and tomato, to serve
  • crispy shallots

Why You'll Love It: Dark soy glaze and crispy shallots make fried rice deeply savory.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Indonesian Nasi Goreng, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
