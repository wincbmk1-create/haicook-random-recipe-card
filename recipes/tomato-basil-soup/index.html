Tomato Basil Soup

Easy

Ripe tomatoes are simmered down and blended until velvety smooth, then finished with fresh basil and a touch of cream for extra richness and a gentle sweetness. The bright, tangy tomato flavor shines through even with the creamy texture. It's the quintessential cozy soup, best enjoyed with a crispy grilled cheese sandwich for dipping.

Ingredients

  • 1kg ripe tomatoes, chopped (or 2 cans whole tomatoes)
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • handful of fresh basil
  • 2 cups vegetable broth

Why You'll Love It: Pairs perfectly with a grilled cheese sandwich.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Sauté onion and garlic in a pot until softened.
  2. Add tomatoes and vegetable broth, then simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Blend the soup until smooth using an immersion blender.
  4. Stir in the cream and fresh basil, and simmer for another 5 minutes.
  5. Season to taste and serve hot with grilled cheese on the side.
