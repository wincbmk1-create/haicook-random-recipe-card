Korean Jjajangmyeon

Medium

Korean jjajangmyeon is glossy, hearty, and deeply savory. Chewy noodles are topped with black bean sauce cooked with pork, onion, and vegetables, then finished with crisp cucumber.

Ingredients

  • 200 g wheat noodles
  • 3 tbsp chunjang black bean paste
  • 100 g pork, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1/2 zucchini, diced
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • cucumber strips

Why You'll Love It: Black bean sauce gives noodles a deep savory comfort.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Stir-fry pork until browned.
  2. Add onion and zucchini and cook until softened.
  3. Fry black bean paste briefly with sugar.
  4. Add water and simmer into a sauce.
  5. Serve over noodles with cucumber.
