Salmon Corn Chowder

Medium

Salmon corn chowder is creamy, sweet, and coastal. Tender salmon, corn, potatoes, and dill simmer in a soft cream broth for a satisfying bowl.

Ingredients

  • 200 g salmon pieces
  • 2 cups corn
  • 2 potatoes, diced
  • 3 cups broth
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • dill
  • celery

Why You'll Love It: Salmon and corn make chowder rich but still fresh.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and chop the vegetables, then cut any raw meat, seafood, tofu, or aromatics into even pieces before heating the pot.
  2. Saute the aromatics in a little fat until fragrant, then add spices or tomato paste so the soup base develops flavor before liquid goes in.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Stir the Salmon Corn Chowder gently as it finishes so the ingredients stay evenly distributed and the broth tastes balanced.
  5. Taste the soup, adjust salt and brightness, then ladle into bowls with fresh herbs or toppings.
