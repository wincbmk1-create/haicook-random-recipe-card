Creamy Mushroom Tagliatelle

Easy

Wide ribbons of tagliatelle are tossed in a creamy mushroom sauce that clings to every strand. Sauteed mushrooms bring earthy depth, parmesan adds savory richness, and parsley keeps the dish bright. It feels elegant but comes together with weeknight-friendly ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 350g dry tagliatelle
  • 300g mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup cream
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • fresh parsley, chopped
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: Silky pasta with deep mushroom flavor and a cozy bistro feel.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Creamy Mushroom Tagliatelle, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
