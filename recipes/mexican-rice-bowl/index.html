Mexican Rice Bowl

Easy

This build-your-own style bowl layers fluffy rice with hearty black beans, sweet charred corn, and creamy avocado, all brightened up with a generous squeeze of fresh lime juice. It's naturally vegetarian, endlessly customizable with your favorite toppings like salsa or cheese, and comes together mostly from pantry staples. The combination of textures and bold Tex-Mex flavors makes it a go-to for quick, colorful weeknight dinners.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups water or broth
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/4 onion, diced
  • fresh cilantro
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheese

Why You'll Love It: A vibrant bowl loaded with color and bold Tex-Mex flavor.

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Drain and pat dry the tofu, beans, or legumes, then season and warm them with aromatics until flavorful.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Mexican Rice Bowl, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
