Grilled Chicken Avocado Salad

Easy

Grilled chicken avocado salad is fresh, colorful, and satisfying. Juicy chicken, creamy avocado, crisp greens, tomatoes, and feta come together with a bright vinaigrette.

Ingredients

  • 1 raw chicken breast or thigh
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 4 cups mixed greens
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/3 cup feta
  • 2 tbsp vinaigrette

Why You'll Love It: Grilled chicken and avocado make salad feel like a full meal.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash and dry the greens or vegetables thoroughly, then slice the fresh toppings and measure the dressing ingredients.
  2. If the recipe includes grains, meat, tofu, seafood, or potatoes, cook them from raw and let them cool slightly; otherwise keep the salad components crisp and fresh.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Arrange the washed greens, toppings, and dressing for Grilled Chicken Avocado Salad, keeping crisp items separate until serving if needed.
  5. Toss just before serving, then adjust salt, acidity, herbs, or crunchy toppings to taste.
