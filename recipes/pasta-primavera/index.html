Pasta Primavera

Easy

Pasta primavera keeps dinner colorful with tender-crisp vegetables, olive oil, parmesan, and bright herbs. Farfalle catches little pieces of zucchini, tomato, asparagus, and peas, creating a bowl that feels generous but still fresh.

Ingredients

  • 350g farfalle
  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 cup asparagus pieces
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • grated parmesan

Why You'll Love It: Crisp vegetables make pasta feel light, bright, and seasonal.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook farfalle until al dente.
  2. Saute zucchini and asparagus in olive oil until just tender.
  3. Add tomatoes and peas and cook briefly.
  4. Toss vegetables with pasta and a splash of pasta water.
  5. Serve with parmesan and black pepper.
