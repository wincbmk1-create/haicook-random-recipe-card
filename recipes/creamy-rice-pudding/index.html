Creamy Rice Pudding

Easy

Creamy rice pudding simmers rice slowly in milk until soft and spoonable. Cinnamon, vanilla, and raisins give it a familiar warmth, while the texture stays rich and comforting. It can be served warm or chilled depending on the mood.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup short-grain rice
  • 4 cups milk
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/3 cup raisins
  • pinch of salt

Why You'll Love It: A soft, cinnamon-scented bowl that feels calm and nostalgic.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Combine rice, milk, sugar, and salt in a saucepan.
  2. Simmer gently, stirring often, until the rice is tender and creamy.
  3. Stir in vanilla, cinnamon, and raisins.
  4. Cook a few minutes more until thickened.
  5. Serve warm or chill for later.
