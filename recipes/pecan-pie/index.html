Pecan Pie

Medium

Pecan pie combines a flaky crust with glossy pecans and a rich caramel-like filling. The nuts toast as the pie bakes, giving each slice deep flavor and a satisfying crunch. It is classic, generous, and especially good with whipped cream.

Ingredients

  • 1 pie crust
  • 1.5 cups pecans
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup corn syrup or golden syrup
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 4 tbsp butter, melted
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Why You'll Love It: Glossy pecans and caramel filling make a classic sweet slice.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Arrange pecans in the pie crust.
  2. Whisk eggs, syrup, brown sugar, melted butter, and vanilla.
  3. Pour the filling over the pecans.
  4. Bake at 175C (350F) until set around the edges with a slight wobble in the center.
  5. Cool completely before slicing.
