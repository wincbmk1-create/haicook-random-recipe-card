Shrimp Scampi Spaghetti

Easy

Shrimp scampi spaghetti is fast, fragrant, and full of lemony garlic butter flavor. The shrimp cook quickly while pasta water turns the pan juices into a glossy sauce. It is a classic weeknight seafood pasta that feels brighter than it is difficult.

Ingredients

  • 350g spaghetti
  • 350g shrimp, peeled
  • 4 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tbsp parsley
  • chili flakes, optional

Why You'll Love It: Garlic butter shrimp makes spaghetti quick, glossy, and lively.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook spaghetti until al dente; reserve pasta water.
  2. Saute garlic in butter and olive oil.
  3. Add shrimp and cook until pink.
  4. Toss spaghetti with shrimp, lemon juice, and pasta water.
  5. Finish with parsley and chili flakes if using.
