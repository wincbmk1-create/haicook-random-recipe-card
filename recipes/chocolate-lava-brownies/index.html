Chocolate Lava Brownies

Easy

Chocolate lava brownies are dense, fudgy, and glossy with a soft molten center. They deliver deep chocolate comfort in a simple square.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup dark chocolate
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/3 cup flour
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • pinch salt

Why You'll Love It: A molten chocolate center makes brownies feel extra indulgent.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Melt butter and chocolate together.
  2. Whisk eggs and sugar until glossy.
  3. Fold in chocolate, flour, cocoa, and salt.
  4. Bake until edges set but centers stay soft.
  5. Cool briefly before slicing.
