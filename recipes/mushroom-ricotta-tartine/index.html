Mushroom Ricotta Tartine

Easy

Mushroom ricotta tartine is earthy, creamy, and elegant. Garlicky mushrooms are piled over ricotta on crisp sourdough, with thyme adding a fragrant finish. It is a small cafe-style meal with plenty of depth.

Ingredients

  • 1 slice sourdough
  • 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • 1/3 cup ricotta
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Garlicky mushrooms and ricotta make a rich open-faced toast.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the fillings from raw ingredients: wash and slice vegetables, season any meat or seafood, and mix spreads or sauces from their listed components.
  2. Cook raw proteins or vegetables until tender, then toast or warm the bread so it can hold the filling without turning soggy.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Layer the cooked fillings, fresh toppings, and sauce into the bread for Mushroom Ricotta Tartine while balancing moisture and crunch.
  5. Serve while the bread is warm or crisp, adding delicate herbs, greens, or pickles at the very end.
