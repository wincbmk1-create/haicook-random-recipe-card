Crispy Fish Sandwich

Medium

Crispy fish sandwich is golden, crunchy, and bright. A crisp fish fillet sits in a soft bun with lettuce, slaw, and tartar sauce, giving every bite contrast. It is casual coastal comfort with a fresh finish.

Ingredients

  • 1 brioche bun
  • 1 crispy white fish fillet
  • 1/2 cup slaw
  • 1 lettuce leaf
  • 2 tbsp tartar sauce
  • lemon wedge

Why You'll Love It: Crunchy fish and cool slaw make a lively sandwich.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Toast the bun lightly.
  2. Cook fish fillet until crisp and hot.
  3. Spread tartar sauce on the bun.
  4. Layer lettuce, fish, and slaw.
  5. Serve with a lemon wedge.
