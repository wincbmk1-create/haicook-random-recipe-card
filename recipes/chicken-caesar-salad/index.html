Chicken Caesar Salad

Easy

Crisp romaine lettuce is tossed in a creamy, garlicky Caesar dressing, then topped with juicy grilled chicken, shaved parmesan, and crunchy croutons for contrast. Unlike lighter salads, the protein and satisfying crunch make this one hearty enough to keep you full well past lunchtime. It's a timeless classic that proves salads don't have to feel like a compromise.

Ingredients

  • 1 head romaine lettuce, chopped
  • 2 chicken breasts, grilled and sliced
  • 1/2 cup shaved parmesan
  • 1 cup croutons
  • 1/2 cup Caesar dressing

Why You'll Love It: A protein-packed salad that actually keeps you full.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Season and grill the chicken breasts until cooked through; let rest, then slice.
  2. Toss the chopped romaine with Caesar dressing in a large bowl.
  3. Add the croutons and toss again lightly.
  4. Top with the sliced grilled chicken.
  5. Finish with shaved parmesan before serving.
