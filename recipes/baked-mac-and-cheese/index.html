Baked Mac and Cheese

Easy

Tender macaroni gets folded into a velvety cheddar cheese sauce, then topped with a layer of buttery breadcrumbs and baked until golden and bubbling at the edges. The contrast between the crunchy top and the creamy interior is what makes this such a beloved comfort classic. It's a guaranteed crowd-pleaser at family dinners, potlucks, and everything in between.

Ingredients

  • 350g elbow macaroni
  • 3 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 cups milk
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs

Why You'll Love It: Bubbly, cheesy comfort food loved by kids and adults alike.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook macaroni in salted boiling water until al dente; drain.
  2. Melt butter in a saucepan, whisk in flour, and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Gradually whisk in milk and cook until thickened.
  4. Stir in cheddar cheese until melted, then fold in the cooked macaroni.
  5. Transfer to a baking dish, top with breadcrumbs, and bake at 190°C (375°F) for 20 minutes until golden.
