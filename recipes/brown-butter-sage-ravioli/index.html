Brown Butter Sage Ravioli

Easy

Brown butter sage ravioli is simple but deeply flavorful. Butter cooks until nutty, sage turns crisp, and ravioli gets coated in a glossy sauce. Parmesan and walnuts add a savory finish with just enough crunch.

Ingredients

  • 400g cheese ravioli
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 8 sage leaves
  • 1/3 cup parmesan
  • 2 tbsp toasted walnuts
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Brown butter and sage make ravioli taste rich with minimal effort.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook ravioli until tender and drain carefully.
  2. Brown butter in a skillet until fragrant.
  3. Add sage leaves and let them crisp.
  4. Toss ravioli gently in the brown butter.
  5. Top with parmesan, walnuts, and black pepper.
