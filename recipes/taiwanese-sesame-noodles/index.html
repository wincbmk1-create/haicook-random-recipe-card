Taiwanese Sesame Noodles

Easy

Taiwanese sesame noodles are cool, nutty, and refreshing. Springy noodles are coated in sesame paste, soy sauce, garlic, and vinegar, then topped with crisp cucumber for balance.

Ingredients

  • 200 g wheat dry noodles
  • 2 tbsp sesame paste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • 1 clove garlic, grated
  • 1/2 cucumber, julienned
  • chili oil

Why You'll Love It: A creamy sesame sauce makes chilled noodles deeply satisfying.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Taiwanese Sesame Noodles, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
