Taiwanese Sesame Noodles

Easy

Taiwanese sesame noodles are cool, nutty, and refreshing. Springy noodles are coated in sesame paste, soy sauce, garlic, and vinegar, then topped with crisp cucumber for balance.

Ingredients

  • 200 g wheat noodles
  • 2 tbsp sesame paste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • 1 clove garlic, grated
  • 1/2 cucumber, julienned
  • chili oil

Why You'll Love It: A creamy sesame sauce makes chilled noodles deeply satisfying.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook noodles and rinse under cool water.
  2. Whisk sesame paste, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and a splash of water.
  3. Toss noodles with the sesame sauce.
  4. Top with cucumber.
  5. Drizzle with chili oil if desired.
