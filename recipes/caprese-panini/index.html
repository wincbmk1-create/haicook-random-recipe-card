Caprese Panini

Easy

This caprese panini presses mozzarella, tomato, and basil pesto between crisp ciabatta until the cheese turns soft and melty. The outside gets golden while the inside stays fresh and bright, making it a quick cafe-style meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 ciabatta rolls
  • 200g fresh mozzarella, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 cup fresh basil
  • 2 tbsp grated parmesan
  • 2 tbsp nuts or seeds
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh basil leaves

Why You'll Love It: Classic caprese flavors become warm, crisp, and satisfying.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the fillings from raw ingredients: wash and slice vegetables, season any meat or seafood, and mix spreads or sauces from their listed components.
  2. Cook raw proteins or vegetables until tender, then toast or warm the bread so it can hold the filling without turning soggy.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Layer the cooked fillings, fresh toppings, and sauce into the bread for Caprese Panini while balancing moisture and crunch.
  5. Serve while the bread is warm or crisp, adding delicate herbs, greens, or pickles at the very end.
