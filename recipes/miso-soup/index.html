Miso Soup

Easy

A light dashi broth is gently whisked with savory miso paste, then simmered with soft cubes of tofu and delicate seaweed for a simple, nourishing bowl. It's meant to be made quickly and eaten right away, so the miso keeps its delicate flavor and doesn't turn bitter from overcooking. This humble, comforting soup is a staple of Japanese meals for good reason.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups dashi broth
  • 3 tbsp miso paste
  • 1/2 block tofu, cubed
  • 2 tbsp dried wakame seaweed
  • 1 scallion, sliced

Why You'll Love It: A quick, comforting soup that's ready in 10 minutes.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and chop the vegetables, then cut any raw meat, seafood, tofu, or aromatics into even pieces before heating the pot.
  2. Saute the aromatics in a little fat until fragrant, then add spices or tomato paste so the soup base develops flavor before liquid goes in.
  3. Drain and pat dry the tofu, beans, or legumes, then season and warm them with aromatics until flavorful.
  4. Stir the Miso Soup gently as it finishes so the ingredients stay evenly distributed and the broth tastes balanced.
  5. Taste the soup, adjust salt and brightness, then ladle into bowls with fresh herbs or toppings.
