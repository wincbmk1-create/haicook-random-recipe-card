Lemon Herb Chicken Rice

Easy

This bowl pairs fluffy rice with sliced lemon herb chicken and simple vegetables. The lemon brightens the whole dish, while parsley and olive oil bring a clean Mediterranean feel. It is the kind of dependable meal that works for lunch prep or a relaxed dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: Lemon and herbs keep this chicken rice bowl light but satisfying.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Season chicken with lemon zest, garlic, salt, pepper, and olive oil.
  2. Sear or grill until cooked through, then slice.
  3. Saute zucchini until lightly browned.
  4. Toss rice with lemon juice and parsley.
  5. Serve chicken and zucchini over the lemon herb rice.
