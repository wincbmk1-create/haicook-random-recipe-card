Lemon Chicken Orzo Soup

Easy

Lemon chicken orzo soup is bright, soothing, and light. Tender chicken, orzo, spinach, and lemon simmer in broth for a clean bowl with gentle comfort.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup shredded chicken
  • 1/2 cup orzo
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 cup spinach
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • parsley

Why You'll Love It: Lemon keeps chicken soup lively and comforting.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring broth to a simmer with carrot.
  2. Add orzo and cook until tender.
  3. Stir in chicken and spinach.
  4. Add lemon juice to taste.
  5. Finish with parsley.
