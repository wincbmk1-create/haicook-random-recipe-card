Thai Basil Chicken Rice

Easy

This rice bowl pairs fluffy jasmine rice with a quick stir-fry of chicken, garlic, chili, and plenty of Thai basil. The sauce is savory with a little sweetness, soaking into the rice without making it heavy. Add a fried egg if you want the full comforting finish.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked jasmine rice
  • 300g ground or diced chicken
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 chili, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 cup Thai basil leaves

Why You'll Love It: Fragrant basil and chili turn plain rice into a fast street-food bowl.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Stir-fry garlic and chili in a hot pan until fragrant.
  2. Add chicken and cook until browned.
  3. Season with soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar.
  4. Fold in basil just until wilted.
  5. Serve immediately over warm rice.
