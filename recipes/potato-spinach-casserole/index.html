Potato Spinach Casserole

Easy

Potato spinach casserole is creamy, cheesy, and comforting. Sliced potatoes bake with spinach, garlic, cream, and cheese until the top is browned and the center is tender.

Ingredients

  • 4 potatoes, sliced
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 1 cup cream
  • 1 cup cheese
  • 1 clove garlic
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Spinach and cheese make potatoes cozy with a fresh note.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Potato Spinach Casserole, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
