Cacio e Pepe

Medium

Cacio e pepe proves how elegant simple cooking can be. Pecorino, black pepper, and starchy pasta water transform into a glossy sauce that clings to spaghetti. The key is patience, heat control, and a generous hand with pepper.

Ingredients

  • 350g spaghetti
  • 1 cup finely grated pecorino
  • 2 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
  • salt
  • reserved pasta water

Why You'll Love It: Three core ingredients create a beautifully glossy Roman classic.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook spaghetti in lightly salted water until al dente.
  2. Toast black pepper in a dry skillet until fragrant.
  3. Add a splash of pasta water to the skillet.
  4. Toss pasta with pepper water, then add pecorino off the heat.
  5. Stir until glossy, adding pasta water a little at a time.
