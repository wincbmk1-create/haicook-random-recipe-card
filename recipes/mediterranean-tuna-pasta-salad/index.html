Mediterranean Tuna Pasta Salad

Easy

Mediterranean tuna pasta salad is bright, briny, and sturdy enough for lunches. Fusilli gets tossed with tuna, olives, tomatoes, cucumber, herbs, and lemony dressing. It is light but filling, with plenty of texture in every forkful.

Ingredients

  • 350g fusilli
  • 1 can tuna, drained
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1/3 cup olives
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • parsley

Why You'll Love It: A chilled tuna pasta salad that is colorful and meal-prep friendly.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook fusilli, rinse under cool water, and drain well.
  2. Whisk olive oil with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Toss pasta with tuna, tomatoes, cucumber, and olives.
  4. Add dressing and parsley.
  5. Chill before serving if time allows.
