French Onion Soup

Medium

Onions are cooked low and slow until they turn deeply caramelized and almost jammy, forming the rich, sweet backbone of this classic French soup. A slice of toasted bread and a generous layer of melted cheese are broiled right on top, creating a bubbly, golden crust you have to break through with your spoon. It's an indulgent, restaurant-worthy dish that's well worth the slower cooking time.

Ingredients

  • 4 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 6 cups beef broth
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 4 slices baguette, toasted
  • 1.5 cups grated gruyère cheese

Why You'll Love It: Caramelized onions and melted cheese in every spoonful.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and chop the vegetables, then cut any raw meat, seafood, tofu, or aromatics into even pieces before heating the pot.
  2. Saute the aromatics in a little fat until fragrant, then add spices or tomato paste so the soup base develops flavor before liquid goes in.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Stir the French Onion Soup gently as it finishes so the ingredients stay evenly distributed and the broth tastes balanced.
  5. Taste the soup, adjust salt and brightness, then ladle into bowls with fresh herbs or toppings.
