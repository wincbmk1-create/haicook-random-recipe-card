Tofu Peanut Slaw Bowl

Easy

Tofu peanut slaw bowl is crunchy, nutty, and colorful. Crisp tofu, cabbage, carrots, cucumber, cilantro, and peanut sauce make a satisfying plant-based bowl.

Ingredients

  • 200 g tofu cubes
  • 2 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1/2 cucumber
  • 3 tbsp peanut sauce
  • cilantro
  • lime

Why You'll Love It: Peanut sauce ties tofu and crisp slaw together beautifully.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash and dry the greens or vegetables thoroughly, then slice the fresh toppings and measure the dressing ingredients.
  2. If the recipe includes grains, meat, tofu, seafood, or potatoes, cook them from raw and let them cool slightly; otherwise keep the salad components crisp and fresh.
  3. Drain and pat dry the tofu, beans, or legumes, then season and warm them with aromatics until flavorful.
  4. Arrange the washed greens, toppings, and dressing for Tofu Peanut Slaw Bowl, keeping crisp items separate until serving if needed.
  5. Toss just before serving, then adjust salt, acidity, herbs, or crunchy toppings to taste.
