Breakfast Bagel Sandwich

Easy

Breakfast bagel sandwich stacks fluffy egg, crisp bacon, cheddar, and avocado inside a toasted bagel. It is filling, cheerful, and perfect when you want a brunch-style breakfast without a long prep list.

Ingredients

  • 2 sesame bagels
  • 3 eggs, scrambled
  • 4 slices bacon
  • 2 slices cheddar
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: A full breakfast tucked into one warm, satisfying bagel.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the fillings from raw ingredients: wash and slice vegetables, season any meat or seafood, and mix spreads or sauces from their listed components.
  2. Cook raw proteins or vegetables until tender, then toast or warm the bread so it can hold the filling without turning soggy.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Layer the cooked fillings, fresh toppings, and sauce into the bread for Breakfast Bagel Sandwich while balancing moisture and crunch.
  5. Serve while the bread is warm or crisp, adding delicate herbs, greens, or pickles at the very end.
