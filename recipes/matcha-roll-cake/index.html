Matcha Roll Cake

Medium

Matcha roll cake pairs a soft green tea sponge with a cloud of lightly sweetened cream. The spiral looks impressive when sliced, but the flavors stay gentle and balanced. A few berries on the side add freshness and color without distracting from the matcha.

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup cake flour
  • 1 tbsp matcha powder
  • 1 cup whipped cream
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar
  • berries, to serve

Why You'll Love It: A light sponge cake with a beautiful green tea swirl.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Whisk eggs and sugar until pale and airy.
  2. Fold in sifted cake flour and matcha powder gently.
  3. Spread batter on a lined sheet pan and bake at 180C (350F) for 10-12 minutes.
  4. Cool, then spread with lightly sweetened whipped cream.
  5. Roll carefully, chill, and slice before serving with berries.
