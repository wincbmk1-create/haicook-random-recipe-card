Creamy Tomato Tortellini Soup

Easy

Creamy tomato tortellini soup is cozy, tangy, and cheesy. Tomato broth is softened with cream, then cheese tortellini and basil make it feel generous.

Ingredients

  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 3 cups broth
  • 250 g cheese tortellini
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • basil
  • parmesan
  • garlic

Why You'll Love It: Cheese tortellini turns tomato soup into a full meal.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Simmer tomatoes, broth, and garlic.
  2. Add tortellini and cook until tender.
  3. Stir in cream.
  4. Season to taste.
  5. Finish with basil and parmesan.
