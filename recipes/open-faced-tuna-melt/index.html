Open-Faced Tuna Melt

Easy

Open-faced tuna melt is warm, cheesy, and familiar. Tuna salad is spooned onto toasted bread, topped with cheddar, and broiled until bubbling. Celery and pickles keep the filling bright and crunchy.

Ingredients

  • 1 slice bread
  • 1 can tuna, drained
  • 1 tbsp mayo
  • 1 tbsp diced celery
  • 1 tbsp chopped pickles
  • 1/3 cup cheddar
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Creamy tuna and melted cheddar make a cozy open sandwich.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Mix tuna with mayo, celery, pickles, and pepper.
  2. Toast the bread lightly.
  3. Spread tuna salad over the toast.
  4. Top with cheddar.
  5. Broil until melted and golden.
