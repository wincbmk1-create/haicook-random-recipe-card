Eggplant Rice Pilaf

Medium

Eggplant rice pilaf is soft, savory, and fragrant with cumin and herbs. The eggplant turns silky as it cooks, while tomato and broth season the rice from within. It is excellent with yogurt, cucumber salad, or toasted nuts.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups basmati rice
  • 1 eggplant, cubed
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2.5 cups broth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • mint or parsley

Why You'll Love It: Golden eggplant gives rice a rich, silky texture.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Eggplant Rice Pilaf, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
