Pavlova

Medium

Pavlova has a crisp meringue shell with a soft, marshmallowy center, topped with whipped cream and fresh fruit. Passion fruit adds tangy brightness against the sweet base. It looks dramatic but tastes light, making it a beautiful finish for a gathering.

Ingredients

  • 4 egg whites
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 1 cup whipped cream
  • 2 cups mixed berries
  • passion fruit pulp

Why You'll Love It: Crisp meringue, soft center, cream, and fruit feel celebratory.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Whip egg whites until foamy, then gradually add sugar.
  2. Beat until glossy and stiff, then fold in vinegar and cornstarch.
  3. Shape into a nest on parchment.
  4. Bake low and slow until crisp outside, then cool completely.
  5. Top with whipped cream, berries, and passion fruit.
