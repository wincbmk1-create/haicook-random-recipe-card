Southwest Black Bean Salad

Easy

Black beans, roasted corn, avocado, tomatoes, and romaine come together in a colorful salad with a cilantro lime dressing. Tortilla strips add crunch and make the bowl feel fun without weighing it down. It is quick, flexible, and hearty enough for lunch.

Ingredients

  • 1 can black beans, drained
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 2 cups romaine, chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 red onion, diced
  • cilantro lime dressing
  • tortilla strips

Why You'll Love It: Beans, corn, avocado, and lime make it filling and bright.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Combine romaine, black beans, corn, tomatoes, and red onion in a bowl.
  2. Add diced avocado just before serving.
  3. Drizzle with cilantro lime dressing.
  4. Toss gently so the avocado stays intact.
  5. Top with tortilla strips for crunch.
