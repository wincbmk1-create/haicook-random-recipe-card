Lemon Blueberry Pavlova

Medium

Lemon blueberry pavlova is crisp, creamy, and bright. A meringue shell holds whipped cream, lemon curd, blueberries, and lemon zest.

Ingredients

  • 1 meringue shell
  • 1 cup whipped cream
  • 1/2 cup lemon curd
  • 1 cup blueberries
  • lemon zest
  • mint

Why You'll Love It: Lemon curd and blueberries balance sweet meringue beautifully.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Place meringue shell on a plate.
  2. Spoon whipped cream into the center.
  3. Add lemon curd.
  4. Top with blueberries.
  5. Finish with lemon zest and mint.
