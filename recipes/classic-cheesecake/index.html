Classic Cheesecake

Medium

A buttery graham cracker crust supports a thick, velvety filling of sweetened cream cheese baked low and slow until perfectly set but still creamy. Baking it gently and letting it cool gradually helps prevent cracks on top, giving it that clean, bakery-style finish. It looks like an impressive showstopper, but it's actually far more approachable to make at home than most people expect.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 6 tbsp melted butter
  • 900g cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Why You'll Love It: A crowd-pleaser that's easier to make than it looks.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Mix graham cracker crumbs with melted butter and press into the base of a springform pan.
  2. Beat the cream cheese and sugar until smooth and creamy.
  3. Add the eggs one at a time, beating gently after each addition, then stir in vanilla.
  4. Pour the filling over the crust and bake at 160°C (325°F) for 55-60 minutes until the center is just set.
  5. Cool gradually in the oven with the door ajar, then chill for at least 4 hours before slicing.
