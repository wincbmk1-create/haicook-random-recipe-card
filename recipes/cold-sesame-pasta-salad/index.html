Cold Sesame Pasta Salad

Easy

Chilled noodles are coated in a nutty, savory-sweet sesame dressing and tossed with crisp cucumber and sharp scallions for a refreshing crunch in every bite. Because it's served cold, it's an ideal make-ahead dish that only gets better as the flavors mingle in the fridge. It's a favorite for hot summer days, packed lunches, and potlucks alike.

Ingredients

  • 350g dry spaghetti or soba noodles
  • 3 tbsp sesame paste (or peanut butter)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 cucumber, julienned
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds

Why You'll Love It: The perfect make-ahead lunch for hot summer days.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Cold Sesame Pasta Salad, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
