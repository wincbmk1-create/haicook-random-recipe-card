Cold Sesame Pasta Salad

Easy

Chilled noodles are coated in a nutty, savory-sweet sesame dressing and tossed with crisp cucumber and sharp scallions for a refreshing crunch in every bite. Because it's served cold, it's an ideal make-ahead dish that only gets better as the flavors mingle in the fridge. It's a favorite for hot summer days, packed lunches, and potlucks alike.

Ingredients

  • 350g spaghetti or soba noodles
  • 3 tbsp sesame paste (or peanut butter)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 cucumber, julienned
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds

Why You'll Love It: The perfect make-ahead lunch for hot summer days.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook noodles according to package instructions, then rinse under cold water and drain well.
  2. Whisk together sesame paste, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and sesame oil to make the dressing.
  3. Toss the cold noodles with the dressing until evenly coated.
  4. Add julienned cucumber and sliced scallions, tossing again to combine.
  5. Chill for at least 30 minutes and garnish with toasted sesame seeds before serving.
