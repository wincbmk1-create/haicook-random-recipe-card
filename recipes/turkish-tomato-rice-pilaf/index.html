Turkish Tomato Rice Pilaf

Easy

Turkish tomato rice pilaf is soft, buttery, and gently tangy. Rice is toasted in butter, simmered with tomato and broth, then rested until fluffy. It pairs beautifully with grilled vegetables, kebabs, beans, or a spoonful of yogurt.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups baldo or long-grain rice
  • 2 tomatoes, grated
  • 2.5 cups broth
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp tomato paste
  • parsley
  • salt to taste

Why You'll Love It: Tomato and butter make rice tender, glossy, and comforting.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Drain and pat dry the tofu, beans, or legumes, then season and warm them with aromatics until flavorful.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Turkish Tomato Rice Pilaf, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
