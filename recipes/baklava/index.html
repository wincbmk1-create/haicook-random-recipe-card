Baklava

Medium

Baklava layers buttered phyllo pastry with chopped nuts, then soaks everything in fragrant honey syrup. The top stays crisp and flaky while the center turns sticky and rich. It is sweet, celebratory, and perfect in small pieces with tea or coffee.

Ingredients

  • 1 pack phyllo pastry
  • 2 cups pistachios or walnuts, chopped
  • 1 cup butter, melted
  • 1 cup honey
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

Why You'll Love It: Crisp pastry layers soak up honey syrup and toasted nuts.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Layer phyllo sheets in a pan, brushing each with melted butter.
  2. Add chopped nuts between layers.
  3. Cut into diamonds before baking.
  4. Bake until deeply golden and crisp.
  5. Pour warm honey syrup over the hot pastry and let it soak.
