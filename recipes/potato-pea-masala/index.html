Potato Pea Masala

Easy

Potato pea masala is warmly spiced, colorful, and homey. Tender potatoes and peas simmer in a tomato-ginger masala with garam masala and cilantro.

Ingredients

  • 3 potatoes, cubed
  • 1 cup peas
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • cilantro

Why You'll Love It: Potatoes and peas soak up warm tomato masala beautifully.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute cumin and ginger until fragrant.
  2. Add tomato and cook into a sauce.
  3. Stir in potatoes, peas, and spices.
  4. Add a splash of water and simmer until tender.
  5. Finish with cilantro.
