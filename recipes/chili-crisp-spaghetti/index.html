Chili Crisp Spaghetti

Easy

Chili crisp spaghetti is a fast fusion bowl with heat, crunch, and savory depth. Garlic, scallions, soy, and chili crisp coat the noodles in a glossy sauce. It is bold, pantry-friendly, and especially good with a fried egg or greens.

Ingredients

  • 350g spaghetti
  • 2 tbsp chili crisp
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • sesame seeds

Why You'll Love It: Chili crisp gives spaghetti heat, crunch, and instant flavor.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook spaghetti until al dente; reserve pasta water.
  2. Warm garlic, chili crisp, soy sauce, sesame oil, and vinegar in a pan.
  3. Toss spaghetti with the sauce.
  4. Loosen with pasta water as needed.
  5. Finish with scallions and sesame seeds.
