Korean Japchae

Medium

Chewy, glass-like sweet potato noodles are stir-fried with a rainbow of colorful vegetables and tender beef, all glazed in a savory-sweet soy sauce dressing. Each ingredient is traditionally cooked separately before being combined, which keeps every element vibrant and distinct in flavor and texture. It's a festive, crowd-pleasing dish often served at Korean celebrations and family gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 200g sweet potato glass noodles
  • 150g beef, thinly sliced
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 cup spinach
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil

Why You'll Love It: Glossy noodles tossed with veggies for a festive, crowd-pleasing dish.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook glass noodles according to package instructions; drain and cut into shorter lengths.
  2. Sauté the beef separately until cooked, then set aside.
  3. Sauté the carrot, onion, and spinach separately until just tender.
  4. Combine noodles, beef, and vegetables in a large bowl with soy sauce, sugar, and sesame oil.
  5. Toss everything together well and serve warm or at room temperature.
