Persian Jeweled Rice

Medium

Persian jeweled rice is fragrant, colorful, and beautiful on the table. Long-grain basmati is tinted with saffron and scattered with pistachios, almonds, orange zest, and dried fruit. Each spoonful is aromatic, slightly sweet, and full of texture.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1/4 tsp saffron threads
  • 1/3 cup pistachios
  • 1/3 cup almonds
  • 1/2 cup dried cranberries or barberries
  • 1/3 cup golden raisins
  • 1 orange, zested
  • 2 tbsp butter

Why You'll Love It: Saffron rice with nuts and dried fruit feels bright and celebratory.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse basmati rice until the water runs mostly clear.
  2. Cook rice until fluffy, then gently fold in saffron-infused water.
  3. Toast nuts in butter until fragrant.
  4. Add dried fruit and orange zest to the nuts.
  5. Scatter the jeweled topping over the rice before serving.
