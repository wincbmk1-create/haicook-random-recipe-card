Smoked Salmon Bagel

Easy

Smoked salmon bagel brings together tangy cream cheese, silky salmon, capers, red onion, dill, and lemon on a toasted bagel. It feels special enough for brunch but takes only a few minutes to assemble.

Ingredients

  • 2 everything bagels
  • 4 tbsp cream cheese
  • 120g smoked salmon
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • fresh dill
  • lemon wedges

Why You'll Love It: A polished brunch classic with creamy, salty, and fresh flavors.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Toast the bagels until lightly crisp.
  2. Spread cream cheese generously on each half.
  3. Layer smoked salmon over the cream cheese.
  4. Top with capers, red onion, and dill.
  5. Serve with lemon wedges.
