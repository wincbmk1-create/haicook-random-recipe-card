Saffron Shrimp Rice

Medium

Saffron shrimp rice is golden, aromatic, and full of gentle seafood flavor. The rice simmers with tomato, broth, and saffron, then shrimp are added near the end so they stay tender. Parsley and lemon keep the finished bowl fresh.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups short-grain rice
  • 300g shrimp, peeled
  • 1 pinch saffron
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 3 cups seafood or chicken broth
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • parsley and lemon

Why You'll Love It: Saffron and shrimp make a golden rice dish that feels polished.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Bloom saffron in warm broth for 5 minutes.
  2. Saute garlic and tomato in olive oil.
  3. Add rice and stir to coat.
  4. Pour in saffron broth and simmer until rice is nearly tender.
  5. Add shrimp for the final few minutes and finish with parsley and lemon.
