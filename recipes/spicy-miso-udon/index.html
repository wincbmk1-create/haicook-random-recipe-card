Spicy Miso Udon

Medium

Spicy miso udon is creamy, rich, and warming. Thick udon noodles sit in a red miso broth with ground pork, corn, bok choy, scallions, chili oil, and a soft egg for a bold comfort bowl.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions dry udon noodles
  • 250g ground pork
  • 4 cups broth
  • 3 tbsp miso paste
  • 1 tbsp chili paste
  • 1 cup corn
  • 2 heads bok choy
  • 2 soft-boiled eggs
  • scallions

Why You'll Love It: Miso broth, thick noodles, and chili oil make a cozy bowl.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Spicy Miso Udon, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
