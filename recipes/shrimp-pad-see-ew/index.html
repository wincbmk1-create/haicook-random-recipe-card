Shrimp Pad See Ew

Medium

Shrimp pad see ew is smoky, glossy, and satisfying. Wide rice noodles are stir-fried with shrimp, egg, Chinese broccoli, and sweet soy sauce until the edges pick up a little char.

Ingredients

  • 250 g wide rice noodles
  • 8 shrimp
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup Chinese broccoli
  • 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar

Why You'll Love It: Wide noodles and soy sauce create a wok-charred favorite.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Stir-fry shrimp until just pink and remove.
  2. Scramble egg in the wok.
  3. Add broccoli and noodles.
  4. Toss with soy sauces and sugar.
  5. Return shrimp and stir-fry until glossy.
