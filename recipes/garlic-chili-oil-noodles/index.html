Garlic Chili Oil Noodles

Easy

These noodles are all about speed and flavor. Hot oil blooms garlic, chili flakes, and scallions into a fragrant sauce that coats each noodle with savory heat. A splash of soy sauce and vinegar balances the richness, making it a quick pantry meal with serious personality.

Ingredients

  • 250g wheat noodles
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp chili oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 tsp sugar

Why You'll Love It: A fast noodle bowl with big garlicky heat and glossy sauce.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook noodles according to package directions and drain.
  2. Place garlic, scallions, sesame seeds, and chili flakes or chili oil in a heatproof bowl.
  3. Heat neutral oil until shimmering and pour it over the aromatics.
  4. Stir in soy sauce, vinegar, and sugar.
  5. Toss with hot noodles until glossy and serve right away.
