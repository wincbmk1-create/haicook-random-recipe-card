Classic Spaghetti Bolognese

Easy

This is the pasta dish most of us grew up on — a rich, slow-simmered sauce of ground beef, garlic, and tomatoes ladled generously over al dente spaghetti. Letting the sauce simmer a little longer deepens the flavor and helps it cling perfectly to every strand of pasta. It's endlessly forgiving, easy to double for a crowd, and reheats beautifully, making it a reliable go-to for busy weeknights.

Ingredients

  • 350g spaghetti
  • 400g ground beef
  • 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • grated parmesan, to serve

Why You'll Love It: The ultimate weeknight classic that everyone in the family loves.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan and sauté onion and garlic until fragrant.
  2. Add ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it up as it cooks.
  3. Stir in tomato paste, then add crushed tomatoes and simmer for 25-30 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente; drain.
  5. Toss the pasta with the sauce and top with grated parmesan before serving.
