French Toast

Easy

Thick slices of bread are soaked in a cinnamon-spiced custard of eggs and milk, then pan-fried until the outside turns golden and slightly crisp while the inside stays soft and custardy. It's a wonderful way to use up slightly stale bread, transforming it into a warm, sweet weekend treat. Served with a drizzle of syrup, it's the kind of breakfast that gets requested again and again.

Ingredients

  • 4 thick slices bread (slightly stale)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp butter, for frying
  • maple syrup, to serve

Why You'll Love It: A weekend breakfast treat the whole family will ask for again.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Whisk together eggs, milk, and cinnamon in a shallow dish.
  2. Dip each bread slice into the mixture, soaking both sides briefly.
  3. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat.
  4. Fry the soaked bread for 2-3 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Serve warm with a drizzle of maple syrup.
