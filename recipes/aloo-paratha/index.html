Aloo Paratha

Medium

Aloo paratha wraps spiced mashed potatoes inside a golden pan-cooked flatbread. The filling is fragrant with cumin, chili, and cilantro, while yogurt and pickle on the side make each bite balanced and comforting.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mashed potatoes
  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 green chili, minced
  • 1/2 cup cilantro, chopped
  • yogurt and pickle, to serve
  • ghee or oil

Why You'll Love It: Spiced potato filling turns flatbread into a satisfying meal.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Aloo Paratha, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
