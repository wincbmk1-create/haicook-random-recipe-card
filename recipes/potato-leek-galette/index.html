Potato Leek Galette

Medium

Potato leek galette is rustic, golden, and fragrant. Thin potato slices and softened leeks are layered over pastry with thyme and cheese, then baked until crisp at the edges.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour or crumbs
  • 6 tbsp cold butter
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2-3 tbsp cold water
  • 2 potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 leek, sliced
  • 1/2 cup cheese
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 egg wash
  • salt

Why You'll Love It: Thin potatoes and leeks bake into a rustic savory tart.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Potato Leek Galette, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
