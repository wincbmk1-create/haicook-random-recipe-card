Potato Leek Galette

Medium

Potato leek galette is rustic, golden, and fragrant. Thin potato slices and softened leeks are layered over pastry with thyme and cheese, then baked until crisp at the edges.

Ingredients

  • 1 pie crust or pastry sheet
  • 2 potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 leek, sliced
  • 1/2 cup cheese
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 egg wash
  • salt

Why You'll Love It: Thin potatoes and leeks bake into a rustic savory tart.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute leeks until soft.
  2. Roll pastry onto a baking tray.
  3. Layer potatoes, leeks, thyme, and cheese.
  4. Fold pastry edges over the filling.
  5. Brush with egg wash and bake until golden.
