Teriyaki Salmon Rice Bowl

Easy

This bowl layers fluffy steamed rice with tender salmon brushed in a sweet-savory teriyaki glaze. Crisp broccoli, cucumber, and carrots add color and freshness, while sesame seeds and scallions bring a simple finishing crunch. It is satisfying enough for dinner but still clean and balanced for a weekday meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked white rice
  • 2 salmon fillets
  • 1/3 cup teriyaki sauce
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 scallions, sliced

Why You'll Love It: A glossy rice bowl that feels fresh, filling, and restaurant-worthy.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Steam or warm the rice and divide it between bowls.
  2. Sear salmon fillets in a lightly oiled pan until almost cooked through.
  3. Brush with teriyaki sauce and cook for 1-2 minutes until glossy.
  4. Steam broccoli until bright green and tender-crisp.
  5. Arrange salmon, vegetables, sesame seeds, and scallions over the rice before serving.
