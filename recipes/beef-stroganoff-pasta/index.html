Beef Stroganoff Pasta

Medium

Beef stroganoff pasta pairs tender beef strips and browned mushrooms with a tangy sour cream sauce. Egg noodles make it hearty and familiar, while parsley keeps the finish fresh. It is comfort food with a polished dinner-table feel.

Ingredients

  • 250 g dry pasta
  • 350g egg noodles
  • 300g beef strips
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: Tender beef and mushrooms make a rich, cozy pasta bowl.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Beef Stroganoff Pasta, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
