Beef Stroganoff Pasta

Medium

Beef stroganoff pasta pairs tender beef strips and browned mushrooms with a tangy sour cream sauce. Egg noodles make it hearty and familiar, while parsley keeps the finish fresh. It is comfort food with a polished dinner-table feel.

Ingredients

  • 350g egg noodles
  • 300g beef strips
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: Tender beef and mushrooms make a rich, cozy pasta bowl.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook egg noodles until tender and drain.
  2. Sear beef strips quickly and set aside.
  3. Saute onion and mushrooms until browned.
  4. Add broth and simmer, then stir in sour cream off the heat.
  5. Return beef to the pan and toss with noodles and parsley.
