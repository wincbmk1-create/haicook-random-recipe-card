Sweet Potato Chickpea Bowl

Easy

Sweet potato chickpea bowl is nourishing, bright, and satisfying. Roasted sweet potatoes and chickpeas sit over greens with tahini lemon sauce for a balanced meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 1 can chickpeas
  • 2 cups greens
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • paprika

Why You'll Love It: Roasted sweet potato and chickpeas make a colorful power bowl.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Drain and pat dry the tofu, beans, or legumes, then season and warm them with aromatics until flavorful.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Sweet Potato Chickpea Bowl, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
