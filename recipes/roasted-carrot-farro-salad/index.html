Roasted Carrot Farro Salad

Easy

Roasted carrot farro salad is earthy, chewy, and colorful. Nutty farro, roasted carrots, feta, herbs, and pistachios are tossed with lemon dressing.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked farro
  • 3 carrots, roasted
  • 1/3 cup feta
  • 2 tbsp pistachios
  • parsley
  • 2 tbsp lemon dressing
  • pepper

Why You'll Love It: Farro and roasted carrots make a hearty salad with texture.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Roast carrots until caramelized.
  2. Place cooked farro in a bowl.
  3. Add carrots, feta, herbs, and pistachios.
  4. Toss with lemon dressing.
  5. Season with pepper.
