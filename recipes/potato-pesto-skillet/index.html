Potato Pesto Skillet

Easy

Potato pesto skillet is herby, bright, and colorful. Crisp potatoes are tossed with pesto, cherry tomatoes, green beans, and parmesan for a fast side or light meal.

Ingredients

  • 3 potatoes, diced
  • 1/3 cup pesto
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 cup green beans
  • 2 tbsp parmesan
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Pesto turns crisp potatoes into a fresh skillet meal.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Parboil potatoes until just tender.
  2. Crisp potatoes in olive oil in a skillet.
  3. Add green beans and cook until tender-crisp.
  4. Toss with pesto and cherry tomatoes.
  5. Finish with parmesan.
