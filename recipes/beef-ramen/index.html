Beef Ramen

Medium

A deeply savory broth, simmered until rich and full-bodied, forms the soul of this hearty ramen bowl. Tender slices of beef, springy noodles, and a soft-boiled egg with a jammy yolk are layered on top, along with fresh scallions for a pop of color and bite. It's the kind of steaming, slurpable bowl that instantly warms you up on a cold or exhausting day.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions ramen noodles
  • 4 cups beef or pork broth
  • 200g thinly sliced beef
  • 2 soft-boiled eggs, halved
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce

Why You'll Love It: A steaming bowl that instantly feels like comfort in a bowl.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring the broth to a simmer and season with soy sauce.
  2. Sear the sliced beef quickly in a hot pan until just cooked.
  3. Cook the ramen noodles according to package instructions; drain.
  4. Divide noodles between bowls and ladle hot broth over the top.
  5. Top with seared beef, a halved soft-boiled egg, and sliced scallions before serving.
