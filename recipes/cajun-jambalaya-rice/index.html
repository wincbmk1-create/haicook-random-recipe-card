Cajun Jambalaya Rice

Medium

Jambalaya is a bold one-pot rice dish loaded with smoky sausage, chicken, shrimp, peppers, and Cajun seasoning. The rice cooks directly in the seasoned broth, soaking up layers of spice and savory flavor. It is hearty, colorful, and made for big appetites.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups long-grain rice
  • 200g smoked sausage, sliced
  • 250g chicken, diced
  • 200g shrimp, peeled
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 3 cups broth
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning

Why You'll Love It: A spicy one-pot rice dish packed with protein and color.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Cajun Jambalaya Rice, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
