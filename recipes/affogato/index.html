Affogato

Easy

Affogato is the simplest kind of dessert magic: hot espresso poured over cold vanilla gelato. The coffee melts the gelato into a creamy bittersweet sauce while chocolate shavings add a little texture. It takes minutes but feels wonderfully special.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 shots hot espresso
  • dark chocolate shavings
  • small pinch of sea salt, optional

Why You'll Love It: Hot espresso over cold gelato is effortless and dramatic.
