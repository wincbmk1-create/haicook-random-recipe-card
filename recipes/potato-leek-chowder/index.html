Potato Leek Chowder

Easy

Potato leek chowder is creamy, mild, and deeply comforting. Tender potatoes and softened leeks simmer in broth before cream gives the bowl a silky finish.

Ingredients

  • 3 potatoes, diced
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 3 cups broth
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • chives
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Potatoes and leeks make a velvety soup with gentle sweetness.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook leeks in butter until soft.
  2. Add potatoes and broth.
  3. Simmer until potatoes are tender.
  4. Blend part of the soup for body.
  5. Stir in cream and finish with chives.
