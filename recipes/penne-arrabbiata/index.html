Penne Arrabbiata

Easy

Penne arrabbiata is simple, fiery, and deeply satisfying. Garlic and chili flakes bloom in olive oil before tomato sauce simmers into a bold coating for ridged penne. Fresh basil and parmesan round out the heat with a clean bistro finish.

Ingredients

  • 350g penne
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh basil
  • grated parmesan

Why You'll Love It: A punchy tomato pasta with just enough heat to wake up dinner.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook penne in salted boiling water until al dente; reserve some pasta water.
  2. Saute garlic and chili flakes in olive oil until fragrant.
  3. Add tomato sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Toss penne with the sauce, adding pasta water as needed.
  5. Finish with basil and parmesan before serving.
