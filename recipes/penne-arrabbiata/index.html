Penne Arrabbiata

Easy

Penne arrabbiata is simple, fiery, and deeply satisfying. Garlic and chili flakes bloom in olive oil before tomato sauce simmers into a bold coating for ridged penne. Fresh basil and parmesan round out the heat with a clean bistro finish.

Ingredients

  • 350g dry penne
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh basil
  • grated parmesan

Why You'll Love It: A punchy tomato pasta with just enough heat to wake up dinner.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Penne Arrabbiata, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
