Chicken Shawarma Pita

Medium

Chicken shawarma pita is warm, spiced, and fresh at the same time. Juicy chicken strips are tucked into soft pita with cucumber, tomato, lettuce, pickled onion, and tahini sauce for a handheld meal full of texture.

Ingredients

  • 2 large pita breads
  • 300g chicken strips
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • lettuce leaves
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • 2 tbsp water

Why You'll Love It: Warm pita wraps savory chicken and crisp vegetables in every bite.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the fillings from raw ingredients: wash and slice vegetables, season any meat or seafood, and mix spreads or sauces from their listed components.
  2. Cook raw proteins or vegetables until tender, then toast or warm the bread so it can hold the filling without turning soggy.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Layer the cooked fillings, fresh toppings, and sauce into the bread for Chicken Shawarma Pita while balancing moisture and crunch.
  5. Serve while the bread is warm or crisp, adding delicate herbs, greens, or pickles at the very end.
