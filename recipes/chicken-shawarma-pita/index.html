Chicken Shawarma Pita

Medium

Chicken shawarma pita is warm, spiced, and fresh at the same time. Juicy chicken strips are tucked into soft pita with cucumber, tomato, lettuce, pickled onion, and tahini sauce for a handheld meal full of texture.

Ingredients

  • 2 large pita breads
  • 300g chicken strips
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • lettuce leaves
  • 2 tbsp tahini sauce

Why You'll Love It: Warm pita wraps savory chicken and crisp vegetables in every bite.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Season chicken with cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. Cook chicken in a hot skillet until browned and cooked through.
  3. Warm pita breads briefly.
  4. Fill pita with lettuce, chicken, cucumber, and tomato.
  5. Drizzle with tahini sauce before serving.
