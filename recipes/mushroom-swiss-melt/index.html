Mushroom Swiss Melt

Easy

This mushroom Swiss melt is rich, savory, and wonderfully crisp. Sauteed mushrooms and caramelized onions sit under melted Swiss cheese between toasted rye slices, creating a vegetarian sandwich that feels hearty and bistro-ready.

Ingredients

  • 4 slices rye bread
  • 250g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 4 slices Swiss cheese
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: Earthy mushrooms and melted cheese make a deeply comforting sandwich.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute mushrooms and onion in butter until browned.
  2. Spread Dijon on the bread slices.
  3. Layer mushrooms, onions, and Swiss cheese between bread.
  4. Toast in a skillet until both sides are golden.
  5. Slice and serve while the cheese is melted.
