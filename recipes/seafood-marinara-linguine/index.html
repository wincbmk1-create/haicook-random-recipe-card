Seafood Marinara Linguine

Medium

Seafood marinara linguine layers shrimp, mussels, and calamari into a bright tomato sauce with garlic and parsley. The pasta soaks up the briny sauce without feeling too rich, making it a polished dinner for seafood lovers.

Ingredients

  • 350g dry linguine
  • 250g shrimp
  • 200g mussels
  • 150g calamari rings
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: A generous seafood pasta that feels like a coastal bistro meal.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Seafood Marinara Linguine, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
