Seafood Marinara Linguine

Medium

Seafood marinara linguine layers shrimp, mussels, and calamari into a bright tomato sauce with garlic and parsley. The pasta soaks up the briny sauce without feeling too rich, making it a polished dinner for seafood lovers.

Ingredients

  • 350g linguine
  • 250g shrimp
  • 200g mussels
  • 150g calamari rings
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: A generous seafood pasta that feels like a coastal bistro meal.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook linguine until al dente and reserve pasta water.
  2. Saute garlic in olive oil until fragrant.
  3. Add marinara and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Add seafood and cook until shrimp are pink and mussels open.
  5. Toss with linguine and finish with parsley.
