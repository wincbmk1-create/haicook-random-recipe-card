Vanilla Panna Cotta

Easy

Vanilla panna cotta is a simple chilled cream dessert with a delicate wobble and a silky finish. Berry compote adds brightness and color against the gentle vanilla base. It is easy to prepare ahead and feels elegant without demanding much work.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cream
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 2 tsp gelatin
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup berry compote

Why You'll Love It: A creamy no-bake dessert that looks beautifully refined.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Bloom gelatin in a little cold milk.
  2. Warm cream, remaining milk, sugar, and vanilla until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Stir in bloomed gelatin until fully melted.
  4. Pour into molds or glasses and chill until set.
  5. Serve with berry compote on top.
