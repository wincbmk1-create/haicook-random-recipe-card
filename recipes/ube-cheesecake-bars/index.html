Ube Cheesecake Bars

Medium

Ube cheesecake bars are creamy, colorful, and lightly earthy. A graham crust supports a smooth ube cream cheese filling.

Ingredients

  • 1 graham crust
  • 450 g cream cheese
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup ube halaya
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Why You'll Love It: Ube gives cheesecake bars color and mellow sweetness.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare graham crust in a square pan.
  2. Beat cream cheese and sugar smooth.
  3. Mix in eggs, ube, and vanilla.
  4. Pour over crust.
  5. Bake until set and chill before slicing.
