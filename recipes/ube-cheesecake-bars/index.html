Ube Cheesecake Bars

Medium

Ube cheesecake bars are creamy, colorful, and lightly earthy. A graham crust supports a smooth ube cream cheese filling.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour or crumbs
  • 6 tbsp cold butter
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2-3 tbsp cold water
  • 450 g cream cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup ube halaya
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Why You'll Love It: Ube gives cheesecake bars color and mellow sweetness.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Measure the flour, sugar, dairy, eggs, fruit, or chocolate, then prepare the pan, mold, or serving cups before mixing.
  2. Make the batter, custard, dough, cream, or fruit filling from the listed ingredients, mixing only until the texture is smooth or just combined.
  3. Cook, bake, chill, steam, or set the mixture until the dessert reaches the proper texture, watching closely so it stays tender rather than dry.
  4. Shape, pour, layer, or portion the Ube Cheesecake Bars mixture carefully so the finished dessert sets evenly.
  5. Cool, chill, or rest the dessert as needed, then garnish and serve once the texture has set properly.
