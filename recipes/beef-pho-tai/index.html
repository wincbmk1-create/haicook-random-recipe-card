Beef Pho Tai

Medium

Beef pho tai is aromatic, clean, and deeply comforting. Rice noodles are topped with thin beef slices and hot spiced broth, then finished with herbs, onion, and lime.

Ingredients

  • 200 g rice noodles
  • 120 g thin beef slices
  • 3 cups pho broth
  • 1/4 onion, thinly sliced
  • bean sprouts
  • Thai basil
  • lime wedges

Why You'll Love It: Thin beef and fragrant broth make a clear, soothing bowl.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook rice noodles until tender.
  2. Place noodles and raw beef slices in bowls.
  3. Bring pho broth to a strong simmer.
  4. Pour hot broth over beef to cook it.
  5. Serve with sprouts, basil, and lime.
