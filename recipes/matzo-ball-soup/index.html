Matzo Ball Soup

Medium

Golden chicken broth holds tender matzo balls, sliced carrots, celery, and plenty of fresh dill. The broth stays clear and soothing while the matzo balls make the bowl feel generous. It is classic comfort food with a clean, gentle flavor.

Ingredients

  • 8 matzo balls
  • 6 cups chicken broth
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery stalks, sliced
  • 2 cups shredded chicken, optional
  • fresh dill
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Fluffy matzo balls turn clear broth into a beloved comfort meal.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring chicken broth to a simmer with carrots and celery.
  2. Cook until the vegetables are tender.
  3. Add prepared matzo balls and warm them through gently.
  4. Stir in shredded chicken if using.
  5. Finish with dill and black pepper before serving.
