Cajun Dirty Rice Skillet

Easy

Dirty rice is a deeply savory skillet dish with rice, smoky sausage, peppers, celery, onion, and Cajun seasoning. Everything cooks together until the rice absorbs the spice and pan juices. It is hearty enough as a main and excellent for leftovers.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 250g smoked sausage, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 celery stalk, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • scallions, to finish

Why You'll Love It: A bold skillet rice with smoky sausage and the classic Cajun trinity.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Brown sausage slices in a large skillet.
  2. Add onion, celery, and bell pepper; cook until softened.
  3. Stir in Cajun seasoning until fragrant.
  4. Fold in cooked rice and fry until hot.
  5. Finish with scallions before serving.
