Fudgy Brownies

Easy

Fudgy brownies are dense, glossy, and deeply chocolatey with the classic crackly top. A little salt sharpens the chocolate flavor and keeps each square from tasting flat. They are simple to make, easy to share, and excellent slightly warm.

Ingredients

  • 170g dark chocolate
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Why You'll Love It: Crackly tops and a dense center make them hard to resist.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Melt chocolate and butter together until smooth.
  2. Whisk in sugar, then eggs one at a time.
  3. Fold in flour, cocoa powder, and salt.
  4. Spread into a lined pan.
  5. Bake at 180C (350F) until the edges set but the center stays fudgy.
