Roasted Beet Goat Cheese Salad

Easy

Roasted beets bring earthy sweetness to peppery arugula, creamy goat cheese, toasted walnuts, and a glossy balsamic drizzle. Orange segments add brightness so the salad feels balanced instead of heavy. It is colorful, polished, and satisfying as a starter or light lunch.

Ingredients

  • 4 roasted beets, cut into wedges
  • 4 cups arugula
  • 1/2 cup goat cheese
  • 1/3 cup walnuts, toasted
  • 1 orange, segmented
  • 2 tbsp balsamic glaze
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Roasted beets and creamy goat cheese feel restaurant-ready.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Arrange arugula on a large plate or shallow bowl.
  2. Add roasted beet wedges and orange segments.
  3. Scatter goat cheese and walnuts over the top.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil and balsamic glaze.
  5. Season with salt and pepper before serving.
