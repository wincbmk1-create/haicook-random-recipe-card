Muffuletta Sandwich

Easy

Muffuletta sandwich is bold, briny, and built for sharing. Round bread is layered with salami, ham, provolone, and chopped olive salad, then pressed so the flavors settle together. It is rich, punchy, and satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 1 round sandwich roll
  • 3 slices salami
  • 3 slices ham
  • 2 slices provolone
  • 1/3 cup olive salad
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Olive salad and cured meats make a picnic-style classic.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Slice the roll in half.
  2. Brush the bread lightly with olive oil.
  3. Layer meats and provolone inside.
  4. Spoon olive salad over the filling.
  5. Press, slice, and serve.
