Miso Mushroom Udon

Easy

Miso mushroom udon is warm, earthy, and calming. Thick udon noodles sit in a savory miso broth with mushrooms, seaweed, and scallions. It is simple enough for a weeknight but layered with comfort.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions dry udon noodles
  • 2 tbsp miso paste
  • 2 cups dashi or broth
  • 1 cup mushrooms
  • 1 sheet nori
  • 2 scallions
  • 1 tsp sesame oil

Why You'll Love It: Miso and mushrooms create a deep, soothing noodle bowl.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Miso Mushroom Udon, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
