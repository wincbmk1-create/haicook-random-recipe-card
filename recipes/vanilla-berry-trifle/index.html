Vanilla Berry Trifle

Easy

Vanilla berry trifle is layered, creamy, and festive. Sponge cake, vanilla custard, berries, and whipped cream stack into a colorful dessert bowl.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sponge cake cubes
  • 1 cup vanilla custard
  • 2 cups mixed berries
  • 1 cup whipped cream
  • mint

Why You'll Love It: Custard, berries, and cream make a cheerful layered dessert.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Layer sponge cake in a glass dish.
  2. Spoon berries over the cake.
  3. Add vanilla custard.
  4. Top with whipped cream.
  5. Garnish with more berries and mint.
