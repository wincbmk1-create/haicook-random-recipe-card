Tuna Nicoise Salad

Medium

Tuna Nicoise is a composed French salad with seared tuna, green beans, baby potatoes, boiled eggs, tomatoes, olives, and crisp lettuce. Every element brings a different texture and flavor, so it feels complete without being heavy. A lemony vinaigrette keeps the whole plate bright.

Ingredients

  • 250g tuna steak
  • 2 boiled eggs, quartered
  • 1 cup green beans, blanched
  • 1 cup baby potatoes, cooked
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/3 cup olives
  • 4 cups lettuce
  • lemon vinaigrette

Why You'll Love It: Seared tuna, eggs, olives, and potatoes make it elegant and filling.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Season and sear the tuna, then slice it.
  2. Arrange lettuce on a large plate or bowl.
  3. Add green beans, potatoes, eggs, tomatoes, and olives.
  4. Place tuna slices on top.
  5. Drizzle with lemon vinaigrette before serving.
