Mini Potato Frittatas

Easy

Mini potato frittatas are savory, tender, and easy to share. Diced potatoes, spinach, eggs, cheese, and herbs bake in muffin cups for portable breakfast bites.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked diced potatoes
  • 5 eggs
  • 1 cup spinach
  • 1/2 cup cheese
  • 1 tbsp herbs
  • salt and pepper

Why You'll Love It: Mini frittatas turn potatoes into meal-prep friendly bites.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Grease a muffin tin.
  2. Divide potatoes and spinach among cups.
  3. Whisk eggs with cheese, herbs, salt, and pepper.
  4. Pour into cups.
  5. Bake until set and lightly golden.
