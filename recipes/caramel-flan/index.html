Caramel Flan

Medium

Caramel flan is smooth, glossy, and gently sweet. A vanilla custard bakes over caramel, then unmolds into a simple dessert with dramatic sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 can evaporated milk
  • 1 can condensed milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Why You'll Love It: Soft custard and caramel sauce make an elegant classic.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook sugar into amber caramel and pour into a mold.
  2. Blend eggs, milks, and vanilla.
  3. Pour custard over caramel.
  4. Bake in a water bath until set.
  5. Chill and unmold.
