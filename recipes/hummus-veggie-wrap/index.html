Hummus Veggie Wrap

Easy

This hummus veggie wrap is colorful, creamy, and easy to pack. Hummus anchors crisp cucumber, roasted peppers, carrots, spinach, and feta inside a soft tortilla, giving every bite a good balance of freshness and comfort.

Ingredients

  • 2 large tortillas
  • 1/2 cup hummus
  • 1 roasted red pepper, sliced
  • 1 cucumber, cut into sticks
  • 1 carrot, shredded
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 1/3 cup feta crumbles

Why You'll Love It: Creamy hummus and crunchy vegetables make lunch feel effortless.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Spread hummus over each tortilla.
  2. Layer spinach, roasted pepper, cucumber, carrot, and feta.
  3. Fold in the sides and roll tightly.
  4. Slice each wrap in half.
  5. Serve right away or chill for lunch.
