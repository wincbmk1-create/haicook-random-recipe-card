Banana Bread

Easy

Overripe, spotty bananas are mashed and folded into a simple batter along with crunchy walnuts, creating a loaf that's naturally sweet, moist, and full of flavor. It fills the whole kitchen with a warm, homey aroma while it bakes, making it feel like an instant act of comfort. Sliced and served plain or with a little butter, it's just as good for breakfast as it is for an afternoon snack.

Ingredients

  • 3 overripe bananas, mashed
  • 1 3/4 cups flour
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts

Why You'll Love It: The best way to use up overripe bananas.

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the fillings from raw ingredients: wash and slice vegetables, season any meat or seafood, and mix spreads or sauces from their listed components.
  2. Cook raw proteins or vegetables until tender, then toast or warm the bread so it can hold the filling without turning soggy.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Layer the cooked fillings, fresh toppings, and sauce into the bread for Banana Bread while balancing moisture and crunch.
  5. Serve while the bread is warm or crisp, adding delicate herbs, greens, or pickles at the very end.
