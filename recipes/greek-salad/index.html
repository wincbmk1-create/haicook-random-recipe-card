Greek Salad

Easy

Crisp cucumber, juicy tomatoes, and briny Kalamata olives are tossed together with thick slabs of tangy feta cheese and a generous drizzle of good olive oil. No lettuce needed here — the vegetables themselves are the star, kept simple so their fresh flavors truly shine through. It's a refreshing, no-cook salad that comes together in minutes and tastes even better with a squeeze of lemon.

Ingredients

  • 2 cucumbers, chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 200g feta cheese, sliced
  • 1/2 cup Kalamata olives
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano

Why You'll Love It: A crisp, no-cook salad ready in under 10 minutes.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Combine cucumber, tomato, red onion, and olives in a large bowl.
  2. Top with the sliced feta cheese.
  3. Drizzle generously with olive oil.
  4. Sprinkle with dried oregano.
  5. Toss gently just before serving to keep the feta intact.
