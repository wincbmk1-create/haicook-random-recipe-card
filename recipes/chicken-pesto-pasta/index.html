Chicken Pesto Pasta

Easy

Chicken pesto pasta is fresh, fragrant, and dependable. Fusilli catches the basil pesto in every curve, while grilled chicken adds protein and cherry tomatoes bring juicy sweetness. Toasted pine nuts and parmesan finish it with a gentle crunch.

Ingredients

  • 350g fusilli
  • 1 grilled chicken breast, sliced
  • 1/2 cup basil pesto
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp pine nuts
  • grated parmesan

Why You'll Love It: Bright basil pesto turns simple chicken pasta into something lively.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook fusilli until al dente and reserve a little pasta water.
  2. Toss hot pasta with pesto, loosening with pasta water if needed.
  3. Fold in sliced grilled chicken and cherry tomatoes.
  4. Toast pine nuts briefly in a dry pan.
  5. Serve with pine nuts and parmesan on top.
