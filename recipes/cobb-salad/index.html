Cobb Salad

Easy

Neat rows of hard-boiled egg, crispy bacon, creamy avocado, and tangy blue cheese are arranged over a bed of crisp lettuce, creating a salad that's as visually striking as it is filling. Each element brings its own distinct flavor and texture, so every forkful tastes a little different. It's hearty enough to serve as a complete meal rather than just a side.

Ingredients

  • 1 head lettuce, chopped
  • 3 eggs, hard-boiled and sliced
  • 4 strips bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup crumbled blue cheese
  • 2 tomatoes, diced

Why You'll Love It: A protein-loaded salad that doubles as a full meal.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Arrange the chopped lettuce as the base on a large platter.
  2. Arrange rows of sliced egg, crumbled bacon, diced tomato, sliced avocado, and blue cheese on top.
  3. Keep each ingredient in its own neat row for the classic Cobb presentation.
  4. Drizzle with your favorite vinaigrette.
  5. Toss just before serving, or serve as is for presentation.
