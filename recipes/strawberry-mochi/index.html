Strawberry Mochi

Medium

Strawberry mochi wraps juicy strawberries and sweet red bean paste in a soft, chewy rice flour dough. The contrast between tender mochi and fresh fruit makes each bite feel playful and delicate. It is a lovely chilled dessert for tea time.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup glutinous rice flour
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 8 strawberries
  • 1 cup red bean paste
  • potato starch for dusting

Why You'll Love It: Chewy mochi, fruit, and red bean make a delicate bite.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Mix rice flour, water, and sugar into a smooth batter.
  2. Steam or microwave until the dough turns translucent and sticky.
  3. Dust a surface generously with starch.
  4. Wrap each strawberry with red bean paste, then mochi dough.
  5. Pinch closed and chill briefly before serving.
