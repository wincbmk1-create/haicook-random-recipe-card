Turkey Cobb Power Bowl

Easy

Turkey Cobb power bowl is hearty, classic, and colorful. Sliced turkey, egg, tomatoes, avocado, greens, blue cheese, and vinaigrette make a satisfying lunch bowl.

Ingredients

  • 120 g sliced turkey
  • 3 cups greens
  • 1 boiled egg
  • 1/2 avocado
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup blue cheese
  • 2 tbsp vinaigrette

Why You'll Love It: Turkey gives Cobb salad a lean protein boost.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Arrange greens in a bowl.
  2. Add turkey, egg, avocado, and tomatoes.
  3. Sprinkle with blue cheese.
  4. Drizzle with vinaigrette.
  5. Season lightly and serve.
