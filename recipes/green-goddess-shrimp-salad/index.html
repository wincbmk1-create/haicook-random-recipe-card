Green Goddess Shrimp Salad

Easy

Green goddess shrimp salad layers grilled shrimp over butter lettuce, avocado, cucumber, snap peas, radish, and plenty of herbs. The creamy green dressing ties everything together with a fresh, garden-like flavor. It is light, bright, and still satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 300g shrimp, peeled
  • 4 cups butter lettuce
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 cup snap peas
  • 4 radishes, sliced
  • green goddess dressing
  • fresh dill and mint

Why You'll Love It: Grilled shrimp and herb dressing make a polished green bowl.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash and dry the greens or vegetables thoroughly, then slice the fresh toppings and measure the dressing ingredients.
  2. If the recipe includes grains, meat, tofu, seafood, or potatoes, cook them from raw and let them cool slightly; otherwise keep the salad components crisp and fresh.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Arrange the washed greens, toppings, and dressing for Green Goddess Shrimp Salad, keeping crisp items separate until serving if needed.
  5. Toss just before serving, then adjust salt, acidity, herbs, or crunchy toppings to taste.
