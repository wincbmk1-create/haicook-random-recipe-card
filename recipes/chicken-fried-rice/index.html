Chicken Fried Rice

Easy

This weeknight favorite starts with day-old rice, which fries up firmer and less sticky than fresh rice. Tender bites of chicken, scrambled egg, and sweet peas are tossed together in a hot wok with a splash of savory soy sauce, giving every spoonful a satisfying mix of textures. It's a fantastic way to use up leftovers while still feeling like a proper home-cooked meal, and it comes together in under 20 minutes flat.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked jasmine rice (day-old)
  • 1 boneless chicken breast, diced
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas and carrots
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 green onions, sliced

Why You'll Love It: One-pan magic that turns last night's leftover rice into a brand-new meal.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat.
  2. Add diced chicken and cook until golden and cooked through, about 5 minutes; remove and set aside.
  3. Push rice to one side, crack in eggs on the other side, and scramble until just set.
  4. Add garlic, peas and carrots, and cooked chicken; stir-fry everything together for 2 minutes.
  5. Pour in soy sauce, toss well to coat, and garnish with sliced green onions before serving.
