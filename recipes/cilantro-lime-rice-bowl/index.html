Cilantro Lime Rice Bowl

Easy

Cilantro lime rice forms a fresh base for grilled chicken, black beans, corn salsa, and avocado. The lime keeps everything lively, while the beans and chicken make the bowl satisfying. It is flexible enough for meal prep and colorful enough to feel exciting.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups water or broth
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1/2 cup cilantro, chopped
  • 1 raw chicken breast or thigh
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 cup black beans
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/4 onion, diced
  • fresh cilantro

Why You'll Love It: Cilantro lime rice turns simple toppings into a bright, balanced bowl.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Cilantro Lime Rice Bowl, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
