Cilantro Lime Rice Bowl

Easy

Cilantro lime rice forms a fresh base for grilled chicken, black beans, corn salsa, and avocado. The lime keeps everything lively, while the beans and chicken make the bowl satisfying. It is flexible enough for meal prep and colorful enough to feel exciting.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1/2 cup cilantro, chopped
  • 1 grilled chicken breast, sliced
  • 1 cup black beans
  • 1 cup corn salsa
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • salt to taste

Why You'll Love It: Cilantro lime rice turns simple toppings into a bright, balanced bowl.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Toss warm rice with lime juice, cilantro, and salt.
  2. Slice grilled chicken into strips.
  3. Warm black beans if desired.
  4. Arrange rice, chicken, beans, corn salsa, and avocado in a bowl.
  5. Finish with extra lime and cilantro.
