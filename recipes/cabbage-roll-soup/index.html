Cabbage Roll Soup

Easy

Cabbage roll soup is hearty, tomato-rich, and homey. Cabbage, ground beef, rice, and herbs simmer together for the comfort of cabbage rolls with less work.

Ingredients

  • 200 g ground beef
  • 3 cups chopped cabbage
  • 1 cup uncooked white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups water or broth
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 can tomatoes
  • 4 cups broth
  • 1 onion
  • dill

Why You'll Love It: All the flavor of cabbage rolls comes together in one pot.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and chop the vegetables, then cut any raw meat, seafood, tofu, or aromatics into even pieces before heating the pot.
  2. Saute the aromatics in a little fat until fragrant, then add spices or tomato paste so the soup base develops flavor before liquid goes in.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Stir the Cabbage Roll Soup gently as it finishes so the ingredients stay evenly distributed and the broth tastes balanced.
  5. Taste the soup, adjust salt and brightness, then ladle into bowls with fresh herbs or toppings.
