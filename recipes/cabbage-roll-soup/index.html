Cabbage Roll Soup

Easy

Cabbage roll soup is hearty, tomato-rich, and homey. Cabbage, ground beef, rice, and herbs simmer together for the comfort of cabbage rolls with less work.

Ingredients

  • 200 g ground beef
  • 3 cups chopped cabbage
  • 1/2 cup cooked rice
  • 1 can tomatoes
  • 4 cups broth
  • 1 onion
  • dill

Why You'll Love It: All the flavor of cabbage rolls comes together in one pot.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Brown ground beef with onion.
  2. Add cabbage, tomatoes, and broth.
  3. Simmer until cabbage is tender.
  4. Stir in cooked rice.
  5. Finish with dill.
