Crispy Rice Salmon Bowl

Medium

Crispy rice salmon bowl is crunchy, fresh, and colorful. Crisped rice pieces, salmon, avocado, cucumber, edamame, nori, and sesame dressing make a lively bowl.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups washed salad greens or vegetables
  • 1 cup uncooked white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups water or broth
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 120 g salmon
  • 1/2 avocado
  • 1/2 cucumber
  • 1/2 cup edamame
  • nori strips
  • sesame dressing

Why You'll Love It: Crispy rice adds a fun crunch to a salmon bowl.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash and dry the greens or vegetables thoroughly, then slice the fresh toppings and measure the dressing ingredients.
  2. If the recipe includes grains, meat, tofu, seafood, or potatoes, cook them from raw and let them cool slightly; otherwise keep the salad components crisp and fresh.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Arrange the washed greens, toppings, and dressing for Crispy Rice Salmon Bowl, keeping crisp items separate until serving if needed.
  5. Toss just before serving, then adjust salt, acidity, herbs, or crunchy toppings to taste.
