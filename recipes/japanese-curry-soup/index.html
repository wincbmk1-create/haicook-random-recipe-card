Japanese Curry Soup

Easy

Japanese curry soup is warm, savory, and gently sweet. Potatoes, carrots, and onions simmer in curry broth, then rice and scallions make it satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups curry broth
  • 1 potato, cubed
  • 1 carrot, sliced
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1/2 cup cooked rice
  • scallions
  • peas

Why You'll Love It: Japanese curry flavor turns soup into a cozy bowl meal.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Simmer onion, potato, and carrot in curry broth.
  2. Cook until vegetables are tender.
  3. Add peas and warm through.
  4. Spoon rice into bowls.
  5. Ladle soup over rice and top with scallions.
