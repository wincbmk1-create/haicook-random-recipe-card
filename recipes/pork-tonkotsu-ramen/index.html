Pork Tonkotsu Ramen

Medium

Pork tonkotsu ramen is rich, savory, and deeply comforting. Springy noodles sit in creamy pork broth with chashu, egg, scallions, and bamboo shoots for a classic ramen-style bowl.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions dry ramen noodles
  • 3 cups tonkotsu broth
  • 4 slices chashu pork
  • 1 soft-boiled egg
  • scallions
  • bamboo shoots
  • nori

Why You'll Love It: Creamy pork broth and chashu make a classic ramen bowl.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Pork Tonkotsu Ramen, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
