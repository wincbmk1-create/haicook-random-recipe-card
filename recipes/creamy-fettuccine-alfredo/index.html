Creamy Fettuccine Alfredo

Easy

With just a handful of ingredients — butter, cream, and freshly grated parmesan — this dish transforms simple fettuccine into something luxuriously silky. The sauce comes together in the time it takes the pasta to boil, making it one of the fastest routes to a truly indulgent dinner. It's rich enough to feel like a treat, yet simple enough to whip up on even the busiest weeknight.

Ingredients

  • 350g fettuccine
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup grated parmesan
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: Rich, silky pasta ready in under 20 minutes.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook fettuccine in salted boiling water until al dente; reserve 1/2 cup pasta water and drain.
  2. Melt butter in a pan and sauté garlic until fragrant.
  3. Pour in cream and simmer gently for 2-3 minutes.
  4. Stir in parmesan until melted and smooth, adding pasta water to loosen if needed.
  5. Toss in the drained fettuccine, season with salt and pepper, and serve immediately.
