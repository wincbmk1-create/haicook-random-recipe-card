Cilantro Coconut Lime Rice

Easy

Cilantro coconut lime rice is creamy, fragrant, and bright. Coconut milk gives the rice a soft richness, while lime and cilantro keep it lively. Serve it with grilled fish, beans, tofu, or roasted vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups jasmine rice
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 lime, zested and juiced
  • 1/2 cup cilantro, chopped
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • salt to taste

Why You'll Love It: Coconut rice gets a bright lift from lime and cilantro.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Cilantro Coconut Lime Rice, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
