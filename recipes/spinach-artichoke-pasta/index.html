Spinach Artichoke Pasta

Easy

Spinach artichoke pasta turns a beloved dip flavor into a full dinner. Rigatoni is coated in a creamy garlic parmesan sauce with tender spinach and artichoke hearts. It is cozy, green, and satisfyingly savory.

Ingredients

  • 350g rigatoni
  • 1 cup artichoke hearts
  • 3 cups spinach
  • 1 cup cream cheese or cream
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

Why You'll Love It: It has the comfort of spinach artichoke dip in pasta form.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook rigatoni until al dente and reserve pasta water.
  2. Saute garlic, then add artichoke hearts and spinach.
  3. Stir in cream cheese or cream until smooth.
  4. Add parmesan and loosen with pasta water.
  5. Toss with rigatoni and season to taste.
