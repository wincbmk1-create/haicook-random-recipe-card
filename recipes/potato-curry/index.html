Potato Curry

Easy

Chunks of potato are simmered in a warmly spiced tomato-based sauce until they turn tender and soak up all the flavor of cumin, turmeric, and coriander. It's an incredibly budget-friendly dish that still delivers deep, comforting spice and richness. Served with rice or warm bread, it's proof that simple, humble ingredients can make a truly satisfying curry.

Ingredients

  • 4 potatoes, cubed
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • fresh cilantro, to garnish

Why You'll Love It: A budget-friendly curry that's big on comfort and flavor.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pot and sauté the onion until soft.
  2. Add cumin, turmeric, and coriander, stirring until fragrant.
  3. Stir in the chopped tomatoes and cook until they break down.
  4. Add the cubed potatoes along with 1 cup of water, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes until tender.
  5. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve with rice or bread.
