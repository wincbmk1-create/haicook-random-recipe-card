Chocolate Chip Cookies

Easy

Butter is creamed with sugar until light and fluffy, then studded generously with chocolate chips before baking into cookies with crisp edges and soft, chewy centers. The smell alone as they bake is enough to bring everyone into the kitchen. This is the classic treat that never goes out of style, equally perfect for lunchboxes, bake sales, or a late-night snack.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 cups flour
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 cups chocolate chips
  • 1 tsp baking soda

Why You'll Love It: The classic treat that never fails to please a crowd.

Dessert

  4. Shape, pour, layer, or portion the Chocolate Chip Cookies mixture carefully so the finished dessert sets evenly.
  5. Cool, chill, or rest the dessert as needed, then garnish and serve once the texture has set properly.
