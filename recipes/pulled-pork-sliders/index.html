Pulled Pork Sliders

Easy

Pulled pork sliders are saucy, soft, and crowd-friendly. Tender pork is tucked into mini brioche buns with coleslaw, pickles, and barbecue sauce for a sweet, tangy, and smoky bite.

Ingredients

  • 6 slider buns
  • 2 cups cooked pulled pork
  • 1/2 cup barbecue sauce
  • 1 cup coleslaw
  • pickle slices
  • 1 tbsp butter

Why You'll Love It: Mini buns make smoky pulled pork easy to serve and fun to eat.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Warm pulled pork with barbecue sauce.
  2. Toast slider buns lightly with butter.
  3. Pile pulled pork onto each bun.
  4. Top with coleslaw and pickles.
  5. Close buns and serve warm.
