Kimchi Ramen

Easy

Kimchi ramen is spicy, tangy, and deeply cozy. Curly noodles simmer in a red broth with kimchi, pork belly, scallions, sesame, and a soft egg, creating a fast bowl with lots of flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions ramen noodles
  • 1 cup kimchi
  • 4 cups broth
  • 150g pork belly slices
  • 2 soft-boiled eggs
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp gochujang
  • sesame seeds

Why You'll Love It: Kimchi adds tangy heat to a cozy ramen bowl.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Brown pork belly slices in a pot.
  2. Add kimchi, broth, and gochujang and simmer.
  3. Cook ramen noodles in the broth until tender.
  4. Divide into bowls and top with eggs and scallions.
  5. Finish with sesame seeds.
