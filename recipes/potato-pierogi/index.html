Potato Pierogi

Medium

Potato pierogi are soft dumplings filled with mashed potato and cheese, then pan-seared with caramelized onions. A spoonful of sour cream and chives makes them rich, tangy, and deeply comforting.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mashed potatoes
  • 1 cup farmer cheese or cheddar
  • 24 dumpling wrappers or pierogi dough rounds
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • sour cream
  • chives

Why You'll Love It: Potato-cheese dumplings are cozy, satisfying, and pan-seared golden.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Mix mashed potatoes with cheese and seasoning.
  2. Fill dough rounds and seal into half-moons.
  3. Boil pierogi until they float.
  4. Pan-sear with butter and onions until golden.
  5. Serve with sour cream and chives.
