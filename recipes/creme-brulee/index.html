Creme Brulee

Medium

Creme brulee is all about contrast: a glassy caramelized sugar shell over cool, silky vanilla custard. The ingredient list is short, but the result feels polished and restaurant-worthy. Crack the top with a spoon and the creamy custard underneath does the rest.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 5 egg yolks
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • extra sugar for topping
  • berries, optional

Why You'll Love It: A crackly caramel top hides a silky vanilla custard.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Warm cream with vanilla until steaming, then cool slightly.
  2. Whisk egg yolks with sugar until smooth.
  3. Slowly whisk warm cream into the yolks.
  4. Bake in ramekins in a water bath until just set, then chill.
  5. Sprinkle with sugar and torch until caramelized before serving.
