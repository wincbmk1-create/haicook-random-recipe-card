Korean Bulgogi Salad Bowl

Medium

Korean bulgogi salad bowl is savory, vibrant, and filling. Tender marinated beef sits over greens and rice with kimchi, cucumber, carrots, and sesame dressing.

Ingredients

  • 250 g raw beef, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 2 cups greens
  • 1 cup uncooked white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups water or broth
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup kimchi
  • 1/2 cucumber
  • carrots
  • sesame dressing

Why You'll Love It: Sweet-savory beef turns a salad bowl into a hearty meal.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash and dry the greens or vegetables thoroughly, then slice the fresh toppings and measure the dressing ingredients.
  2. If the recipe includes grains, meat, tofu, seafood, or potatoes, cook them from raw and let them cool slightly; otherwise keep the salad components crisp and fresh.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Arrange the washed greens, toppings, and dressing for Korean Bulgogi Salad Bowl, keeping crisp items separate until serving if needed.
  5. Toss just before serving, then adjust salt, acidity, herbs, or crunchy toppings to taste.
