Pistachio Baklava Cups

Medium

Pistachio baklava cups are crisp, nutty, and honeyed. Buttery phyllo cups hold chopped pistachios and warm syrup for a bite-sized dessert.

Ingredients

  • phyllo sheets
  • 1 cup pistachios
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

Why You'll Love It: Mini phyllo cups make baklava neat, crisp, and shareable.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Layer buttered phyllo and cut into squares.
  2. Press squares into muffin cups.
  3. Fill with pistachios and cinnamon.
  4. Bake until crisp and golden.
  5. Drizzle with warm honey syrup.
