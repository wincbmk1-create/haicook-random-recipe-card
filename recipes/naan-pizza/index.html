Naan Pizza

Easy

Soft, pillowy naan bread makes a surprisingly great shortcut pizza crust — no dough-making or rising time required. Simply spread on some tomato sauce, scatter over your favorite cheese and toppings, and bake until melty and slightly crisp at the edges. It's a fun, fast way to satisfy a pizza craving on a busy weeknight, and endlessly customizable for picky eaters.

Ingredients

  • 2 pieces naan bread
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • toppings of choice (pepperoni, peppers, olives)

Why You'll Love It: A speedy pizza night hack using naan as the crust.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
  2. Place naan bread on a baking tray and spread tomato sauce evenly over each piece.
  3. Sprinkle shredded mozzarella over the sauce.
  4. Add your favorite toppings.
  5. Bake for 8-10 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
