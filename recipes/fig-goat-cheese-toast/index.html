Fig Goat Cheese Toast

Easy

Fig goat cheese toast is sweet, tangy, and elegant. Toasted sourdough is spread with goat cheese, topped with figs, walnuts, and honey, then finished with a little black pepper. It is simple but feels special.

Ingredients

  • 1 slice sourdough
  • 2 tbsp goat cheese
  • 2 figs, sliced
  • 1 tbsp walnuts
  • 1 tsp honey
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Figs, honey, and goat cheese make toast feel refined.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Toast sourdough until crisp.
  2. Spread goat cheese over the toast.
  3. Arrange fig slices on top.
  4. Add walnuts and drizzle with honey.
  5. Finish with black pepper.
