Garlic Herb Roasted Vegetable Rice

Easy

Roasted vegetables bring sweetness, color, and texture to this herb rice bowl. Garlic, olive oil, parsley, and lemon tie everything together without making it heavy. It works as a vegetarian main or a generous side dish.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 zucchini, chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: Roasted vegetables make rice bright, hearty, and flexible.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Roast zucchini, tomatoes, and bell pepper with olive oil and garlic.
  2. Fluff cooked rice in a large bowl.
  3. Fold roasted vegetables into the rice.
  4. Add lemon juice, parsley, salt, and pepper.
  5. Serve warm or at room temperature.
