Garlic Herb Roasted Vegetable Rice

Easy

Roasted vegetables bring sweetness, color, and texture to this herb rice bowl. Garlic, olive oil, parsley, and lemon tie everything together without making it heavy. It works as a vegetarian main or a generous side dish.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups water or broth
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 zucchini, chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: Roasted vegetables make rice bright, hearty, and flexible.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Garlic Herb Roasted Vegetable Rice, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
