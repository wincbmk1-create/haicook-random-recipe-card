Sundubu Jjigae

Medium

This Korean soft tofu stew is bold, bubbling, and packed with savory chili warmth. Silken tofu slips into a red broth with seafood, scallions, sesame oil, and an egg cracked in at the end. It is fiery enough to wake up dinner but still deeply comforting.

Ingredients

  • 300g silken tofu
  • 150g seafood mix
  • 1 egg
  • 3 cups broth
  • 1 tbsp gochugaru
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 scallions
  • 1 tsp sesame oil

Why You'll Love It: Soft tofu and spicy broth make every spoonful bold and soothing.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and chop the vegetables, then cut any raw meat, seafood, tofu, or aromatics into even pieces before heating the pot.
  2. Saute the aromatics in a little fat until fragrant, then add spices or tomato paste so the soup base develops flavor before liquid goes in.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Stir the Sundubu Jjigae gently as it finishes so the ingredients stay evenly distributed and the broth tastes balanced.
  5. Taste the soup, adjust salt and brightness, then ladle into bowls with fresh herbs or toppings.
