Sesame Honey Fried Bananas

Easy

Sesame honey fried bananas are crisp, warm, and sweet. Banana pieces are lightly battered, fried until golden, and drizzled with honey and sesame.

Ingredients

  • 3 bananas
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • oil for frying

Why You'll Love It: Crisp fried bananas and sesame honey make a warm dessert.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Mix flour and water into a light batter.
  2. Dip banana pieces in batter.
  3. Fry until golden and crisp.
  4. Drain briefly.
  5. Drizzle with honey and sesame seeds.
