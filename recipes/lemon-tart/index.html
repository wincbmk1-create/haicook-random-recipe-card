Lemon Tart

Medium

A glossy lemon tart balances sharp citrus curd with a buttery, crisp pastry shell. The filling is smooth and bright, while the crust adds enough richness to keep the lemon from feeling too sharp. It is a cheerful dessert that cuts cleanly and looks beautiful on the table.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour or crumbs
  • 6 tbsp cold butter
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2-3 tbsp cold water
  • 3 eggs
  • 3 lemons, juiced and zested
  • 6 tbsp butter
  • powdered sugar, optional

Why You'll Love It: Tangy lemon curd and crisp pastry make a clean, sunny dessert.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Measure the flour, sugar, dairy, eggs, fruit, or chocolate, then prepare the pan, mold, or serving cups before mixing.
  2. Make the batter, custard, dough, cream, or fruit filling from the listed ingredients, mixing only until the texture is smooth or just combined.
  3. Cook, bake, chill, steam, or set the mixture until the dessert reaches the proper texture, watching closely so it stays tender rather than dry.
  4. Shape, pour, layer, or portion the Lemon Tart mixture carefully so the finished dessert sets evenly.
  5. Cool, chill, or rest the dessert as needed, then garnish and serve once the texture has set properly.
