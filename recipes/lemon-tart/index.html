Lemon Tart

Medium

A glossy lemon tart balances sharp citrus curd with a buttery, crisp pastry shell. The filling is smooth and bright, while the crust adds enough richness to keep the lemon from feeling too sharp. It is a cheerful dessert that cuts cleanly and looks beautiful on the table.

Ingredients

  • 1 baked tart shell
  • 3 eggs
  • 3 lemons, juiced and zested
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 6 tbsp butter
  • powdered sugar, optional

Why You'll Love It: Tangy lemon curd and crisp pastry make a clean, sunny dessert.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Whisk eggs, lemon juice, zest, and sugar in a heatproof bowl.
  2. Cook gently over simmering water until thickened.
  3. Whisk in butter until smooth and glossy.
  4. Pour into the baked tart shell and chill until set.
  5. Dust with powdered sugar or lemon zest before serving.
