Espresso Chocolate Mousse

Medium

Espresso chocolate mousse is airy, bold, and silky. Dark chocolate and espresso fold into whipped cream for a chilled dessert with deep flavor.

Ingredients

  • 150 g dark chocolate
  • 1 shot espresso
  • 2 eggs, separated
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 cup cream
  • chocolate shavings

Why You'll Love It: Espresso deepens the flavor of airy chocolate mousse.

Dessert

  5. Cool, chill, or rest the dessert as needed, then garnish and serve once the texture has set properly.
