Herb Potato Salad

Easy

Herb potato salad is fresh, tangy, and lighter than creamy versions. Warm potatoes soak up Dijon, vinegar, olive oil, dill, parsley, and chives for a clean bistro side.

Ingredients

  • 600 g small potatoes
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • dill
  • parsley
  • chives

Why You'll Love It: A mustardy herb dressing keeps potato salad bright and clean.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Boil potatoes until tender and slice them warm.
  2. Whisk Dijon, oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper.
  3. Toss potatoes with dressing while warm.
  4. Fold in chopped herbs.
  5. Serve warm or room temperature.
