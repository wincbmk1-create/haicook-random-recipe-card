Avgolemono Soup

Medium

Avgolemono turns chicken broth, rice, eggs, and lemon into a silky Greek soup that tastes both comforting and bright. The egg mixture is gently tempered so it thickens the broth without scrambling. Each spoonful is creamy without cream, with fresh lemon lifting the whole bowl.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1/2 cup rice
  • 5 cups chicken broth
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • fresh dill
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Lemon and egg turn simple broth into something silky and special.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Simmer rice in chicken broth until tender.
  2. Stir in shredded chicken and warm through.
  3. Whisk eggs and lemon juice in a bowl until smooth.
  4. Slowly whisk hot broth into the egg mixture to temper it.
  5. Return the mixture to the pot on low heat, stir until silky, and finish with dill.
