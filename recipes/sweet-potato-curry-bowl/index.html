Sweet Potato Curry Bowl

Easy

Sweet potato curry bowl is creamy, fragrant, and colorful. Cubed sweet potatoes simmer with chickpeas, coconut milk, curry spices, and spinach for a satisfying bowl.

Ingredients

  • 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 1 can chickpeas
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp curry paste
  • 2 cups spinach
  • cilantro
  • lime

Why You'll Love It: Sweet potatoes make coconut curry naturally hearty.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Simmer sweet potatoes with curry paste and coconut milk.
  2. Add chickpeas and cook until potatoes are tender.
  3. Stir in spinach until wilted.
  4. Season with salt and lime.
  5. Serve with cilantro.
