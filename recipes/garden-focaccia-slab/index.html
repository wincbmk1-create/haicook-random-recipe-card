Garden Focaccia Slab

Medium

Garden focaccia slab is golden, airy, and dotted with bright vegetables. Cherry tomatoes, olives, zucchini, and rosemary bake into the surface, making each slice fragrant and colorful. It works as a snack, side, or simple lunch with a salad.

Ingredients

  • 1 focaccia dough slab
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup olives
  • 1/2 zucchini, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh rosemary
  • flaky salt

Why You'll Love It: Vegetables and herbs turn focaccia into an edible garden.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Press dough into an oiled baking pan.
  2. Dimple the surface with your fingers.
  3. Arrange tomatoes, olives, zucchini, and rosemary on top.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with salt.
  5. Bake until puffed and golden.
