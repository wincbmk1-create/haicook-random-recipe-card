Potato Frittata

Easy

Potato frittata is a hearty skillet dish with sliced potatoes, eggs, spinach, onions, and herbs. It is golden on top, tender inside, and just as good warm as it is at room temperature.

Ingredients

  • 2 potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 6 eggs
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh herbs

Why You'll Love It: Tender potatoes make a simple frittata hearty enough for brunch.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook potatoes and onion in olive oil until tender.
  2. Add spinach and wilt briefly.
  3. Whisk eggs with milk, cheese, salt, and pepper.
  4. Pour eggs into the skillet and cook until edges set.
  5. Bake or broil until the center is set and golden.
