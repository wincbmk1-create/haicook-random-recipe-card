Spanish Tortilla

Medium

Thinly sliced potatoes and onions are slowly cooked in olive oil until soft and slightly caramelized, then bound together with beaten eggs and cooked into a thick, satisfying omelette. It's traditionally flipped whole in the pan, a small kitchen ritual that Spanish home cooks take pride in mastering. Delicious warm, at room temperature, or even cold the next day, it's a humble but beloved staple of Spanish cuisine.

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 6 eggs
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • salt to taste

Why You'll Love It: A humble potato omelette that's a beloved Spanish staple.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Scrub, peel if desired, and cut the potatoes evenly so they cook at the same pace.
  2. Boil, roast, saute, or steam the potatoes from raw until tender inside and ready to absorb seasoning.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Spanish Tortilla, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
