Spanish Tortilla

Medium

Thinly sliced potatoes and onions are slowly cooked in olive oil until soft and slightly caramelized, then bound together with beaten eggs and cooked into a thick, satisfying omelette. It's traditionally flipped whole in the pan, a small kitchen ritual that Spanish home cooks take pride in mastering. Delicious warm, at room temperature, or even cold the next day, it's a humble but beloved staple of Spanish cuisine.

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 6 eggs
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • salt to taste

Why You'll Love It: A humble potato omelette that's a beloved Spanish staple.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan and slowly cook the potatoes and onion over low heat until soft, about 20 minutes.
  2. Drain the potatoes and onion, reserving the oil, and let cool slightly.
  3. Whisk the eggs in a large bowl and fold in the potatoes and onion.
  4. Pour a little reserved oil into a non-stick pan, add the mixture, and cook over medium-low heat until the bottom sets.
  5. Flip the tortilla using a plate and cook the other side until golden and just set in the center.
