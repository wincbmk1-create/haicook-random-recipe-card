Lobster Roll

Medium

Lobster roll is buttery, tender, and coastal. Sweet lobster meat is warmed with butter and lemon, then tucked into a toasted split-top bun. Chives add freshness without hiding the seafood.

Ingredients

  • 1 split-top bun
  • 120 g lobster meat
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp chives
  • pinch salt

Why You'll Love It: Sweet lobster in a toasted bun feels celebratory and clean.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the fillings from raw ingredients: wash and slice vegetables, season any meat or seafood, and mix spreads or sauces from their listed components.
  2. Cook raw proteins or vegetables until tender, then toast or warm the bread so it can hold the filling without turning soggy.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Layer the cooked fillings, fresh toppings, and sauce into the bread for Lobster Roll while balancing moisture and crunch.
  5. Serve while the bread is warm or crisp, adding delicate herbs, greens, or pickles at the very end.
