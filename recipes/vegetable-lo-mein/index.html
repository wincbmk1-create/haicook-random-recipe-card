Vegetable Lo Mein

Easy

Vegetable lo mein is colorful, saucy, and quick. Egg noodles are tossed with broccoli, carrots, mushrooms, and a savory soy-based sauce for a flexible weeknight noodle dish.

Ingredients

  • 200 g egg noodles
  • 1 cup broccoli
  • 1 carrot, sliced
  • 1 cup mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp sugar

Why You'll Love It: A glossy stir-fry sauce makes vegetables and noodles satisfying.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook noodles and drain.
  2. Stir-fry vegetables until tender-crisp.
  3. Mix soy sauce, sesame oil, and sugar.
  4. Add noodles and sauce to the pan.
  5. Toss until glossy and hot.
