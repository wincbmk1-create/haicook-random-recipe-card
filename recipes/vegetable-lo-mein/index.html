Vegetable Lo Mein

Easy

Vegetable lo mein is colorful, saucy, and quick. Egg noodles are tossed with broccoli, carrots, mushrooms, and a savory soy-based sauce for a flexible weeknight noodle dish.

Ingredients

  • 200 g dry egg noodles
  • 1 cup broccoli
  • 1 carrot, sliced
  • 1 cup mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp sugar

Why You'll Love It: A glossy stir-fry sauce makes vegetables and noodles satisfying.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Vegetable Lo Mein, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
