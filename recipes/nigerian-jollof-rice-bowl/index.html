Nigerian Jollof Rice Bowl

Medium

Jollof rice is rich, red, and full of personality. Rice cooks in a tomato and pepper base with onion, thyme, and warm seasoning until every grain turns savory and deeply colored. It is excellent with grilled chicken, fried plantain, or simply a handful of fresh herbs on top.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 2 cups tomato pepper sauce
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 cups stock
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 tbsp oil

Why You'll Love It: Tomato-pepper rice brings smoky depth and party-table energy.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Nigerian Jollof Rice Bowl, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
