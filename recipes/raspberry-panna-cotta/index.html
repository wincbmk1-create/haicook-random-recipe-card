Raspberry Panna Cotta

Medium

Raspberry panna cotta is silky, creamy, and bright. A vanilla cream base sets softly under a glossy raspberry sauce for an elegant chilled dessert.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cream
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 tsp gelatin
  • 1 cup raspberries
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Why You'll Love It: Tart raspberries brighten silky vanilla cream.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Bloom gelatin in cold water.
  2. Warm cream with sugar and vanilla.
  3. Stir in gelatin until dissolved.
  4. Pour into molds and chill until set.
  5. Top with raspberry sauce.
