Burrata Peach Arugula Salad

Easy

Burrata peach arugula salad is creamy, sweet, and elegant. Peppery arugula, ripe peaches, torn burrata, basil, and balsamic glaze create a simple summer plate.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups arugula
  • 1 peach, sliced
  • 1 ball burrata
  • fresh basil
  • 1 tbsp balsamic glaze
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Juicy peaches and burrata make greens feel special.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Arrange arugula on a plate.
  2. Add peach slices.
  3. Tear burrata over the salad.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil and balsamic glaze.
  5. Finish with basil and pepper.
