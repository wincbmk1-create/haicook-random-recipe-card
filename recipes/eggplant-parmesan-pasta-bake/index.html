Eggplant Parmesan Pasta Bake

Medium

This pasta bake folds roasted eggplant, marinara, mozzarella, and parmesan into tender pasta. The top bakes golden while the center stays saucy and rich. It gives you the comfort of eggplant parmesan without breading or frying.

Ingredients

  • 350g short pasta
  • 1 eggplant, cubed
  • 2 cups marinara
  • 1.5 cups mozzarella
  • 1/3 cup parmesan
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh basil

Why You'll Love It: Eggplant parmesan flavors become an easy baked pasta dinner.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Roast eggplant cubes with olive oil until tender.
  2. Cook pasta until just al dente.
  3. Toss pasta with marinara, eggplant, and half the cheese.
  4. Transfer to a baking dish and top with remaining cheese.
  5. Bake until bubbling and golden, then finish with basil.
