Roasted Carrot Coconut Soup

Easy

Roasted carrot coconut soup is silky, sweet, and fragrant. Roasted carrots blend with coconut milk, ginger, and lime for a vibrant bowl.

Ingredients

  • 6 carrots
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 2 cups broth
  • 1 tsp ginger
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • lime juice
  • cilantro

Why You'll Love It: Roasting carrots deepens their sweetness before blending.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Roast carrots with olive oil until tender.
  2. Simmer roasted carrots with broth and ginger.
  3. Blend until smooth.
  4. Stir in coconut milk.
  5. Finish with lime and cilantro.
