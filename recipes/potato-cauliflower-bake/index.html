Potato Cauliflower Bake

Easy

Potato cauliflower bake is cheesy, gentle, and golden. Tender potatoes and cauliflower are covered in mustardy cheese sauce and breadcrumbs, then baked until bubbling.

Ingredients

  • 3 potatoes, sliced
  • 2 cups cauliflower florets
  • 1 1/2 cups cheese sauce
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • parsley

Why You'll Love It: Potatoes and cauliflower make a bubbling family-style bake.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Parboil potatoes and cauliflower.
  2. Place them in a baking dish.
  3. Stir mustard into cheese sauce and pour over vegetables.
  4. Top with breadcrumbs.
  5. Bake until golden and bubbling.
