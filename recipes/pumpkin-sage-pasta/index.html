Pumpkin Sage Pasta

Easy

Pumpkin sage pasta is creamy, golden, and gently sweet. Pumpkin puree blends with garlic, cream, parmesan, and sage for a sauce that coats noodles beautifully. It is a cozy autumn-style pasta that still works any time you want a soft, comforting bowl.

Ingredients

  • 350g pasta
  • 1 cup pumpkin puree
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1/3 cup parmesan
  • 6 sage leaves
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Pumpkin and sage make a warm, silky sauce for pasta.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook pasta until al dente; reserve pasta water.
  2. Melt butter and crisp sage leaves briefly.
  3. Add garlic, pumpkin puree, cream, and parmesan.
  4. Loosen sauce with pasta water.
  5. Toss with pasta and finish with pepper and sage.
