Pumpkin Sage Pasta

Easy

Pumpkin sage pasta is creamy, golden, and gently sweet. Pumpkin puree blends with garlic, cream, parmesan, and sage for a sauce that coats noodles beautifully. It is a cozy autumn-style pasta that still works any time you want a soft, comforting bowl.

Ingredients

  • 350g dry pasta
  • 1 cup pumpkin puree
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1/3 cup parmesan
  • 6 sage leaves
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Pumpkin and sage make a warm, silky sauce for pasta.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Pumpkin Sage Pasta, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
