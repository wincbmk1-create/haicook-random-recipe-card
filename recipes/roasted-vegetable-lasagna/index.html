Roasted Vegetable Lasagna

Medium

Roasted vegetable lasagna is rich, colorful, and satisfying. Layers of pasta, marinara, ricotta, mozzarella, zucchini, eggplant, and peppers bake into a bubbling tray with sweet roasted flavor in every slice.

Ingredients

  • 9 dry lasagna sheets
  • 2 cups roasted vegetables
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1.5 cups ricotta
  • 1 cup mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup parmesan
  • fresh basil

Why You'll Love It: Roasted vegetables bring sweetness and depth to classic lasagna.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Roasted Vegetable Lasagna, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
