Singapore Noodles

Easy

Thin rice vermicelli noodles are stir-fried with a generous dusting of curry powder, giving them a warm golden color and fragrant spiced flavor. Juicy shrimp and scrambled egg add heartiness, while a quick toss over high heat keeps the noodles light and never mushy. It's a fast, aromatic dish that punches well above its short cooking time.

Ingredients

  • 200g rice vermicelli noodles
  • 150g shrimp, peeled
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil

Why You'll Love It: Curry-spiced noodles that are quick to make and full of flavor.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak vermicelli noodles in warm water until softened; drain.
  2. Heat oil in a wok and scramble the eggs briefly; remove and set aside.
  3. Stir-fry shrimp and bell pepper until the shrimp turns pink.
  4. Add the noodles, curry powder, and soy sauce, tossing everything together over high heat.
  5. Fold the scrambled eggs back in and serve immediately.
