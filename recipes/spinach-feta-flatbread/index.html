Spinach Feta Flatbread

Easy

Spinach feta flatbread is crisp, colorful, and quick to bake. Warm flatbread is topped with spinach, feta, roasted peppers, olives, and herbs, giving you pizza-like comfort with a fresher Mediterranean feel.

Ingredients

  • 2 flatbreads
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 1/2 cup feta crumbles
  • 1/2 cup roasted red peppers
  • 1/3 cup olives, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • dried oregano

Why You'll Love It: A crisp flatbread with salty cheese, greens, and Mediterranean color.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 220C (425F).
  2. Brush flatbreads with olive oil.
  3. Top with spinach, feta, roasted peppers, olives, and oregano.
  4. Bake for 8-10 minutes until edges are crisp.
  5. Slice and serve warm.
