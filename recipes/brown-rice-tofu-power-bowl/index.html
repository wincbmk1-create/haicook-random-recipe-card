Brown Rice Tofu Power Bowl

Easy

This power bowl layers chewy brown rice with crisp tofu, edamame, cucumber, carrots, and sesame dressing. It is hearty without feeling heavy and works well for meal prep. Every bite has protein, crunch, and a savory nutty finish.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups water or broth
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 300g firm tofu, cubed
  • 1 cup edamame
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 carrot, shredded
  • 2 tbsp sesame dressing
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • sesame seeds

Why You'll Love It: Brown rice, tofu, and vegetables make a steady, balanced bowl.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Drain and pat dry the tofu, beans, or legumes, then season and warm them with aromatics until flavorful.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Brown Rice Tofu Power Bowl, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
