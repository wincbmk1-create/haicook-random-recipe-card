Garlic Butter Shrimp Rice

Easy

Garlic butter shrimp rice is simple but generous. Shrimp are quickly cooked with garlic, butter, lemon, and parsley, then spooned over warm rice so the buttery sauce sinks into every grain. It is a quick dinner that tastes polished without needing many ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups water or broth
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 300g shrimp, peeled
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tbsp parsley, chopped
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: Juicy shrimp and garlic butter make plain rice feel special fast.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear, then cook it with water or broth and a pinch of salt until tender; spread it out briefly if the dish needs drier grains.
  2. Prep the supporting ingredients: mince aromatics, slice vegetables, measure seasonings, and mix any sauce or dressing from its raw components.
  3. Pat the seafood dry, season lightly, and cook it just until opaque so it stays tender.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Garlic Butter Shrimp Rice, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
