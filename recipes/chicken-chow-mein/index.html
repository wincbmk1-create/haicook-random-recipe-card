Chicken Chow Mein

Easy

Springy noodles are stir-fried in a hot wok with tender strips of chicken and crisp cabbage, all coated in a glossy, savory soy-based sauce. The high heat gives the noodles a slightly smoky edge that's hard to replicate any other way. It's a familiar takeout favorite that's actually quicker, fresher, and healthier to make yourself at home.

Ingredients

  • 250g chow mein dry noodles
  • 1 chicken breast, sliced thin
  • 2 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil

Why You'll Love It: A takeout favorite you can make faster (and healthier) at home.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Chicken Chow Mein, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
