Rosemary Focaccia Sandwich

Easy

Rosemary focaccia sandwich layers salty prosciutto, creamy mozzarella, roasted peppers, and peppery arugula inside tender, olive-oil-rich bread. It is rustic, fragrant, and easy to slice for lunch or a casual dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 rosemary focaccia slab
  • 120g prosciutto
  • 200g fresh mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup roasted peppers
  • 1 cup arugula
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • black pepper

Why You'll Love It: Herby focaccia turns simple fillings into a generous bistro sandwich.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Slice focaccia horizontally.
  2. Layer mozzarella, roasted peppers, prosciutto, and arugula.
  3. Drizzle with olive oil and season with black pepper.
  4. Close the sandwich and press gently.
  5. Cut into portions and serve.
