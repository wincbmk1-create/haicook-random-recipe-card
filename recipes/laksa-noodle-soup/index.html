Laksa Noodle Soup

Medium

Laksa brings together rice noodles, coconut milk, curry paste, and bright herbs in one aromatic bowl. The broth is creamy and spicy, while shrimp, tofu puffs, and bean sprouts add texture. It is a lively noodle soup for days when you want comfort with a little spark.

Ingredients

  • 250g dry rice noodles
  • 2 tbsp laksa or red curry paste
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 3 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 250g shrimp, peeled
  • 1 cup bean sprouts
  • tofu puffs, optional
  • lime and herbs, to serve

Why You'll Love It: Creamy coconut curry broth makes every spoonful feel vibrant.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Season the raw chicken with salt, pepper, and the recipe spices, then sear, simmer, bake, or grill it until fully cooked and juicy.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Laksa Noodle Soup, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
