Lemon Ricotta Pasta

Easy

Creamy ricotta melts into hot pasta water to create an effortlessly smooth sauce, while freshly grated lemon zest cuts through the richness with a bright, sunny lift. A shower of parmesan on top adds a savory finishing touch. It feels like a dish you'd get at a charming little trattoria, yet it comes together with barely any effort on a weeknight.

Ingredients

  • 350g dry pasta (spaghetti or penne)
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh basil, torn

Why You'll Love It: A light pasta dish that feels fancy but takes minutes.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Lemon Ricotta Pasta, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
