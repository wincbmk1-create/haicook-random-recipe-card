Berry Crumble

Easy

Berry crumble is simple, generous, and full of cozy contrast. Juicy mixed berries bubble under a crisp oat topping, creating a dessert that feels homemade without much effort. It is best served warm with vanilla ice cream melting over the top.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups mixed berries
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 6 tbsp cold butter

Why You'll Love It: Bubbling berries and a golden oat topping make an easy crowd-pleaser.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Toss berries with sugar, lemon juice, and cornstarch in a baking dish.
  2. Mix oats, flour, brown sugar, and cold butter until crumbly.
  3. Scatter the crumble topping over the berries.
  4. Bake at 190C (375F) for 30-35 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  5. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.
