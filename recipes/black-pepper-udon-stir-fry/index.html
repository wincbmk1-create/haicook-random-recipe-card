Black Pepper Udon Stir Fry

Easy

Black pepper udon stir fry is savory, peppery, and quick. Chewy udon noodles are tossed with beef, bell peppers, scallions, and a glossy black pepper sauce for a satisfying pan meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 portions dry udon noodles
  • 150 g beef slices
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • scallions

Why You'll Love It: Black pepper sauce gives chewy udon a bold finish.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Soak or boil the dry noodles until flexible but still springy, then drain and rinse if the dish needs a cooler or stir-fry texture.
  2. Cook aromatics, vegetables, proteins, or broth from raw ingredients, then season the sauce or soup base before adding noodles.
  3. Season the raw meat, then brown it in a hot pan so it develops color before simmering or finishing it with the vegetables and sauce.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Black Pepper Udon Stir Fry, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
