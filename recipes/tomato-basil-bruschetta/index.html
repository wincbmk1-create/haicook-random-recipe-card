Tomato Basil Bruschetta

Easy

Tomato basil bruschetta is all about contrast: crisp toasted bread, juicy tomatoes, fragrant basil, and a little balsamic sweetness. It is simple, colorful, and perfect when you want something fresh but still satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 1 small baguette, sliced
  • 2 cups diced tomatoes
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil, chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp balsamic glaze
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Why You'll Love It: Juicy tomatoes and crunchy toast make a bright starter or snack.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Toast baguette slices until golden and crisp.
  2. Mix tomatoes, garlic, basil, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spoon the tomato mixture over the toast.
  4. Drizzle lightly with balsamic glaze.
  5. Serve immediately while the bread is still crisp.
