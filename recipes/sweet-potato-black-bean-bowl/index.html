Sweet Potato Black Bean Bowl

Easy

This sweet potato black bean bowl is colorful, filling, and full of contrast. Roasted sweet potatoes meet black beans, avocado, corn salsa, cilantro, lime, and a creamy chipotle drizzle for a fresh but hearty meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 1 can black beans, drained
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 cup corn salsa
  • 1 lime
  • fresh cilantro
  • 2 tbsp chipotle mayo or yogurt sauce
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: Roasted sweet potato and beans make a bright, filling bowl.

Potato

Cooking Steps

  1. Roast sweet potato cubes with olive oil and salt until browned.
  2. Warm black beans gently.
  3. Arrange sweet potatoes, beans, avocado, and corn salsa in bowls.
  4. Drizzle with chipotle sauce.
  5. Finish with lime and cilantro.
