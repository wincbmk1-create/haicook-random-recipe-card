Croque Monsieur

Medium

Croque monsieur is a deeply satisfying French cafe classic. Ham and gruyere are layered between bread, topped with bechamel and more cheese, then baked or broiled until golden, bubbling, and crisp around the edges.

Ingredients

  • 4 slices sandwich bread
  • 4 slices ham
  • 1 cup grated gruyere
  • 1/2 cup bechamel sauce
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp butter

Why You'll Love It: A classic French cafe sandwich with bubbly cheese and creamy sauce.

Bread

Cooking Steps

  1. Spread Dijon on the bread slices.
  2. Layer ham and gruyere between the slices.
  3. Butter the outside and toast lightly in a pan.
  4. Spread bechamel on top and sprinkle with more gruyere.
  5. Broil until bubbling and golden.
