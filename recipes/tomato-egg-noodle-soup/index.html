Tomato Egg Noodle Soup

Easy

Tomato egg noodle soup is gentle, bright, and homey. Tomatoes simmer into a light broth before egg ribbons and noodles are added, creating a soothing bowl with simple ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 200 g noodles
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 3 cups broth
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • scallions
  • white pepper

Why You'll Love It: Tomato and egg make a soft comforting noodle soup.

Noodles

Cooking Steps

  1. Simmer tomatoes in broth until softened.
  2. Season with soy sauce and white pepper.
  3. Cook noodles in the broth.
  4. Drizzle in beaten eggs to form ribbons.
  5. Top with scallions and serve.
