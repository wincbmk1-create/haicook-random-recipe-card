Truffle Mushroom Gnocchi

Easy

Truffle mushroom gnocchi feels plush without being difficult. Pillowy gnocchi are folded through creamy mushrooms, parmesan, parsley, and a tiny drizzle of truffle oil. The result is earthy, aromatic, and deeply comforting.

Ingredients

  • 500g gnocchi
  • 300g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 tsp truffle oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • fresh parsley

Why You'll Love It: Soft gnocchi and mushrooms create a cozy restaurant-style bowl.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Truffle Mushroom Gnocchi, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
