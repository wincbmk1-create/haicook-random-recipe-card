Quinoa Power Bowl

Easy

Protein-rich quinoa is paired with a colorful medley of roasted seasonal vegetables, then finished with a creamy, nutty tahini drizzle that ties everything together. Because it holds up so well in the fridge, it's a favorite for meal prepping ahead of a busy week. Wholesome, energizing, and satisfying, it tastes just as good cold the next day as it does freshly made.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup quinoa, cooked
  • 1 zucchini, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Why You'll Love It: A meal-prep favorite that's just as good the next day.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Toss the zucchini, bell pepper, and cherry tomatoes with olive oil.
  2. Roast at 200°C (400°F) for 20-25 minutes until tender and slightly caramelized.
  3. Whisk together tahini, lemon juice, and a splash of water to make the dressing.
  4. Divide the cooked quinoa between bowls and top with the roasted vegetables.
  5. Drizzle with the tahini dressing before serving.
