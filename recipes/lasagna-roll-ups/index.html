Lasagna Roll-Ups

Medium

Lasagna roll-ups turn classic lasagna into tidy, generous portions. Each pasta sheet is filled with ricotta and spinach, rolled into marinara, topped with mozzarella, and baked until bubbling. They are comforting, cheerful, and easy to share.

Ingredients

  • 10 dry lasagna sheets
  • 1.5 cups ricotta
  • 2 cups spinach, chopped
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella
  • 1 egg
  • fresh basil

Why You'll Love It: Individual rolls make lasagna easier to serve and fun to plate.

Pasta

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil, then cook the dry pasta until just al dente; reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  2. Build the sauce from aromatics, vegetables, dairy, cheese, herbs, or seafood while the pasta cooks, loosening it with reserved pasta water if needed.
  3. Cook the main ingredients with the aromatics and seasonings until tender, fragrant, and safe to eat.
  4. Combine the cooked base, fresh toppings, and sauce for Lasagna Roll-Ups, folding or tossing gently so the main texture stays intact.
  5. Finish with herbs, citrus, sauce, or toppings, then serve while the textures are at their best.
