Chicken Biryani

Medium

Biryani is a fragrant, celebratory rice dish beloved across South Asia, made by layering long-grain basmati rice with chicken marinated in yogurt and a warm blend of spices like cardamom, cinnamon, and cloves. The whole pot is slow-cooked so the rice absorbs all those aromas while staying light and fluffy. Each spoonful delivers a different balance of spice, tenderness, and fragrance, making it a dish that feels special enough for guests yet comforting enough for a family dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500g chicken thighs
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp biryani spice mix
  • 1/2 tsp saffron, soaked in warm milk
  • 3 tbsp ghee
  • fresh mint and cilantro

Why You'll Love It: Fragrant layers of spiced rice and tender chicken in every bite.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt and biryani spices for at least 1 hour.
  2. Fry sliced onions in ghee until deep golden brown; set half aside for garnish.
  3. Cook the marinated chicken with the remaining fried onions until nearly done.
  4. Parboil the basmati rice separately until 70% cooked, then drain.
  5. Layer the rice over the chicken, drizzle with saffron milk, cover tightly, and steam on low heat for 20 minutes before fluffing and serving.
