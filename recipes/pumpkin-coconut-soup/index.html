Pumpkin Coconut Soup

Easy

Pumpkin or squash blends into a golden soup with coconut milk, garlic, onion, and a touch of curry. The result is smooth, fragrant, and lightly sweet, with toasted seeds adding crunch. A few drops of chili oil make it feel warm and restaurant-polished without much effort.

Ingredients

  • 700g pumpkin or squash, cubed
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp curry paste or curry powder
  • 4 cups broth
  • pumpkin seeds
  • chili oil

Why You'll Love It: Pumpkin sweetness, coconut creaminess, and gentle spice work beautifully together.

Soup

Cooking Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and chop the vegetables, then cut any raw meat, seafood, tofu, or aromatics into even pieces before heating the pot.
  2. Saute the aromatics in a little fat until fragrant, then add spices or tomato paste so the soup base develops flavor before liquid goes in.
  3. Add the main ingredients and liquid, then simmer until the vegetables, grains, beans, or proteins are tender.
  4. Stir the Pumpkin Coconut Soup gently as it finishes so the ingredients stay evenly distributed and the broth tastes balanced.
  5. Taste the soup, adjust salt and brightness, then ladle into bowls with fresh herbs or toppings.
