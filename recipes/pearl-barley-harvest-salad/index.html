Pearl Barley Harvest Salad

Easy

Pearl barley gives this harvest salad a chewy, nutty base that pairs beautifully with roasted squash, kale, pomegranate, pecans, and feta. A lightly sweet vinaigrette ties the warm and fresh elements together. It is colorful, sturdy, and great for meal prep.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked pearl barley
  • 2 cups roasted squash cubes
  • 2 cups kale, chopped
  • 1/3 cup pomegranate seeds
  • 1/3 cup pecans
  • 1/2 cup feta
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup

Why You'll Love It: Chewy barley and roasted squash make a cozy grain salad.

Salad

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook barley until tender, then cool slightly.
  2. Massage kale with olive oil and a pinch of salt.
  3. Add barley, roasted squash, pomegranate, pecans, and feta.
  4. Whisk olive oil with maple syrup and vinegar or lemon juice.
  5. Toss everything together and serve warm or chilled.
