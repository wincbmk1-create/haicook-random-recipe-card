Coconut Turmeric Rice Bowl

Easy

This golden rice bowl cooks rice with coconut milk and turmeric for a creamy, aromatic base. Chickpeas or vegetables add substance, while herbs and lime keep it bright. It is plant-friendly, cozy, and colorful enough for a weeknight reset.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups jasmine rice
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 1 cup greens
  • 1 lime
  • cilantro

Why You'll Love It: Coconut and turmeric make rice fragrant, creamy, and colorful.

Rice

Cooking Steps

  1. Cook rice with coconut milk, water, turmeric, and salt.
  2. Warm chickpeas in a small pan.
  3. Wilt greens briefly with a splash of water.
  4. Layer golden rice with chickpeas and greens.
  5. Finish with lime juice and cilantro.
