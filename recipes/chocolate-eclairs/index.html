Chocolate Eclairs

Medium

Chocolate eclairs are made from airy choux pastry filled with vanilla pastry cream and finished with a glossy chocolate glaze. The crisp shell and cool cream create a classic bakery texture. They take a little care, but the result feels completely worth it.

Ingredients

  • 1 batch choux pastry
  • 2 cups pastry cream
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1 egg wash

Why You'll Love It: Cream-filled pastry and shiny chocolate glaze feel bakery-perfect.

Dessert

Cooking Steps

  1. Pipe choux pastry into long strips on a lined baking sheet.
  2. Brush with egg wash and bake until puffed and golden.
  3. Cool completely, then fill with pastry cream.
  4. Melt chocolate with cream to make a smooth glaze.
  5. Dip the tops of the eclairs in glaze and let set.
